If you had a quiet weekend planned, bad news. SEGA and Sports Interactive have opened Football Manager 27 to everyone, free, until Tuesday. It's the Launch Preview build, normally reserved for people who pre-ordered, and this time anyone with a PC or Mac can jump in and start a career.

![The Football Manager 27 timeline: free until November 3, pre-order access until November 10 and full release, with Steam prices in shekels and dollars](img/posts/football-manager-27-launch-preview-en.webp "FM27 dates and prices")

## When and where

Free access runs until 18:00 GMT on November 3, which is 20:00 in Israel. You can download it on Steam, the Epic Games Store and the Microsoft Store, or through Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass if you subscribe.

The key detail: any career you start now carries over to the full game. Buy it, or pre-purchase at 10% off, and you keep playing straight through November 3–10. If you don't, you'll wait for the full release on November 10 and pick up where you left off.

## What it costs in Israel

On Steam's Israeli store the game is ₪239 (about $77), or ₪215.10 (about $69) as a pre-purchase. The US store charges $59.99 (around ₪187), so the Israeli price is higher. Game Pass subscribers get it as part of their plan.

## What's new

FM27 brings a few changes fans have been asking about for a while:

- **Tactical Effectiveness:** a screen that shows how well your tactics are actually working, not just whether you won.
- **Tactical Tweaks:** small adjustments mid-match without tearing up your whole setup.
- **Crowds and stadiums:** a visual upgrade for grounds and the atmosphere in the stands.
- **Cup draws and transfer deadline day:** both are back in the game.
- **Shuffle Draft:** a new multiplayer mode where you draft a squad against friends.
- **1,500+ competition updates** around the world.

![The English League Cup screen in Football Manager 27 with a list of fixtures and results](img/posts/football-manager-27-cup-draw.webp "The cup screen in FM27. Image: SEGA / Sports Interactive")

## Before you start

- **No Hebrew.** The game is translated into 20 languages, Arabic included, but not Hebrew.
- **Denuvo:** activations are capped at five a day. If you hop between computers a lot, keep that in mind.
- **Minimum specs:** 6GB of RAM, a GTX 960 and 20GB of free space. Almost any computer from the last few years will run it.

There's no reason not to try it. If you're already hooked on the series, start the career you'll stick with all season. If you've never played, now's your chance to find out why your friends vanish every November.
