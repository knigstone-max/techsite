Samsung showed off its smart glasses back in July and has said little since. Now there's finally a date, though not an official one. According to Korean outlet ChosunBiz, Samsung Intelligent Eyewear will launch in mid-December, in South Korea and the US only. China, Europe and Japan follow in 2027. Israel isn't mentioned, and the price still hasn't been set.

## What's inside

These aren't AR glasses with a screen. There's no display in the lenses at all. They work mostly by voice, with a 12MP camera, stereo speakers and several microphones, running Android XR with Google's Gemini assistant on a Qualcomm Snapdragon AR1 Gen 1 chip.

What do you do with them? Samsung lists live translation, navigation, capturing notes, and sharing your point of view on a video call. With a Galaxy Watch9 you can also control them with hand gestures. Samsung quotes up to 9 hours per charge, and the case holds 7 more full charges.

## Two frame brands, and a price nobody knows yet

Samsung is working with two eyewear brands: Korea's Gentle Monster, with a black frame, and America's Warby Parker, with a brown one. Four styles have been shown so far.

On price, all Samsung said in July was that it would be "reasonable" for a product "in the premium range". For comparison, Meta's latest smart glasses cost $299 (about ₪938), and its top model, the Oakley Meta Vanguard, is $499 (about ₪1,565). 9to5Google doesn't expect Samsung to hit $299.

Samsung is also pitching the glasses differently from Meta. Rather than a device that replaces your phone, it describes them as an extension of it, much like a smartwatch.

## What about Israel?

There's no information about an official launch here yet, and there's a precedent suggesting it could take a while. Samsung's Galaxy XR headset also launched first in Korea and the US, and only reached the UK in July. If you're interested, the first realistic route will probably be a personal import from the US, without an official importer's warranty. It's also worth checking whether features like translation and Gemini work properly in Hebrew before you order.
