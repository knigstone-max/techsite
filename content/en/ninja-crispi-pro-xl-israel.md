The air fryer stopped being a gadget a long time ago. In plenty of Israeli kitchens it now does more work than the oven. Most of them share one weak point, though: a non-stick drawer that starts to flake after a year or two, traps grease in the corners and needs scrubbing. Ninja's answer is to drop the drawer altogether.

The Ninja CRISPi PRO XL (model AS101) is a heating head that sits on top of a glass dish. You cook in the glass, watch the food through it, carry the same dish to the table, and put the leftovers in the fridge with a lid. Sarig, Ninja's official importer in Israel, sells it for ₪1,099 (about $344) with a three-year warranty.

![Ninja CRISPi PRO XL annotated: 2,050 W heating unit, 5.7-litre glass dish, modular base and dishwasher-safe parts](img/posts/ninja-crispi-pro-annotated-en.webp "A heating head, a glass dish and a base. That is the whole idea. Graphic: TechSite")

## How it works

The top unit puts out 2,050 W and sits over one of two CleanCrisp glass dishes. The big one, 5.7 litres, feeds up to six people according to Ninja, and takes a whole chicken of up to 1.2 kg with vegetables. The small one, 2.3 litres, is for sides, a meal for one or two, and reheating. Each dish comes with a crisper plate that lifts food off the bottom, and a storage lid.

The modular base adjusts to the height of each dish, so the food sits at the right distance from the heater. It has heat-resistant feet, so you can lift the dish out and set it straight on the counter or table.

## Seven programs

Sarig lists seven programs: Max Crisp for fast, intense heat, Air Fry, Roast, Bake and Prove for baking and rising dough, Recrisp for bringing leftovers back to life, and Dehydrate for drying fruit and vegetables. You control temperature and time on all of them.

Ninja says air frying in it uses up to 75% less fat than traditional frying. That is the maker's number, and it is close to what nearly every air fryer claims.

## The glass: the upside and the catch

The dishes are made from glass that handles temperature swings, and Ninja stresses they are free of PFAS and PTFE, meaning no Teflon-style coating. If those chemicals worry you, or you are simply tired of throwing out peeling drawers, the appeal is obvious. Dishes, crisper plates and lids all go in the dishwasher.

There is another side. A 5.7-litre glass dish full of food is heavy, and the glass stays hot long after cooking ends. With small kids around, think about where you set it down. And glass, in the end, can break.

## Israeli price vs abroad

₪1,099 is not cheap for an air fryer. In the same Sarig store, the smaller CRISPi (FN103) is ₪649 (about $203), and regular Ninja air fryers start at ₪499. On the other hand, SharkNinja's own UK store lists this model at £264.99, roughly ₪1,134 or $355. For once, the Israeli price is slightly lower than in Europe, which does not happen often.

Sarig is also running a separate deal right now: spend over ₪199 and you can add a 3.3-litre NINJA Premium pot for ₪99. Buying from the official importer also gets you Hebrew instructions and a booklet of kosher recipes.

## Who it suits

If your current air fryer works and you are happy with it, there is no reason to rush. The CRISPi PRO XL makes sense as a first air fryer or a replacement for a tired one, if you want to get away from coatings. It also suits families who cook in bulk, run everything through the dishwasher and are done moving food from drawer to serving plate to fridge box. If you mostly cook for yourself, the smaller model saves you ₪450.
