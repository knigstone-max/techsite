After [the laundry room](/techsite/en/posts/washers-dryers-2026-innovations-roundup/), [the kitchen](/techsite/en/posts/kitchen-appliances-2026-innovations-roundup/) and [dishwashers](/techsite/en/posts/dishwashers-2026-innovations-roundup/), our year-end series moves to the bathroom. The hair dryer may be the appliance the most people in a household use every day, and this year the makers worked hard on it.

The direction is clear: less heat, more sensors, smaller machines. Here's what we saw in 2026, from whom, and what has reached Israeli shops.

![Hair dryers and stylers in 2026: sensors and apps, a compact travel dryer, oil in the airflow and stronger air curls, and what's available in Israel](img/posts/hair-care-2026-trends-en.webp "Four ideas that kept coming back this year. Graphic: TechSite")

## 1. A dryer that looks at your hair before it starts

The strangest announcement of the year, in a good way, came from **Dreame** at CES in January. The **Pilot 20** has two small robotic arms: one carries sensors that scan your scalp and check how damaged your hair is, the other adjusts heat, air speed and airflow direction in real time. The motor spins at 150,000 rpm and pushes air at 70 metres per second, and the dryer stores three profiles so everyone at home gets their own settings.

Dreame hasn't given a price or a date, and we don't know how well the sensors work in practice. For now it's mostly a sign of where the market is going.

What did reach Israel from the same announcement is the **AirStyle Pro HI**, a styler that connects to Wi-Fi and an app that recognises your hair type and adjusts the styling. Zap lists it at 25 shops, **from ₪1,325 (about $397)** up to ₪1,679 (about $504).

![Dreame's booth at CES 2026, with a sparkling wall reading Personal Care and visitors trying the devices](img/posts/dreame-ces-2026-personal-care-booth.webp "Dreame's personal care booth at CES 2026. Image: Dreame")

## 2. Small, folding and ready to fly

The **Dyson Supersonic Travel** came out in April: 32% smaller and 25% lighter than the full Supersonic, with universal voltage that adapts to whatever country you're in. It measures air temperature 100 times a second and takes the attachments from the Supersonic you may already own. Its launch price was $299.99 (about ₪1,000).

**Shark** went the same way with the **SpeedStyle Pro FLEX**, a dryer that folds for storage or a gym bag, with a Scalp Shield mode that limits heat near the scalp. It launched in Australia in June.

The Israeli check: the Supersonic Travel isn't in Dyson Israel's official store. What is on sale there today is the **Supersonic Nural** at ₪1,750 (about $525, currently out of stock) and the **Supersonic r Professional** at ₪1,990 (about $597), down from ₪2,399.

## 3. Oil and serum in the airflow

The **Laifen Swift 4** may be the simplest idea of the year. You put a pod of oil or serum into a special nozzle, and the dryer breaks it into fine particles and spreads it through your hair at a steady 58°C. Beyond that it's a light dryer: 407 grams, a 115,000 rpm motor and six heat settings. It launched in September, and Laifen's US store currently sells it for $149.99 (about ₪500), down from $199.99.

Dreame showed something similar: the **Miracle Pro** sprays a fine moisturising mist as it dries and adds red light for the scalp.

## 4. Stronger curls without a hot iron

The **Shark FlexStyle IonCurl** launched in the US in July at $349.99 (about ₪1,167). It adds ions to fight frizz and stronger airflow that wraps hair tighter around the barrel, so curls hold longer. It joins the original FlexStyle and the FlexStyle Glam in the same family.

## What's on sale in Israel, and what isn't

Most of 2026's new models haven't reached official import yet. Here's what we found:

- **Dreame AirStyle Pro HI:** available, from ₪1,325 (about $397) on Zap
- **Dyson Supersonic Nural and Supersonic r Professional:** on Dyson Israel's site, ₪1,750 and ₪1,990 (about $525 and $597)
- **Dyson Supersonic Travel, Laifen Swift 4, Shark FlexStyle IonCurl:** we didn't find them on official import

## What to check before you buy

**Voltage.** This matters most if you order from abroad. The US product page for the Laifen Swift 4 says 110 to 120 volts. Israel runs on 230 volts. A dryer built for the US isn't meant for an Israeli socket, and a plug adapter doesn't fix that. A universal-voltage model like the Supersonic Travel is a different story, but check the product page before you order.

**Warranty.** A dryer with a high-speed motor and sensors isn't something you want to ship abroad for repair. Official import is worth a few hundred shekels here.

**Heat.** Almost every maker talked about heat control this year. It helps, but what makes the difference is how you use it: distance from your hair and a lower heat setting. No gadget replaces that.

**And if it's a Hanukkah gift:** an all-in-one styler sounds like a safe present, but it really suits people who style their hair every day. For someone who just dries it, a fast, light dryer is the better gift, and a cheaper one.
