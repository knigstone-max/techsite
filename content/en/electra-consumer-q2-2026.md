Electra Consumer Products is one of the big players in Israel's electronics market: it owns Machsanei Hashmal and Shekem Electric, makes and sells air conditioners, and also runs Carrefour and adidas in Israel. Its second-quarter 2026 results, published on 20 August, give a good look at what is happening in stores.

## The headline numbers

- Revenue of about ₪1.8 billion (about $584 million), up 1.7%
- Operating profit of about ₪82 million (about $26.6 million), up 38.5%
- Net profit of about ₪30 million (about $9.7 million), up about 19.5%
- A ₪45 million dividend (about $14.6 million), ₪1.97 a share, paid in October

## Electronics retail

The chains sold ₪650 million (about $211 million) in the quarter, against ₪639 million a year earlier, and segment profit rose to ₪25 million. The company credits same-store growth, online sales and a growing share of directly imported products. That fits what we saw later in the summer: Blackview, Pixel and INOKIM, three brands Electra brings in itself.

## Air conditioners: more units, less money

In consumer electrical products, which includes ACs, revenue fell 3.9% to ₪246 million (about $80 million). More units were sold, at lower prices. Segment profit still rose to ₪14 million thanks to better purchase prices and exchange rates. The company also won a franchise to distribute Samsung data-center cooling.

## What it means for shoppers

Falling AC prices are good news if you plan to replace a unit before next summer. More direct imports mean more brands in the big chains without a middleman, and also that you should check who stands behind the warranty.

*Dollar figures are our conversion at the Bank of Israel representative rate of about ₪3.08 per $1 (26.10.2026), rounded.*
