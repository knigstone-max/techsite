Motorola had more than a flagship phone to show this week. At the same event where it launched the Signature 27, it also announced two accessories meant to build a small ecosystem around it: a fitness watch called the Moto Watch 2 Fit and wireless earbuds called the Moto Buds 2 Ultra. Some markets will sell them as the Moto Watch Fit 27 and Moto Buds Ultra 27, so if you see both names, they are the same products.

What makes both worth a look is who Motorola brought in. The watch gets its fitness and recovery features from Finland's Polar, a familiar name to anyone who runs with a sports watch. The earbuds get their tuning and noise cancelling from Bose.

## Moto Watch 2 Fit: a fitness watch you don't charge every night

Battery is the headline. Motorola says the watch lasts up to 12 days on a charge, the longest of any moto watch so far. If you have ever moved from a smartwatch that needs the charger every evening, you know how much that changes daily life.

The bigger news is Polar. For the first time in this line, the watch gets several of Polar's well-known tools:

- **Running Index**, a score for aerobic fitness and running efficiency based on heart rate, which you can track week to week. Handy if you are training for the Tel Aviv or Jerusalem marathon.
- **Nightly Recharge** and **Sleep Plus Stages**, which look at how you slept and how well your body recovered from the day before.
- **Energy Sources**, which shows where your body drew energy during a workout: fat, carbs or protein.
- **Serene**, a guided breathing exercise with real-time feedback.

On top of that, Motorola adds heart rate and SpO2 tracking, stress insights, automatic detection of more than 100 workout types and dual-frequency GPS, which should hold a better fix when you run between tall buildings. Menstrual cycle tracking also arrives on a moto watch for the first time, built into the app so you don't need a separate tracker.

The 1.85-inch display has 2 mm bezels and peaks at 1,500 nits, bright enough for a Tel Aviv August afternoon. It sits under Gorilla Glass 3 in an aluminum frame, and the watch is rated IP68 and 5ATM, so it can come to the pool. You can also take calls from your wrist over Bluetooth. It comes in three Pantone shades: Stratified Sea (blue), Pigeon (grey) and Ballerina (pink).

## Moto Buds 2 Ultra: Bose in your ears

Each earbud pairs two drivers: an 11 mm dynamic driver for bass and body, and a small balanced armature for fine detail. Bose handled the tuning, branded SoundDesign, and the noise cancelling, Bose QuietControl, is also theirs. There is a Transparency Mode for hearing what is around you, and Hi-Res Audio support.

![Moto Buds 2 Ultra annotated: up to 8 hours of playback, 11 mm driver plus balanced armature, Bose noise cancelling](img/posts/moto-buds-2-ultra-annotated-en.webp "Two drivers per bud, tuned by Bose. Graphic: TechSite")

Every bud has three microphones plus a voice pickup unit that senses the vibration of your speech. Motorola is pitching this at creators too, people who record voiceovers or podcasts straight from their earbuds. With a compatible Motorola phone you also get live translation in your ear, nod-or-shake head gestures for calls, and Dolby Atmos with head tracking.

Battery life is up to 8 hours per charge and 37 hours in total with the case, which charges wirelessly. Both buds and case are rated IP57, and you get dual-device connection and support for Google's Find Hub network.

## Prices, and the Israel question

Motorola lists the watch from €99 (about ₪354, or $111) and the earbuds at €199 (about ₪711, or $223). Both roll out over the coming weeks across Europe, the Middle East, Latin America, North America and Asia-Pacific.

![Moto Watch 2 Fit and Moto Buds 2 Ultra prices in euros, shekels and dollars, with a spec comparison](img/posts/moto-watch-buds-prices-en.webp "Official European prices, converted to shekels and dollars. Graphic: TechSite")

There is no Israeli price yet, and no official word on local availability. Going by past launches, a local price with VAT and an importer warranty usually lands above a straight conversion. If the watch arrives here for under ₪400, it will be up against budget bands and watches from Xiaomi and Huawei, with one clear edge: Polar's metrics, which usually come on pricier watches.

## Bottom line

This pair makes the most sense if you already carry a Motorola phone, since features like live translation and Dolby Atmos only work with one. Even without that, a watch with 12 days of battery and Polar insights, and earbuds with Bose noise cancelling for around €200, is a solid offer. We will update once there is an Israeli price.
