The steam race in wet-dry vacuums keeps going. A few months ago 140°C sounded like a lot, then 160°C models showed up, and now **Tineco** is talking about 230. The Ecovacs-owned brand, one of the biggest names in floor washers, launched two new models in Vietnam on November 13: the **Floor One S9 Artist SuperSteam** and the **Floor One S7 SuperSteam**. Both run on its new steam generation, SuperSteam 3.0.

## What 230°C means

Tineco calls the system HyperSteam. Water is heated in a boiler, the steam is heated again, and it comes out through **eight nozzles** in the cleaning head. The point is to soften dried grease and grime stuck in grout lines, the stuff ordinary-temperature water struggles with.

One caveat: one Vietnamese report says 230°C is the temperature of the heating element, while others present it as the steam temperature. Either way, it's a higher figure than anything sold in Israel right now.

## What both models share

- **30,000Pa suction**, with a roller that can reverse direction for stubborn stains
- **DualBlock** anti-tangle design to stop hair wrapping around the roller
- **180° lay-flat** with a 13 cm tall body, for getting under sofas and beds
- **Edge cleaning on three sides** of the head
- **An iLoop sensor** that reads how dirty the floor is and adjusts suction and water on its own
- **FlashDry**: after cleaning, the dock washes the roller with hot water and dries it with 110°C air in five minutes, according to Tineco
- **SmoothDrive** power assist that makes pushing easier

## What only the S9 Artist SuperSteam gets

The pricier model adds **electrolysed water**, water run through electrolysis to improve disinfection. It also has an **anti-odour stick** in the dirty water tank, which Tineco says keeps smells away for up to 14 days, and a 0.88-litre clean water tank on top of the body to add pressure on the floor. The battery lasts up to **110 minutes** in quiet mode and up to **35 minutes** with steam. Thirty-five minutes covers an average flat, but not a large house with steam on every floor.

The S7 SuperSteam drops the electrolysis and the odour stick and adds an **LED headlight** to show dirt under furniture. It also reaches 110 minutes in quiet mode.

## Price and availability

In Vietnam both models launched at roughly half their list price, with a window-cleaning robot thrown in. We couldn't find any announced date for Europe or Israel.

For a sense of what might happen here, look at Spain, where Tineco entered the market two weeks ago with the previous generation. The S9 Artist Steam Pro costs €749.99 there (about ₪2,682, or $822), and the S7 Stretch Steam Plus with 160°C steam costs €459.99 (about ₪1,645, or $504).

## Should you wait?

If your main problem is kitchen grease and dried-on stains, hotter steam can help, and it's worth seeing whether the new generation arrives before you buy a steam model now. If you mostly need a daily pass over dust and crumbs, steam isn't the point. Hot-air roller drying, anti-tangle design and decent runtime matter more, and cheaper models already have them.
