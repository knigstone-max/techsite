If you own a mopping robot, you know the moment. A coffee spill from yesterday, the robot rolls over it, smears it a little and moves on. Fresh mess is easy. Dried mess is another story, and that's exactly what ECOVACS built the **DEEBOT X12S OmniCyclone** for. When it spots a stain, it sprays water and cleaning solution on it first, and only then goes over it with the roller.

[We covered its Israeli launch](/techsite/en/posts/ecovacs-deebot-x12s-omnicyclone-israel/) in October. Six weeks on, the price has dropped, which makes it a good time for a proper look. I went through the importer's product page, the full US spec sheet (that's where the interesting details are), prices here and abroad, and lined it up against the three robots we reviewed over the past month.

![ECOVACS DEEBOT X12S with numbered callouts: 1.6-litre cyclone bin, 9.8 cm body, OZMO ROLLER 3.0 and the washing tray in the dock](img/posts/ecovacs-x12s-annotated-en.webp "What's in it, according to ECOVACS. Graphic: TechSite")

## What it costs in Israel

Ronlight, ECOVACS' official importer, now sells it on its own site for **₪4,499 (about $1,331)**, down from ₪4,999 (about $1,479) at launch. It's part of a November sale, so don't count on that price lasting past the end of the month.

In the US, ECOVACS is selling it for $899 (about ₪3,039) right now, against a list price of $1,022 (about ₪3,454), and that's before sales tax. The gap is big, as it almost always is with robots, but here you get an importer's warranty, a Hebrew app and a voice assistant that understands Hebrew.

Read the warranty terms carefully: Ronlight gives one year of full coverage plus two more years of limited coverage, provided you register the product online. The battery is covered for 12 months. Register the robot the day it arrives.

## Mopping: a roller instead of pads

The heart of the X12S is the **OZMO ROLLER 3.0**, a roller mop in place of the two spinning pads most robots used until a couple of years ago. While it works, the roller is rinsed with clean water nonstop, and an internal scraper pulls the dirty water out. On paper, that means it doesn't drag kitchen grime into the living room.

A few numbers:

- **27 cm long.** The US spec sheet has a small note worth knowing: that figure includes the end caps. The actual mopping surface is a little narrower
- **4,000Pa** of downward pressure
- **Rotation speed:** here the pages disagree. At launch the figure was 220 rpm, and the importer's page now says 260. It's not a dramatic difference, but it's a reminder to take marketing numbers with a pinch of salt
- **Near rugs** it lifts the roller by 15 mm and covers it, so the rug stays dry

ECOVACS claims the new roller leaves 67% less water on the floor. That's the maker's figure, with no detail on how it was measured. In practice, a roller that's rinsed nonstop is the best mopping method robots have right now, and that's why every rival has moved to it.

## FocusJet: the reason to buy it

Every robot at this price mops with a roller. What sets the X12S apart is **FocusJet**: the camera spots a dried stain, the robot sprays a targeted jet of water and cleaning solution on it to dissolve the mess, and only then goes over it with the roller. The importer describes it in three steps: spray, dissolve, roller pass.

In an Israeli home that covers more spots than you'd think: tahini that dripped by the counter, soy sauce, paw prints from a dog back from the balcony, juice the kids spilled by the fridge and didn't mention. Those are exactly the stains a regular robot smears.

Two caveats. First, FocusJet works on stains the camera can see. A clear spill on light tiles may slip under the radar. Second, the robot itself only carries 110 ml of water, per the US spec sheet. It goes back to the dock to refill, which is by design, but it also means more stops in a big home.

## Suction and hair

The X12S pulls **27,000Pa** according to the US spec sheet, from a 150 W motor that the importer describes as 53AW and 27 litres of airflow per second. ECOVACS claims a 60% improvement over the previous model. Again, the maker's numbers.

What matters more in a home with pets or long hair is **ZeroTangle 4.0**: a main brush with teeth at two angles and a spiral side brush, both meant to release hair instead of letting it wrap around them. The importer talks about "zero tangles". No brush really stays at zero for long, but it's the right direction, and a replacement main brush costs $15.99 on sale (about ₪54) on the US site when you need one.

Noise: 60.2 dB vacuuming and 62.2 dB vacuuming and mopping, per the spec sheet. That's reasonable for a robot. The loud part is emptying into the dock, at 73 dB. It's brief, but if the robot runs while someone sleeps, schedule it accordingly.

## Navigation and obstacles

The robot is **9.8 cm** tall, low enough for most sofas and beds, and weighs 5.45 kg. It navigates with a dToF sensor, a camera and two structured-light modules that help it see obstacles under furniture and in dark corners. There's a physical shutter for the camera if that bothers you.

ECOVACS talks about climbing obstacles up to 40 mm, on 7.6 cm wheels. But the US spec sheet has another line: **with the roller fitted, it's 25 mm**. That's a real difference. Most thresholds between rooms in Israeli apartments are low, but the step out to the balcony, especially in older buildings, can be higher. Measure before you assume it'll make it. If you have tall thresholds, a robot with mechanical legs like the [Xiaomi Robot Vacuum 6 Max](/techsite/en/posts/xiaomi-robot-vacuum-6-max-review-israel/) or a climbing arm like the [Roborock Saros 20](/techsite/en/posts/roborock-saros-20-flow-complete-review-israel/) is the better choice.

## Battery and charging

A 4,000mAh battery with a full charge in two and a half hours. The trick is **PowerBoost**: every time the robot goes back to rinse the roller it also charges, and ECOVACS says it gains 13% in three minutes. The importer claims up to 1,000 m² in a day. That was measured under ideal conditions, but for a four- or five-room apartment, battery life shouldn't be an issue.

## The dock: no bags, hot water

The **OmniCyclone** dock empties the robot into a clear **1.6-litre** cyclone bin, no bags needed. That saves you buying bags every few months, and you can see when it's full. On the other hand, emptying an open dust bin is a bit messier than dropping a sealed bag in the trash. If you're allergic to dust, think about that.

What else the dock does:

- **3.5 litres of clean water and 2.4 litres of dirty water.** In a normal home that's enough for several days
- **Washes the roller with hot water.** The pages are confusing here too: one spot says 100°C heating, another says 75°C. Most likely 100 is the heating element and 75 is what reaches the roller. Assume the lower figure
- **Dries with hot air,** for two, three (the default) or four hours
- **Heats the dirty water tank** to cut down on smells, and cleans its own tray
- **Doses detergent automatically**

The launch figures put it at 33.5 cm wide, 46.5 cm tall and 49.3 cm deep including the ramp. That's not small. Measure the spot before you order, especially if it needs to fit under a counter or in a niche.

## AGENT YIKO: a voice assistant in Hebrew

The YIKO 3.0 voice assistant understands Hebrew, which is rare for robot vacuums. The importer shows an example along the lines of "Hey YIKO, please don't clean the bedroom after 6 PM", and the assistant is meant to build weekly cleaning plans around your preferences. Most people will set the robot up once in the app and forget about it, but in a home with older parents or kids, Hebrew voice commands are a real plus.

## Against the competition

- **[eufy Omni S2](/techsite/en/posts/eufy-omni-s2-robot-vacuum-review/)** (about ₪4,500 when we reviewed it): stronger suction on paper at 30,000Pa, and a dock that does 12 jobs. No stain jet. At the same price, the choice is suction versus stain handling
- **[Xiaomi Robot Vacuum 6 Max](/techsite/en/posts/xiaomi-robot-vacuum-6-max-review-israel/)** (₪4,690 when we reviewed it): mechanical legs for 6 cm thresholds and an arm for corners. If you have high thresholds, it's the better pick
- **[Roborock Saros 20](/techsite/en/posts/roborock-saros-20-flow-complete-review-israel/)** (₪4,990 when we reviewed it): the most advanced at navigation and climbing, a body under 9 cm, and the most expensive of the group

Rival prices come from our reviews and may have changed, especially during this sale season. Check before you decide.

## Who it's for

Good fit: a home with a busy kitchen, kids or a dog, where dried stains are the main problem. Anyone who doesn't want to keep buying dust bags. Anyone who values a Hebrew voice assistant and a local importer.

Less of a fit: homes with tall thresholds between rooms or out to the balcony. People with dust allergies who prefer a sealed bag. And anyone after the strongest suction in this price range.

## The bottom line

8/10

The ECOVACS DEEBOT X12S is a good mopping robot with one idea that genuinely sets it apart: FocusJet, which soaks dried stains before it mops. Add a roller that's rinsed nonstop, a bagless dock that dries with hot air, quick top-up charging during the job and a Hebrew voice assistant. On the other side, obstacle climbing with the roller fitted is lower than the ads suggest, the official pages contradict each other on a few numbers, and the dock is big. At ₪4,499 it's a fair rival to eufy and Xiaomi. At the ₪4,999 launch price it was harder to justify.

**What I liked**

- FocusJet: dedicated handling for dried stains, something its direct rivals don't have
- A 27 cm roller rinsed nonstop that lifts 15 mm on rugs
- A bagless dock with hot-water washing and hot-air drying
- 13% battery in three minutes at every rinse stop
- A voice assistant that understands Hebrew, an official importer and a Hebrew app
- ₪500 off since launch

**What I liked less**

- With the roller fitted it only climbs 25 mm, not 40
- The official pages disagree on roller speed and water temperature
- Emptying into the dock is loud, at 73 dB
- An open cyclone bin is less allergy-friendly than a bag
- The dock takes up space
