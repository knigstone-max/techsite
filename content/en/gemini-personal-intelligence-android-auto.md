Google announced on October 20 that Gemini is now available worldwide on Android Auto and in cars with Google built-in. Alongside that, it started rolling out Personal Intelligence in the car, a feature that lets Gemini pull details from your own Google apps when it answers.

## What it connects to

With your permission, Personal Intelligence reads from Gmail, Google Calendar and the rest of Workspace, plus Google Photos, Google Maps, YouTube and Search. The point is that you don't have to spell out the context or look anything up on your phone while driving.

Google's own examples:

- "What's my flight gate? And is my flight on time?" answered in one go, without opening the confirmation email.
- "Take me to my doctor's appointment," without knowing the address.
- "Find the car wash from the photo I took last week and navigate there."
- Restaurant suggestions along the route that fit what you usually like, for example vegetarian places if that's your habit.

## Privacy and settings

The feature is off until you turn it on, and you can switch it off at any time in Gemini settings. If you've already turned it on in the Gemini app on your phone, the setting carries over to the car. In cars with Google built-in, personal answers only appear when your phone is connected and you're in the car. Without Personal Intelligence, navigation, music, calls and general questions keep working; the answers just aren't personalized.

## Who gets it

It's rolling out now on Android Auto, and in the coming months on cars with Google built-in. According to Android Authority, it's limited to personal Google accounts of users 18 and over. Workspace business, enterprise and education accounts are excluded, and it isn't available in the European Economic Area, Switzerland, the UK or Nigeria.
