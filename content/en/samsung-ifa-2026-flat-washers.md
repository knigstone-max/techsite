The washing machine is one of the few appliances that still looks like the 1990s: a protruding front, a round porthole and a control panel that fights with the cabinet. Samsung wants its mainstream models to get the clean line it kept for premium ones.

## What was shown

At Samsung Connect, the company's own show at Humboldt Carré in Berlin alongside IFA, from 2 to 6 September, Samsung displayed a new front-loader line with a flat front:

- **A uniform front** with no protruding parts, easier to fit into a cabinet or niche
- **A larger door** that opens wider, so bedding goes in and out more easily
- **Bubble Shot**, which sprays water across the drum to cut wash time
- **Ecobubble**, which dissolves detergent before it reaches the clothes so low-temperature washes still clean well

According to Digit's roundup, Samsung also showed two Bespoke AI washers that week: a 10kg model aiming at A-70% efficiency and a 13kg model that fits a standard 60cm depth.

## Why it matters here

In a typical Israeli apartment the washer sits on a narrow service balcony or inside a bathroom cabinet. A flat front and 60cm depth are exactly what let you close the cabinet door.

One caveat: Samsung says these are development-stage display models, specs may change and commercial availability has not been decided. Do not wait for them if your washer died today.
