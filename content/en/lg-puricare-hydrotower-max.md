LG Electronics put the PuriCare HydroTower Max on sale in South Korea on October 18. It's a humidifier and an air purifier in one body, and LG pitches it at living rooms and other large spaces. The existing HydroTower stays in the lineup for bedrooms and smaller rooms.

## How the humidifier works

Water first goes through a filter, then gets heated to 100°C to kill bacteria. After that, an ultrasonic module turns it into a fine mist, and the air that carries the mist out has already passed through the purifier filter. When a humidifying session ends, the unit pulls back the water left inside and dries itself. LG calls this a four-step hygiene process: filtering, sterilizing, purifying and drying.

A new AI humidification mode reads the room's size and temperature and sets the output on its own, so you don't have to keep adjusting it. Maximum output is 850 cc per hour.

## Numbers that matter

- Stainless steel water tank: 4.5 liters
- Continuous run time: up to 18 hours in standard mode at medium fan speed, so one refill a day
- Air purification coverage: about 66 square meters
- Night mode: 26 dB, and a light sensor switches the lights off automatically
- Humidifying and purifying can run separately or together

There are also mood lights (three themed effects and 12 colors) and hidden wheels on the bottom for moving it between rooms.

## Price and availability

LG lists the price at 2.39 million won. Buyers who take it on LG's subscription plan get regular professional maintenance, including a full teardown and internal cleaning in the third year. The announcement covers the Korean market only. LG hasn't said anything about other countries yet.
