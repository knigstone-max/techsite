For years Apple said touchscreens didn't belong on laptops. On October 28 it changed its mind. The new MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display is the first Mac you can touch, and along with that it gets a new OLED screen, an iPhone-style Dynamic Island and a lighter body.

The executive quoted in the announcement is Johny Srouji, Apple's chief hardware officer, who was born in Haifa. He calls it "the world's best laptop display".

## What's new

- **PROLED XDR display:** tandem OLED, meaning two light-emitting layers. Up to 2,000 nits peak, a 10,000,000:1 contrast ratio, 10-120Hz refresh, and both P3 and Adobe RGB color. The screens are slightly bigger too, at 14.2 and 16.3 inches.
- **Touch:** the touch sensor is built into the panel rather than layered on top, which Apple says cuts latency. You can scroll, pinch to zoom and drag windows, and existing apps work right away because macOS turns taps into clicks. The hinge was redesigned so the screen doesn't wobble when you touch it.
- **Dynamic Island:** at the top of the display, and it's now home to Spotlight, Live Activities and Siri AI.
- **Weight:** the 14-inch is 3 pounds (about 1.36kg), half a pound lighter than before. The 16-inch is 4 pounds (about 1.81kg).
- **Chips:** M5 Pro or M5 Max, up to 128GB of memory and up to 16TB of storage.
- **Battery:** up to 24 hours according to Apple, and fast charging to 50% in 30 minutes.
- **Ports:** three Thunderbolt 5, HDMI, an SD card slot, MagSafe and a headphone jack.

## The catch for Hebrew users

There's a footnote here. According to Apple's own notes, Spotlight only moves into the Dynamic Island when Siri AI is available and switched on. Siri AI currently works in English, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish, and Apple Intelligence doesn't support Hebrew. So if your Mac is set to Hebrew, you get the screen, the touch and the speed, but not everything Apple is showing off around the Island and AI.

## Price

US starting prices, before tax:

- **14-inch, M5 Pro:** $2,899 (about ₪9,068)
- **16-inch, M5 Pro:** $3,399 (about ₪10,632)
- **14-inch, M5 Max:** $4,499 (about ₪14,073)
- **16-inch, M5 Max:** $4,799 (about ₪15,011)

![A table of the four models with US dollar prices and their shekel equivalents](img/posts/macbook-pro-proled-xdr-prices-en.webp "US starting prices converted to shekels. No Israeli price has been announced")

As Calcalist points out, that's a big jump over the current models. Pre-orders opened in 30 countries and regions, and deliveries start November 6. Israel isn't mentioned, and there's no official Israeli price yet. When there is one, it'll include VAT, so don't expect the converted figures above.
