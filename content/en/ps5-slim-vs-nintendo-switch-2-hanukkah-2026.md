Hanukkah starts next Friday, and in plenty of Israeli homes the question this year isn't whether to buy a console but which one. On one side there's the PS5, which has been in demand since [GTA 6 came out](/techsite/en/posts/gta-6-review/) on November 19 and is now sold in supermarket chains at a price we haven't seen before. On the other, the Nintendo Switch 2, which you can play on the TV or in your hands.

These two aren't really fighting over the same player. So before the prices, it's worth knowing what each one actually gives you.

![Comparison table: PS5 Slim vs Nintendo Switch 2 on this week's cheapest price, electronics-chain prices, storage, where you play, GTA 6 and warranty](img/posts/ps5-slim-vs-switch-2-table-en.webp "The two consoles side by side. Graphic: TechSite")

## This week's prices

**PS5 Slim:** Victory sells both the disc and digital versions for **₪1,999 (about $600)** to holders of its Victory MAX credit card and **₪2,350 (about $705)** to everyone else. Mahsanei Hashuk sells the digital version for ₪1,999, but only at its HaSapan branch in Eilat and while stock lasts. Both are parallel imports with a one-year warranty, which at Victory comes from Dynamica.

The electronics chains are a different picture. According to Channel 14's check, the disc PS5 Slim costs ₪3,375 to ₪3,745 (about $1,012 to $1,123) at Ivory, starts at ₪3,239 (about $972) on Zap, and the digital version runs ₪3,348 to ₪3,600. Bug didn't have it in stock.

**Nintendo Switch 2:** KSP sells it today for **₪2,195 to ₪2,199 (about $658 to $660)** on official import, in bundles that include a game, one of them with three months of Nintendo Switch Online. The same bundles cost about ₪1,860 at KSP's Eilat price. At Bug, the console alone was ₪2,199 earlier this month, according to Dofek.

In short: if you catch a PS5 at the supermarket, the gap between them almost disappears. At an electronics chain, the PS5 costs ₪1,000 or more extra.

## What you get with the PS5

- **The big games.** GTA 6, plus Sony's exclusives like Ghost of Yōtei. GTA 6 needs a PS5 or an Xbox Series. It didn't come out on PS4, PC or Switch 2.
- **TV only.** The PS5 sits next to the screen and stays there.
- **1TB of storage** on the disc model. Big games take a lot of space, so it fills up faster than you'd think.

## What you get with the Switch 2

- **Handheld and TV.** A 7.9-inch screen, so you can take it to grandma's, on a trip or to another room without fighting over the TV.
- **Family games.** Nintendo's strength is games that every age plays together, and most original Switch games run on it.
- **256GB of storage.** To add room for games you need a microSD Express card. A regular microSD card won't run games, only store screenshots and videos.
- **Mind GameChat:** according to Dofek, the console's voice chat has required a paid Nintendo Switch Online subscription since March 31, 2026.

## Who should get which

**PS5 if:** it's for a teenager or an adult, there's a free TV at home, and GTA 6 is the reason to buy. At ₪1,999 in a supermarket it's a good deal, as long as you're fine with a parallel-import warranty.

**Switch 2 if:** it's for kids or the whole family, you want to play away from the living room, or there's already a library of original Switch games in the house.

## What to check before you pay

- **Official or parallel import.** The supermarket PS5 is a parallel import with one year of warranty. Find out where the service lab is before you buy. The Switch 2 at KSP is an official import, with one year plus another if you register on the importer's site.
- **Branch stock.** Victory hasn't published a list of branches. Call before you drive over.
- **Digital or disc.** At Victory both cost the same, so the disc version is the better buy. It also lets you buy and sell second-hand games.
- **Prices move.** It's Black Friday weekend. Check again on the day you buy.
