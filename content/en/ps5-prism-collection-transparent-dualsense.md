If you grew up with late-90s consoles, you remember see-through plastic with the circuit boards and screws showing. Sony is bringing that look to the PS5. On October 28 it announced the Prism Collection: DualSense controllers and console covers with a glossy transparent finish.

## Three colors

- **Flare Orange:** a loud orange. Sony's design team says it takes on a rosy tint where layers of the clear material overlap.
- **Emerald Green:** a deep green that gets more depth from the transparency.
- **Graphite Gray:** a cool, understated gray.

Each color has a matching controller and console cover, so you can put together a full set.

![A transparent Flare Orange DualSense from the front and back, with arrows pointing out the clear back, the parts visible under the touchpad, and the rosy tint where layers overlap](img/posts/ps5-prism-dualsense-annotated-en.webp "What the plastic shows: the back is clear too, and you can see the internals under the touchpad")

## What the designers say

Leo Cardoso from Sony's Color, Material and Finish team says they know how much fans wanted transparent controllers back, and the aim was a modern take on the late-90s see-through trend that fits the PS5's industrial design. Satoshi Aoyagi from the same team explains that the high-gloss clear finish carries across the back of the controller so you can see the tech inside, and that the middle cover gets a metallic finish to tie the surfaces together.

## Price and dates

- **DualSense controller:** $84.99 RRP (about ₪266), €84.99 or £74.99.
- **Console cover:** $74.99 (about ₪235), €74.99 or £64.99.
- **Pre-orders:** from November 10, 2026 at 10am local time, direct from PlayStation where available and at participating retailers.
- **Launch:** from February 4, 2027. Sony says exact dates and availability can vary by country.

The covers are sold mainly direct from PlayStation, and elsewhere in limited quantities through select retailers.

## What about Israel

Israel isn't on the list of countries where you can order direct from PlayStation. That list is the US, UK, France, Germany, Austria, Belgium, the Netherlands, Luxembourg, Spain, Portugal and Italy. So in Israel these will come, if they come, through the importer and local stores, and no Israeli price has been announced. The shekel figures here use the Bank of Israel rate from October 28 (₪3.128 to the dollar), without VAT or shipping, so the shelf price here will probably be different.

The covers have even tighter availability, so anyone after a matching set should expect to do some hunting.
