An air purifier is something most people buy once and forget about, until the red filter light comes on. But 2026 brought some interesting changes to the category. Makers stopped competing only on how many square metres a purifier covers and started working on noise, on how far the clean air travels, on filters that last for years, and on machines that do three jobs instead of one.

This is our seventh year-end roundup, after [washers and dryers](/techsite/en/posts/washers-dryers-2026-innovations-roundup/), [fridges](/techsite/en/posts/fridges-2026-innovations-roundup/), [floor cleaning](/techsite/en/posts/floor-cleaning-2026-innovations-roundup/), [the kitchen](/techsite/en/posts/kitchen-appliances-2026-innovations-roundup/), [TVs](/techsite/en/posts/tvs-2026-innovations-roundup/) and [air conditioners](/techsite/en/posts/air-conditioners-2026-innovations-roundup/).

## 1. Quiet, with air that still reaches the far wall

The classic purifier problem: on high it's loud, and on low the clean air stays next to it and never reaches the other side of the room. Dyson built its whole new purifier range around that. In September it unveiled **HushJet**, a new nozzle that the company says borrows from aviation engineering to move a lot of air with less noise and project it a long way forward.

There are three models. The **HushJet Pure Cool+ Formaldehyde** and the **HushJet Hot Cool Pure+** are $599 each in the US (about ₪2,004), and the big **HushJet Big+Quiet Cool Pure+ Formaldehyde** is $1,099 (about ₪3,676). Dyson says the mid-size model purifies up to three times more powerfully than its previous range, and that the big one is designed to clean several rooms through an open door.

## 2. Three machines in one body

A purifier that doubles as a fan isn't new, but this year more brands added heat. Dyson's **HushJet Hot Cool Pure+** purifies, heats and cools, and it's the most powerful 3-in-1 Dyson has made.

In November Honeywell launched the **HEPA 3-in-1 Air Comfort System with Turbo360** in Canada: a purifier with HEPA and carbon filters, a fan, and 360° circulation that pushes the warm air collecting near the ceiling back down. It's rated for rooms up to about 37 m² (398 sq ft) and costs CA$329.99 (about ₪770, or $230). Note that it isn't a heater: it moves warm air that's already in the room.

Why does this matter here? An average Israeli flat doesn't have room for three separate machines in one corner. And as our [room-heating comparison](/techsite/en/posts/heating-ac-vs-oil-radiator-vs-fan-heater-cost-israel/) shows, resistive heating is expensive at peak hours, however good-looking the box.

## 3. A filter that lasts six years

The red filter light is why so many purifiers end up as expensive fans. Shark's answer is **BreatheClear NeverChange**: up to six years without a new filter, according to Shark. The two compact models are already on sale in Israel at ₪799 (about $239) and ₪990 (about $296), and [we reviewed them](/techsite/en/posts/shark-breatheclear-neverchange-review-israel/) earlier this month.

The fine print: "six years" was measured under a Chinese standard, 12 hours a day at full power, until performance halves. After six years the purifier still works, at half strength. Still, it's the right direction: the shelf price is almost the whole lifetime cost.

## 4. Humidity from boiled water

Humidifiers are a known home for bacteria, thanks to water sitting in the tank. In October LG launched the **PuriCare HydroTower Max** in Korea, a humidifier and purifier in one that filters the water, boils it to 100°C and only then turns it into mist. When a session ends it pulls back the leftover water and dries itself. It purifies about 66 m² and costs 2.39 million won (about ₪5,946, or $1,778). We covered it [here](/techsite/en/posts/lg-puricare-hydrotower-max/).

It isn't sold in Israel, and on the coastal plain the problem is usually too much humidity rather than too little. But anyone in Jerusalem or the hills who runs the heating all winter knows that dry air.

## 5. Dehumidifiers with inverter compressors

Which brings us to the real Israeli problem: mould in winter, and laundry that won't dry. In March Samsung launched a new generation of **inverter dehumidifiers** in Korea, rated at 18, 21 and 23 litres a day. There's a 32 dB quiet mode, per a Korean certification lab's test, continuous drainage through a hose (sold separately) so you don't empty a tank every few hours, and up to 30% savings via a SmartThings mode. The Korean price: 679,000 to 819,000 won (about ₪1,689 to ₪2,038, or $505 to $609).

An inverter compressor in a dehumidifier works like the one in an AC: instead of cycling on and off, it slows down. That means less noise and less power, which is exactly what you want from a machine running all night in a bedroom.

## 6. A purifier that splits in two

Hyundai Quming, the home appliance brand of Korea's Hyundai Rental Care, launched **Delight Air** in November, a modular purifier. In the living room the units stack together; when you move or a child gets their own room, you split them. There are models for 77 m² and 47 m², a fan and cover you can remove and wash, UV care and a reusable filter frame. It's Korea-only for now, but the idea is a good one: a machine that adapts to your home instead of you buying a new one.

## What reached Israel

Not much of this year's crop:

- **Shark NeverChange** is on sale here, and it's probably the most useful item on this list.
- **Dyson Israel** still sells the previous generation: Purifier Cool, Purifier Hot+Cool and the Formaldehyde versions. HushJet isn't on the Israeli site.
- **LG, Samsung and Hyundai** launched these models in Korea only and haven't announced other markets.

## How to use this when you buy

- **Ask about the filter before the price.** What a replacement costs, and how often. A cheap purifier with an expensive filter can end up the priciest of all.
- **Look for CADR, not just square metres.** The room size on the box usually means one air change an hour. For allergies you need far more.
- **In an Israeli winter, a dehumidifier often does more than a purifier.** If there's mould on the walls or laundry takes two days to dry, start there.
- **3-in-1 is convenient, but not cheap to run.** Electric heating is electric heating, even from a nice-looking machine.
