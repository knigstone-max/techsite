Narwal is mostly known for robots. In early November it launched the **V50 Cube** in the US, which it calls its first cordless dry vacuum for the American market. The interesting part for us is that it's already here: the product page on Narwal's Israeli site went up back in August, and the vacuum sells there today for **₪1,699** (about $508), down from ₪1,899.

## The idea

A regular stick vacuum with a dock that empties it. Put the vacuum back and the dock pulls the dust out of its bin into a sealed bag. Narwal says the bag holds up to 100 days of dirt. During emptying the brush roll is cleared of tangled hair, and a ring scrapes off whatever is stuck to the bin walls. For allergy sufferers that's the main point: no opening a dust bin over the trash and breathing in the cloud.

![Narwal V50 Cube with numbered labels: auto-empty dock, a bag for up to 100 days, a 1.4 kg handheld body, 180-degree lay-flat, and a ZeroWrap floor head with DirtScan light](img/posts/narwal-v50-cube-annotated-en.webp "What's in it. Image: Narwal, labels: TechSite")

The handheld body weighs 1.4 kg, and the wand folds flat to 180 degrees to reach under low beds and sofas. Claimed suction is 210AW. Narwal also brought over a few ideas from its robots: **AI DirtSense**, which measures how much dirt it's picking up and adjusts power, and **CarpetFocus**, which lowers the brush plate on carpet for a tighter seal. A green light in the head shows dust in dark corners. The dock stores the accessories and charges a second battery.

## The Israeli and US pages don't agree

Put the Israeli product page next to Narwal's US announcement and a few numbers don't line up:

![Comparison of the Israeli Narwal V50 Cube product page and the US announcement: price, runtime, motor power, filtration layers, suction, weight and launch date](img/posts/narwal-v50-cube-israel-us-en.webp "One vacuum, two pages. Graphic: TechSite")

- **Runtime:** the Israeli site says up to 180 minutes with two batteries. The US release says up to 110 minutes with two batteries, in Eco mode with the main brush.
- **Motor:** 400W in Israel, 450W in the US.
- **Filtration:** 9 layers with H13 HEPA in Israel. In the US, 6 layers, dual H13 filters and a UV-C light in the dock.

These could be different versions, or one page could be wrong. If runtime matters to you, ask the seller before you buy. We'd plan around the lower number.

## Price: Israel vs the US

- **Israel:** ₪1,699 (about $508) on Narwal's Israeli site, down from ₪1,899 (about $568)
- **US:** $649.99 (about ₪2,173) list, and $499.99 (about ₪1,671) as a launch offer until November 30, on Narwal's site and Amazon. Before tax

For once, the Israeli price is close to the American one, and even below the US list price. Parts are available here too: on the Israeli site a dust bag kit costs ₪29 (about $9), filter kits ₪49 to ₪59 (about $15 to $18) and a main roller brush ₪79 (about $24). With a bagged system, that's a running cost to factor in.

## Who it's for

This is a dry vacuum only, with no mopping. If you want one machine for dust and washing tiles, see [our wet-dry vacuum guide](/techsite/en/posts/wet-dry-vacuum-buying-guide-israel/). The V50 Cube suits people who already have a mopping robot or a floor washer and want a light stick for crumbs, the sofa, the car and shelves, without the bin-emptying ritual. Self-emptying docks for stick vacuums are still uncommon, and ₪1,699 for a dry stick with a dock is a fair price for this market.
