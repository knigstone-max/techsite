Osher Ad is doing what the big electronics chains dislike: selling brand-new Apple gear hundreds of shekels below the usual Israeli list. This time it's the iPhone Duo, Apple's first foldable, in the 256GB configuration for ₪7,999 (about $2,526), with AirPods 5 alongside it for ₪399 (about $126).

Official Duo sales in Israel started on October 23. Less than two weeks later the supermarket already has stock in stores. Geektime reports Osher Ad brought in more than 1,000 units, but only the base 256GB model.

![iPhone Duo 256GB price comparison: Osher Ad at ₪7,999 vs KSP and Partner at ₪8,199, up to iDigital at ₪8,999](img/posts/osher-ad-iphone-duo-prices-en.webp "What you save against the chains. Graphic: TechSite")

## How much you save

According to a price roundup published by Walla Tech, KSP and Partner list the same model at ₪8,199, a ₪200 gap. Dynamics is at ₪8,299, Hot Mobile ₪8,399, Pelephone ₪8,499, and iDigital reaches ₪8,999. That's up to ₪1,000 less for the same storage tier.

The phone itself is the Duo we've already covered: 7.6-inch inner display, 5.4-inch cover display, A20 Pro chip. What changes here is mostly where you buy, how much you pay, and who stands behind the warranty.

## Parallel import, eSIM, DCS warranty

As with Osher Ad's earlier iPhone deals, this is parallel import. Warranty is one year through the DCS lab, not through the official Israeli distributors, and without the extensions those chains sell. Worth knowing before you queue at the checkout.

On SIM: the Duo is eSIM-only worldwide, including official stock. Parallel and official are the same here. What Osher Ad doesn't offer is larger storage. It's 256GB or nothing.

## AirPods 5, with a caveat

The chain is also selling AirPods 5 for ₪399. KSP has them at ₪499, Bug at ₪509, iDigital at ₪549. Savings run ₪100 to ₪150.

![AirPods 5 price comparison: Osher Ad at ₪399 vs KSP at ₪499, Bug at ₪509 and iDigital at ₪549](img/posts/osher-ad-airpods-5-prices-en.webp "The earbuds join the price war too. Graphic: TechSite")

Geektime notes this is the basic version: no wireless-charging case and no on-bud volume controls. Parallel import again. If you want the fuller AirPods 5 kit, confirm at the branch which SKU is actually on offer.

## Where to buy, and what to weigh

These don't sit on a shelf next to the milk. You take a ticket or carton to the till, pay, and collect there. Geektime lists seven branches only: Petah Tikva, Rishon LeZion, Talpiot, Tel Aviv, Afula, Kiryat Yam and Bat HaDar.

If you've already decided on a Duo and can live without a carrier-chain warranty and without a bigger drive, ₪7,999 is currently one of the lowest prices in Israel. If you want carrier service, an extended warranty, or 512GB and up, the few hundred shekels more at KSP or Partner may buy you quieter ownership.

*Conversions use the Bank of Israel representative rate for 5 Nov 2026: ₪3.167 per dollar.*
