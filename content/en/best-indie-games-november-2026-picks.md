November was busy. Beyond GTA VI, which swallowed all the attention, dozens of small games from independent studios came out, and we reviewed more than a dozen of them. A few disappointed. Most were pleasant surprises.

So we pulled together the ten with the highest scores and sorted them by the question you actually ask in the evening: what do I feel like playing right now? Prices are from the Israeli store on the day we reviewed each game. Sales move them around, so check before you buy.

![Table: November's ten indie picks with score, Israeli store price, US price, platforms and a short description](img/posts/indie-november-2026-picks-en.webp "All ten at a glance. Graphic: TechSite")

## Something calm

**[Horses of Hoofprint Bay](/techsite/en/posts/horses-of-hoofprint-bay-review/)** (8.5) is a hand-drawn stable management game from three developers who take horses seriously. Real daily care, training, competitions and breeding. It's one of the month's surprise cosy games, as long as you enjoy routine. **₪98.99 on Steam** ($26.99 in the US).

**[Village in the Shade](/techsite/en/posts/village-in-the-shade-review/)** (8.5) comes from the Yomawari team: a Stardew-style farming game set in a mountain village with eight strange rules and knocks on the door at midnight. Slow to start, but it pays off. It's also the priciest pick: **₪184.99 on Israeli Steam**, more than its $49.99 US price (about ₪167). It's on PS5, Switch 2 and Switch too. If you're on the fence, wait for a sale.

## A good story

**[After the Wane](/techsite/en/posts/after-the-wane-review/)** (8.5) is a graphic novel from French studio Nova-box about a young dancer who comes to the big city looking for the mother who left her. Hand-painted art, frame-by-frame dance animation and a twist worth reaching unspoiled. **₪51.95 on Steam** ($13.99). Note that it's all text, in English, with no Hebrew.

**[Virtue and a Sledgehammer](/techsite/en/posts/virtue-and-a-sledgehammer-review/)** (8) is the new game from Deconstructeam, the studio behind The Red Strings Club. A grieving woman goes home with a giant hammer to tear down a whole town of robots carrying her neighbours' memories. Short, angry and painful, about grief, family and AI. **₪54.49 on Steam** ($14.99).

## Something to think about

**[MONOMOON](/techsite/en/posts/monomoon-review/)** (8.5) is a Metroidvania with no combat and no instructions. Your only tool is a little moon you move with the mouse. One of the most original puzzle games of the year, with a few stretches where you'll feel completely lost. **₪45.99 on Steam** ($11.99).

## Some action

**[Nocturnal 2](/techsite/en/posts/nocturnal-2-review/)** (8) from Swiss studio Sunnyside Games is built around one idea: keep your sword on fire. Gorgeous, fast and short, with excellent boss fights. **₪32.99 on Steam** ($8.99), a price that's hard to argue with.

**[VOID/BREAKER](/techsite/en/posts/void-breaker-review/)** (8) is a roguelite shooter made by one person, the creator of The Entropy Centre. One gun per run, mods that turn it into something absurd, and arenas you can level to the ground. It's the only pick here that's also on Xbox, and it's on PS5 as well: **₪107 on the Israeli PS Store** (about $32), and it's included in Game Pass.

## Playing with friends

**[One More Lawn](/techsite/en/posts/one-more-lawn-review/)** (8) is about mowing lawns from a ride-on mower, with warm beer, a radio full of crude songs and up to six friends on the same lawn. 95% of its Steam reviews are positive. **₪25.49** ($6.99). Before buying it for kids, read what's on the radio in our review.

## Something small and cheap

**[Playrooms](/techsite/en/posts/playrooms-review/)** (7.5) comes from a small German studio and has you tidying ball pits in an empty play centre that feels like the Backrooms. Players love it, and there's a mode with no horror at all. **₪25.25** ($6.99), one of the cheapest fun evenings on Steam.

## Something that hurts (in a good way)

**[Casualties: Unknown](/techsite/en/posts/casualties-unknown-review/)** (8) is a 2D cave survival game from a solo developer that simulates blood flow, every limb and your mood. A splinter in your foot can end a run. 97% positive on Steam. **₪76.99** ($22.99). For people who love unforgiving games, and nobody else.

## If you don't have time to read all that

- **Best overall:** After the Wane or Horses of Hoofprint Bay, depending on whether you're a story person or a routine person.
- **Best value:** Nocturnal 2 at ₪32.99.
- **For an evening with friends:** One More Lawn.
- **On console:** VOID/BREAKER, which is also in Game Pass, or Village in the Shade on PS5 and Switch.

Most of these don't support Hebrew, but most have little text and simple English. The exception is After the Wane, which is all reading.
