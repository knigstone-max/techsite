Israel's appliance market got a new player this week, and not one you'd expect. HaMashbir, the department store chain best known for clothing, bedding and housewares, announced yesterday that it's moving into large electrical appliances. At first everything will be sold on its website, with free home delivery on the whole category. Later the chain plans to bring the products into its stores.

It isn't starting from zero. HaMashbir already sells small appliances in its branches, including Ninja products and coffee machines. Now it's adding the heavy stuff: fridges, freezers, washing machines, dryers, ovens and TVs, along with mixers, blenders, vacuums, steam irons and hair styling tools.

## Starting prices

According to the chain, ovens will start at ₪750 (about $236) and toasters at ₪300 (about $95). Fridges and freezers start at ₪1,099 (about $346), Samsung TVs at ₪1,170 (about $369), washing machines at ₪1,550 (about $489) and dryers at ₪1,590 (about $501).

These are entry prices, not a full price list. HaMashbir hasn't said which brands or models it will carry, apart from the Samsung TVs. So the real test will come when you can compare a specific model number against KSP, Machsanei Hashmal or Payless.

## Why now

Saar Shavit, HaMashbir's deputy CEO, said the move is part of a strategy to strengthen online sales, which he called a significant growth engine that lets the chain enter new categories. It's the second new category for HaMashbir this month, after it started selling toys.

The timing isn't random either. According to data from fintech company Hyp quoted by ice, electrical goods, electronics and computers led the start of November's shopping season, with transaction volume up about 72.6% in the first week of the month compared with the same week last year. Online sales make up roughly 15% to 20% of the sector, and that share keeps growing.

## Against Electra

The more interesting question is competition. Electra Consumer Products owns both Machsanei Hashmal and Shekem Electric, and its share of appliance retail rose from 22% in 2022 to 28% in 2025, according to figures the company presented this month and reported by Walla. It also increasingly imports directly from factories in China, which lets it price its own brand aggressively. By Electra's own comparison, its 12 kg washing machine sells for ₪1,790 (about $564).

So HaMashbir's ₪1,550 entry washer is already below that, at least on paper. Another big chain with a well-known name and its own logistics is good news for anyone shopping for a fridge or a washer. Whether it moves prices depends on which models it actually lists and how long the free delivery lasts.

## Before you order

Buying a fridge from a department store's website is still buying a fridge. A few things to check:

- **Who gives the warranty.** By law, new electrical products over ₪150 usually come with at least a one-year warranty, whether they're official or parallel imports. Anything beyond that, like a second year or a longer motor warranty, depends on who issues it.
- **Repair times.** Consumer protection regulations set one day to fix a fault that stops a fridge or freezer from working, and three days for a washing machine, dryer, dishwasher or air conditioner. These apply no matter where you bought it.
- **Installation and removal.** Free delivery doesn't necessarily mean installation or taking away the old appliance. Ask before you pay.
- **The exact model number.** Compare it, not the product name, across sites.
