Redmi announced on Weibo on Monday that the standard Redmi K100 will launch in China on October 27 at 7:00 pm local time. It is the third phone in the K100 line, after the K100 Pro and K100 Pro Max that went on sale in China in August.

## What Xiaomi has confirmed so far

- A flat 6.83-inch display with a refresh rate of up to 165Hz and a hole-punch selfie camera
- Peak brightness of 3,500 nits, according to Notebookcheck
- Native 165 fps in more than 40 games, according to Xiaomi
- Speakers tuned by Bose, with "Sound by Bose" printed on the camera module
- Two rear cameras with an LED flash in a wide horizontal module
- A flat metal frame
- HyperOS 4, which Gadgets 360 says is based on Android 17
- A green launch color, called Firefly Green in Notebookcheck's translation

## The chip

Notebookcheck writes that Xiaomi has confirmed the Snapdragon 8 Elite together with a separate D2 gaming chip. That is Qualcomm's flagship from 2024, not the newest one. Gadgets 360 still lists the chip as a report, together with 12GB of RAM, 256GB of storage and a built-in cooling fan, so treat those details as unconfirmed until launch day.

## Price and other markets

Xiaomi has not announced a price. Gadgets 360 cites a report of about CNY 3,500, which is not official. There is also no word on an international version. The K100 Pro and Pro Max were sold outside China as the Poco F9 Pro and Poco F9 Ultra, so a Poco version of this model is possible, but nothing has been announced.
