In most Israeli apartments, laundry lives on a cramped service balcony or in a hallway closet. That is why laundry towers, a washer and dryer stacked in a single unit, have done well here in recent years. LG is now adding a third size to its WashTower line: a 25-inch model that sits between the existing 24- and 27-inch versions. Per LG's October 15 announcement, the rollout starts in Asia, the Middle East and Latin America later this year, with other markets from 2027.

LG has not published a price yet, and there is no date for Israel. What we do have is the spec sheet, and it is worth a look.

![LG 25-inch WashTower annotated: heat-pump dryer on top, 6.8-inch control panel in the middle, washer below](img/posts/lg-washtower-annotated-en.webp "Two separate drums that run at the same time. Graphic: TechSite")

## 20 kg in each drum

Capacity is the headline. LG says both the washer and the dryer take loads of up to 20 kg each, and both can run simultaneously. You can dry the previous load while the next one is still washing. For a family running laundry almost daily, that cuts a lot of waiting.

The dryer uses DUAL Inverter HeatPump, a heat-pump system that draws less power than a conventional dryer. Turbo Dry shortens drying times, and TurboWash uses water jets and fast drum moves to cut wash time. A Prepare to Dry function coordinates the two units, and in Intertek testing commissioned by LG it finished washing and drying a 3 kg load within 120 minutes.

## What the AI actually does

AI Wash 2.0 relies on LG's direct-drive motor to read load weight, fabric type and soil level, then adjusts washing and rinsing. AI Dry looks at fabric type, load size and humidity changes to set drying time. LG notes it kicks in for loads under 5 kg and works best with similar fabrics, since not every material is detected.

AI Time Sensing learns from your usage to give more accurate cycle estimates, and after each cycle a short report shows estimated load size, detergent use and energy consumption. Everything runs from a 6.8-inch touchscreen in the middle of the tower, and you can follow progress in the ThinQ app.

## What Israel has today

Israeli stores currently sell LG's WT40 through official importer Brimag. It is a 60 cm wide tower with a 12 kg washer and a 10 kg heat-pump dryer. On Zap today (Nov 8) it lists from ₪8,427 (about $2,638) to over ₪9,000 depending on the shop.

The new model nearly doubles drying capacity and adds washing capacity, in exchange for a slightly wider body: 25 inches is about 63.5 cm. Measure before you get excited. Most built-in laundry niches here were planned for 60 cm, and three and a half centimeters can decide whether it fits at all.

## Bottom line

Until there is an Israeli price and an importer confirming it is coming, this is one to watch. If you need a laundry tower now, the WT40 is available with an official warranty. If you have the space, a big household and can wait a few months, the 25-inch model looks like a real step up in capacity.
