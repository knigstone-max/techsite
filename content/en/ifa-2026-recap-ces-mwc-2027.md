IFA 2026 closed in Berlin on 8 September. After going through the major makers' announcements, four things came up in almost every booth.

## Four trends

1. **"Better than A"**. The EU A rating is no longer enough. Samsung talks of a 10kg washer at A-70%, Haier has an I-Pro Shine A-70% dishwasher, and LG and Whirlpool show washers that beat the A threshold. We wrote a short guide to reading those numbers.
2. **Heat pumps everywhere**. Bosch and Electrolux put heat pumps in combo washer-dryers, and LG paired a heat-pump dryer with its new washer.
3. **Cameras inside appliances**. Haier put a camera in the washer drum and the oven, and LG put an LLM voice assistant in a fridge.
4. **Appliances for small homes**. 60cm depth, fridges that sit flush with cabinets, a new WashTower size and a 7.89cm-tall robot vacuum.

Dreame also showed a robot that cleans with 180°C steam, and robot vacuums in general keep competing on how much they can do without help.

## Next on the calendar

- **CES 2027**, Las Vegas: 6 to 9 January 2027. Mostly TVs, laptops and chip announcements.
- **MWC 2027**, Barcelona: 1 to 4 March 2027 at Fira Gran Via. Usually new phones from Chinese makers and carrier news.
- **IFA 2027**, Berlin: usually early September. We will update when dates are set.

## And New-Tech

New-Tech, the yearly meeting of Israel's electronics industry, was postponed this year because of the security situation and finally took place on 30 June and 1 July in Pavilion 1 at Expo Tel Aviv. It is not a consumer show, but if you follow components, embedded computing and RF, most Israeli companies in the field are there.
