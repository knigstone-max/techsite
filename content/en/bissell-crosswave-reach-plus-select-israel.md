Bissell picked Israel as the first market worldwide for the CrossWave Reach Plus Select. Model 4509N, a cordless vac-mop for hard floors, sells here for ₪1,490 (about $466) through official importer Hamilton Group, with a 24-month warranty. Reports from the launch say global rollout started about a month after Israel.

![Bissell CrossWave Reach Plus Select specs: ₪1,490, up to 40 minutes, 3.99 kg](img/posts/bissell-crosswave-specs-en.webp "The numbers in one place. Graphic: TechSite")

## Vacuum and wash in one pass

The CrossWave idea is familiar: instead of vacuuming and then mopping, the machine does both at once. This model adds ACTIVEFORCE, which Bissell says presses into the floor and cuts drying time in half, to about seven minutes. The brush spins at 400 rpm, with Normal, High and Turbo modes.

The clean-water tank holds 600 ml; the dirty tank holds 450 ml. That is enough for an average apartment in one run, but not a large house without a pause to empty.

## Lay-flat to 180° and clean to the wall

What sets the Reach Plus Select apart from earlier models is the hinge. You can lay the machine fully flat at 180 degrees, so it slides under beds, sofas and low furniture without moving them. Gap Zero pushes the brush out to the wall and baseboards, and a Tangle Free design is meant to keep hair and pet fur from wrapping around the roller.

![Bissell vac-mop annotated: Gap Zero, 180° lay-flat and ACTIVEFORCE](img/posts/bissell-crosswave-annotated-en.webp "Why Bissell is selling reach into corners. Graphic: TechSite")

## Battery, dock and upkeep

A 25.9V battery delivers up to 40 minutes on low, about 30 on high and about 20 in Turbo. A full charge takes roughly 4.5 hours. The dock self-cleans at the push of a button, then dries the brush with hot air. That matters: a wet brush left sealed inside the head is how CrossWaves start to smell.

In the box: the unit, a floor brush, two filters and a sample of cleaning solution. Weight is 3.99 kg; dimensions are about 111×27×22 cm.

## Where the price sits

₪1,490 puts it among mid-to-high cordless vac-mops in Israel, not against the expensive robots. If you live in a flat full of low furniture with parquet or porcelain floors, the full lay-flat and wall-hugging brush are the reasons to look. If you want one tool for the whole house including deep carpets, CrossWave is still mainly a hard-floor machine.
