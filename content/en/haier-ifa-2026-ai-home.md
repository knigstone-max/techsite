If Bosch brought ordinary appliances to Berlin, Haier brought the future, or its idea of it. At its stand in Hall 3.1, humanoid robots loaded laundry and put food in the fridge.

## A camera inside the washer

Haier Vision 15 washers have AI Vision Sense, an internal camera that sees load size, garment colours, foam level and even items forgotten or trapped in the drum. Nine more sensors measure weight, balance, water level and temperature and adjust the cycle.

## Four drums in one body

After last year's three-drum Candy MultiWash, Haier extends the idea with the Multi Care Collection. The flagship Multi Care Laundry Studio combines four drums, so whites, darks and baby clothes can be washed, and dried, at the same time.

## In the kitchen

- **Maestro**: a fridge prototype with AI Defrost Zone, which thaws food gently without losing juices
- **ID Ultimate Series 6**: an oven with a camera that recognises the food and stops when it is done
- **I-Pro Shine A-70%**: a dishwasher that reads load, soil and temperature and picks the cycle itself

## The money behind it

CEO Neil Tunstall said Haier will invest about €13 billion over five years in AI, innovation and IoT. That explains why almost every product had a camera or sensor.

## And in Israel?

Haier and Candy are sold in Israel, but most of what was shown is a prototype or a European model. The camera-equipped Vision washers are the likeliest to arrive first. The robots remain a demo for now.
