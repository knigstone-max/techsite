Yesterday we looked back at [the year in laundry](/techsite/en/posts/washers-dryers-2026-innovations-roundup/). Today it's the floor. 2026 was a busy year for wet-dry vacuums and robot vacuums, and in Israel, where most homes are tiled, this is probably the category that changed the most. We picked six directions that showed up across several brands, and for each one we checked what you can already buy here and what it costs.

![Six floor-cleaning changes of 2026: hotter steam, robots with legs, rollers, hygiene, edge cleaning and new players](img/posts/floor-cleaning-2026-trends-en.webp "The year in floor cleaning at a glance. Graphic: TechSite")

## 1. The steam race in wet-dry vacuums

This was the big one. At the start of the year, steam in a wet-dry vacuum was a rare extra. Now nearly every major brand has a steam model:

- **Tineco S9 Artist Steam Pro**: 140°C steam, ₪2,950 (about $899) on Tineco Israel's site
- **Xiaomi Wet Dry Vacuum 5 Pro**: 160°C, ₪2,490 (about $759) from Hamilton. [Read our review](/techsite/en/posts/xiaomi-wet-dry-vacuum-5-pro-review-israel/)
- **Roborock F25 Ultra Steam**: up to 180°C, ₪3,290 (about $1,002). [The details](/techsite/en/posts/roborock-f25-ultra-steam-israel/)
- **Dreame H16 Pro Steam**: a 200°C heater, ₪2,533 (about $772) on sale from the importer. [Our review](/techsite/en/posts/dreame-h16-pro-steam-review-israel/)
- **Tineco Floor One S9 Artist SuperSteam**: 230°C, launched in Vietnam in November and not in Israel yet. [The story](/techsite/en/posts/tineco-supersteam-3-230c-floor-washers/)

One thing to watch: these numbers don't always measure the same thing. With Dreame, for example, 200°C is the heater, and the steam reaches the floor at 99°C. Another thing they all share: steam lasts 20 to 35 minutes at most, so it's for the kitchen and the dirty spots, not the whole home.

![Steam temperatures claimed by wet-dry vacuum makers, from 140°C to 230°C, with Israeli prices](img/posts/floor-cleaning-2026-steam-race-en.webp "The steam race, per manufacturer figures. Graphic: TechSite")

## 2. Robots with legs and arms

The classic robot-vacuum problem in an Israeli home is the threshold: the step to the balcony, the shower or the safe room. This year robots started climbing:

- **Xiaomi Robot Vacuum 6 Max**: two mechanical legs that cross thresholds up to 6 cm, in a 9 cm tall body. ₪4,690 (about $1,429) in Israel. [Our review](/techsite/en/posts/xiaomi-robot-vacuum-6-max-review-israel/)
- **Roborock Saros 20 Flow Complete**: an arm that climbs thresholds up to 8.8 cm, in a body under 9 cm tall. ₪4,990 (about $1,520). [Our review](/techsite/en/posts/roborock-saros-20-flow-complete-review-israel/)

Arms came along with the legs: a side brush that reaches into corners, a mop that swings out around table legs and a roller that shifts toward the wall.

## 3. Rollers instead of spinning pads

Two round pads spinning under the robot were the standard for years. Their problem is well known: the pad gets dirty as it goes and spreads the dirt around. This year several flagship robots switched to a roller that's rinsed nonstop:

- **Roborock Saros 20**: a 270 mm roller fed by 16 water jets
- **ECOVACS DEEBOT X12S OmniCyclone**: a 27 cm roller with 32 rinse nozzles, plus a jet that softens dried stains before mopping. ₪4,999 (about $1,523). [The story](/techsite/en/posts/ecovacs-deebot-x12s-omnicyclone-israel/)
- **Xiaomi Robot Vacuum 6 Max**: a roller fed by 16 water outlets

On tiles, a roller that keeps getting clean water should leave the last metre as clean as the first. For Israeli homes, this may be the most meaningful change of the year.

## 4. Hygiene: boiling water, hot air and UV-C

The wet-rag smell used to be the top complaint about wet-dry vacuums and mopping robots. This year nearly every model above ₪2,000 comes with a dock that rinses with hot water and dries with hot air. The Dreame H16 Pro Steam rinses at 100°C and dries at 90°C, and Tineco's new steam models dry with 110°C air.

Anker went a step further. The **eufy Omni S2 Pro** adds six UV-C LEDs under the robot and electrolysed water. It costs $1,699 in the US (about ₪5,576) and hasn't been announced here. The regular Omni S2 sells in Israel for ₪4,699 (about $1,432). [The details](/techsite/en/posts/eufy-omni-s2-pro-uv-c-israel/)

## 5. Cleaning right up to the wall

The grey line along the baseboards is where you see whether a wet-dry vacuum is doing its job. This year brands went after it from several angles:

- **Bissell CrossWave Reach Plus Select**: Gap Zero edge cleaning, 180° lay-flat and hot-air brush drying. It reached Israel before the rest of the world, at ₪1,490 (about $454). [The story](/techsite/en/posts/bissell-crosswave-reach-plus-select-israel/)
- **Dreame H16 Pro Steam**: a front arm that reaches forward, plus edge cleaning on both sides of the brush
- **Tineco**: a head that's open on three sides in the new SuperSteam models

## 6. New players, and stick vacuums that empty themselves

**Dyson** moved into mopping robots this year with the R2 Nurovi Wash+Dry, sold in Italy for €849 (about ₪3,062 or $933), alongside the vacuum-only R1 Nurovi Dry at €549 (about ₪1,980 or $603). We haven't seen an Israeli launch announced. [The story](/techsite/en/posts/dyson-r1-r2-nurovi-robot-vacuums-italy/)

In regular cordless vacuums, self-emptying docks are spreading beyond Dyson. Xiaomi's **Mijia Vacuum Cleaner 4 Max**, launched in Europe this week at €549.99 (about ₪1,983 or $604), has a base that empties the bin in about 11 seconds into a 3-litre bag, and suction of up to 280AW. It hasn't been announced in Israel either.

## What to take from all this

- **If your home is mostly tiles and you cook a lot**, a wet-dry vacuum with a hot-air drying dock isn't a luxury anymore. Steam helps with grease and dried stains, but you don't need it.
- **If you're buying a robot**, look for a roller instead of spinning pads, and measure your thresholds before paying extra for legs.
- **Check the warranty**. Wet-dry vacuums have pumps, boilers and batteries. A three-year importer warranty, like Dreame's, is worth money.
