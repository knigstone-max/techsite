Garmin makes watches at every price point, but MARQ is where it takes on luxury watchmakers with titanium, sapphire and a price to match. The third generation was announced on October 13 and is already on sale in Israel through Ronlight Health, at Garmin stores and authorized dealers. Prices start at ₪8,999 (about $2,890). In the US it starts at $2,500 (about ₪7,785), so the Israeli markup is fairly modest for a luxury product.

## Six models on one platform

Every model shares the same hardware. They differ in styling, straps and a few specialist features. The new arrival this time is the Diver, built for dives down to 40 meters.

![The six MARQ Gen 3 models and what each one is for, with battery figures and the Israeli price below](img/posts/garmin-marq-gen3-models-en.webp "Which MARQ is built for what")

## Screen and build

- 1.4-inch AMOLED at 454×454 and up to 3,000 nits, readable even in August sun
- Grade 5 titanium case and sapphire lens
- 47×47×15 mm, 10ATM water rating and 22 mm QuickFit straps
- 62 g for the case alone, 86 g on silicone and 96 g on leather

## Inside

It has 128GB of storage and multi-band GPS. TopoActive maps for the whole world come preloaded, with address search. You also get NFC payments through Garmin Pay, a speaker and mic for calls, and a built-in LED flashlight.

The biggest change from the last generation is inReach. The watch can send messages over cellular or satellite, make LTE calls, share your location and send an SOS to Garmin Response. All of that needs an active subscription, and LTE isn't available everywhere. Before buying in Israel, ask the importer which of these services actually work here.

Health tracking includes an ECG app (availability varies by country), HRV, Pulse Ox and morning and evening reports.

## Battery

- Up to 18 days as a smartwatch, 7 with the always-on display
- Up to 24 days in battery saver
- 32 hours of continuous GPS, or 13 with music
- Up to 87 hours in Max Battery mode and 18 days in Expedition mode

## Who it's for

If you mostly want a great sports watch, a Garmin Fenix or Epix covers most of this for a fraction of the price. MARQ is for someone who wants one watch for the office and the trail, that looks right with a suit, and is willing to pay for materials and design. The new Diver and the inReach features are the main reasons to upgrade from Gen 2. Just make sure they work here first.
