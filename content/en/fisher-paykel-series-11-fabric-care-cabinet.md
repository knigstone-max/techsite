Steam cabinets that freshen clothes have been around for a few years, mostly from LG and Samsung. Now New Zealand's **Fisher & Paykel** is joining in with a whole laundry-room range called **Series 11 Minimal Fabric Care**. Everything comes in a grey glass finish, and you can install the pieces together or put each one somewhere else in the house.

## The cabinet: no drum, no movement

The interesting piece is the **care cabinet** (model FC1260FZB2). It's a tall, narrow unit, **190 cm high, 60 cm wide and 66 cm deep**, where you hang clothes on hangers or lay them on a shelf. There's no spinning drum, so nothing rubs.

It has four programmes:

- **Steam refresh**: relaxes creases and lifts odours without soaking the fabric
- **Deodorise**: mainly for synthetics like polyester and nylon
- **Sanitise**: a mix of heat and time. The company says independent testing found it removes 99.9% of Staphylococcus aureus and E. coli, in a test with five garments loaded
- **Dry**: with circulating warm air

The cabinet handles **16 fibre types**, including leather and cashmere, and holds **up to 12 garments**. It has a 7-inch touchscreen, five hangers with trouser clips and a shoe rack. Fisher & Paykel says it's the only appliance in its range that can care for leather.

## The washer and dryers

The **washer** (WH1260YZB5) takes **12 kg** and measures 85 × 60.2 × 67.6 cm. Its main feature is **ModDose**, which picks and dispenses the right detergent for the fabric. According to Australia's Appliance News, it knows 15 fibre types and adjusts for load size, soil level and colour. It also has a steam refresh programme.

Alongside it come **two heat-pump dryers** with moisture sensors that adapt drying to the fabric. One has no screen at all and is run from the washer. The whole range connects to the SmartHQ app.

## What it costs

In the UK:

- **Care cabinet**: £2,750 (about ₪11,991, about $3,605). You can order it now, with delivery **from April 1, 2027**
- **Washer**: £2,500 (about ₪10,901, about $3,278)

We couldn't find dryer prices.

## And in Israel?

Fisher & Paykel has an official importer here, but the Israeli site lists kitchen appliances only: fridges, dishwashers, hoods and ovens. Laundry isn't sold in Israel, and there's no sign the new range is coming. Even the SmartHQ app is marked "coming soon" on the Israeli site.

If you want a clothes-refreshing cabinet in Israel, the main option for now is LG's Styler, which has an official page on LG Israel's site. At around ₪12,000 for the cabinet alone, it's a niche product, but for a home with lots of suits, wool coats or clothes that usually go to the dry cleaner, the maths may be less crazy than it looks.

*Conversions at the Bank of Israel rate for Nov 20, 2026: ₪4.360 per pound and ₪3.326 per dollar.*
