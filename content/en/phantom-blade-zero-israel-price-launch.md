After years of jaw-dropping trailers, Phantom Blade Zero has finally arrived. The game from Chinese studio S-GAME launched on October 29 at 4:00 a.m. Israel time, worldwide and simultaneously, on PS5 and PC (Steam and Epic). The reviews are strong: 88 on both Metacritic and OpenCritic, and 9/10 from both IGN and GamesRadar+.

## What it is

It's a fast third-person action game that the studio calls "kungfupunk," a world of Chinese wuxia legend mixed with dark machinery. You play Soul, an assassin framed for his master's murder. He has 66 days left to live and needs to find out who set him up.

![Soul faces a giant boss erupting in flames on a dusty, scorched battlefield](img/posts/phantom-blade-zero-boss-fire.webp "Boss fights are the heart of the game. Image: S-GAME")

Combat is what everyone is talking about. There are more than 30 primary weapons and over 20 Phantom Edges, secondary tools with special moves, so you can put together a fairly personal fighting style. It runs on Unreal Engine 5, and the screenshots show it, with rain, fire and dust that look great.

## Israeli prices

![Phantom Blade Zero price table for PS Store Israel, Steam and the US, plus install size, languages and physical release timing](img/posts/phantom-blade-zero-prices-en.webp "Prices on the Israeli digital stores")

- **PS Store Israel**: ₪149.96 (about $48) for the standard edition. The Digital Deluxe Edition is ₪179.96 (about $58).
- **Steam**: ₪149.95 (about $48), plus ₪30 (about $9.60) to upgrade to Deluxe.
- **For comparison**: it's $59.99 (about ₪187) in the US, so for once the Israeli digital price is lower.

What does Deluxe add? Two extra outfits (Wulin Legend and Blood-Steel Form), a digital artbook and the soundtrack. None of it changes the game, so unless you collect this stuff, standard is enough. Pre-order buyers also got Treasure Basin and the Legacy outfit.

If you're waiting for a boxed PS5 copy, you'll need patience: physical editions arrive in 2027, in three versions.

## Before you buy

- **No Hebrew.** Voices are in Chinese or English, and subtitles and menus don't include Hebrew. Basic English is enough to enjoy it.
- **Make room.** It takes about 124.5GB on PS5, and PC needs 145GB free on an SSD.
- **PS5 Pro.** The game is PS5 Pro Enhanced.

![Soul fights in the rain on wooden boards, with a bluish trail of energy swirling around him](img/posts/phantom-blade-zero-rain-combat.webp "The game makes great use of Unreal Engine 5. Image: S-GAME")

## PC requirements

**Minimum** (1080p at 30 fps, with upscaling):

- Intel i7-8700K or Ryzen 5 3600
- 16GB RAM
- GTX 1660 6GB or RX 5500 XT

**Recommended** (1440p at 60 fps):

- Intel i5-10600K or Ryzen 5 5600X
- RTX 3060 Ti or RX 6700 XT

If you enjoyed Sekiro or Black Myth: Wukong, this is probably your winter game. At around ₪150, it's one of the year's big launches and it costs less than usual here.
