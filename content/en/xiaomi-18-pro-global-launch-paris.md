Xiaomi's phone with a screen on its back is heading out of China. Company president Lu Weibing says the Xiaomi 18 Pro series will launch globally on November 17 at an event in Paris, starting at 2pm Central European Time, which is 3pm in Israel. There are two models, the 18 Pro and the 18 Pro Max, and both have been on sale in China since September 23.

What sets them apart from everything else on the shelf is a small AMOLED panel built into the camera module on the back: 2.7 inches on the Pro and 2.9 inches on the Pro Max.

![Xiaomi 18 Pro Max annotated: two 200MP cameras, a 2.9-inch rear screen and an 8,500mAh battery](img/posts/xiaomi-18-pro-max-annotated-en.webp "What's on the back of the bigger model. Graphic: TechSite")

## Why put a screen on the back

The most practical use is selfies. Flip the phone around and shoot yourself with the main camera, using the rear screen as a viewfinder, instead of settling for the front camera. It also shows notifications, timers and small widgets, so you can leave the phone face down on the table and still see what's going on.

Xiaomi has tried something like this before, but this time the screen lives inside the camera module instead of taking over half the back.

## What else is inside

Going by the Chinese models:

- **Main screens:** 6.4 inches on the Pro and 6.9 inches on the Pro Max, both 120Hz with up to 4,000 nits, plus a privacy mode that makes the screen hard to read from the side.
- **Chips:** Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 in the Pro, Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 in the Pro Max, both on a 2nm process.
- **Cameras:** a 200MP main camera with OIS, a 200MP periscope telephoto with OIS and a 50MP ultrawide. Xiaomi's global teaser leans on the two 200MP Leica cameras.
- **Batteries:** 7,000mAh in the Pro and 8,500mAh in the Pro Max, with 100W wired and 50W wireless charging.
- **Software:** HyperOS 4 on Android 17.

Global versions don't always match the Chinese ones. Sometimes the battery shrinks, and sometimes there are fewer memory options. We'll find out on the 17th.

![Xiaomi 18 Pro vs 18 Pro Max: screens, chips, batteries and leaked European prices](img/posts/xiaomi-18-pro-series-specs-en.webp "The two models side by side. Graphic: TechSite")

## What it might cost

Xiaomi hasn't announced prices outside China. In October, an Italian retailer called Epto posted European prices early: €1,549 (about ₪5,498 or $1,734) for a 256GB 18 Pro, and €1,974 (about ₪7,006 or $2,209) for a 512GB Pro Max. These aren't official and could change.

What is confirmed: Japan and South Korea are getting the Pro Max, Japan already has pre-registration open, and Xiaomi's regional accounts in Europe and Asia, the UK included, have told customers to expect the series this month.

## What about Israel

Hamilton, Xiaomi's official importer here, hasn't said anything about the series yet. If the leaked European prices hold, the smaller model would go head to head with the Galaxy S26 Ultra and the iPhone 18 Pro, and the bigger one would sit above them. We'll update once there's an official Israeli price.
