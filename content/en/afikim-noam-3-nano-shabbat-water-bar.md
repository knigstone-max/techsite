Israel's water bar market keeps getting busier. After Electra moved in with a cheap model, Jerusalem-based Afikim Water has started delivering the **Noam 3 Nano**, the newest version of its Shabbat-friendly water bars. The company says its first batch sold out before the units even landed. Now they're here.

The target buyer is obvious: families who want hot and cold water all week, Shabbat included, without a separate urn on the counter.

## What's inside

"Nano" is about size. The unit is 36.9 cm tall, 30.2 cm wide and 37.7 cm deep, so it should fit under most upper kitchen cabinets.

According to Afikim's specs:

- **Hot water** at 80 to 95°C from a 6.38-litre tank.
- **Cold water** at 2 to 13°C from a 1.36-litre tank, plus room-temperature water.
- **A touchscreen** with measured pours, temperature settings, a child lock and four interface languages.
- **Ambient lighting** in 18 colors and two energy-saving modes.
- Four colors: velvet black, pearl white, stone and graphite grey.

## Shabbat mode

This is the main event. The unit switches into Shabbat mode **automatically** based on your location, so nobody has to remember on Friday afternoon. You can also turn it on manually. The screen shows halachic times, and in Shabbat mode the machine runs continuously without responding to what users do.

Afikim says the design is patented, approved by the Technological Institute for Halacha and certified by the Badatz Mehadrin of Rabbi Rubin. A hot tank of more than six litres should cover a family's hot drinks over Shabbat, and that's really the pitch: you can retire the urn.

## Price

Afikim sells the unit over 36 payments, in three plans:

- **Basic:** ₪109 a month, ₪3,924 in total (about $1,210).
- **Extended:** ₪149 a month, ₪5,364 in total (about $1,650).
- **Complete:** ₪189 a month, ₪6,804 in total (about $2,100).

The plans differ in the service package that comes with them. The product page mentions a one-year manufacturer warranty, a six-month filter, delivery and installation, and ongoing service. It's worth going through exactly what each plan includes before you pick one.

For comparison, Afikim's own Noam 1 starts at ₪69 a month and the Noam 2 at ₪79. So you're paying extra for the smaller body, the touchscreen and the automatic Shabbat switch.

## What to check before ordering

- **What the plan covers.** How many filters, for how long, and what happens after the first year of warranty. The gap between Basic and Complete is almost ₪2,900.
- **Installation.** A water bar connects to your plumbing. Make sure there's a water point and an outlet near the spot you have in mind.
- **Power use.** A hot tank running all week and through Shabbat uses electricity. Afikim lists two energy-saving modes but hasn't published consumption figures, so ask.
- **The total.** ₪109 a month sounds small, but it's a three-year commitment. Compare total prices, not monthly ones.

For a Shabbat-observant family that currently keeps both a water bar and an urn, this could free up counter space and end the Friday refill routine. If you don't need Shabbat mode, there are simpler and cheaper water bars around, including from Afikim itself.
