Guns feel different in GTA VI than they did in GTA V. There's no giant wheel with the whole arsenal in your pocket anymore. You carry two sidearms and two long guns, and the rest stays in a weapon locker at any Ammu-Nation or in the trunk of your own car. A gun you pick up from an enemy isn't really yours: die, get arrested or drop it, and it's gone. A gun you bought stays yours, and if you lose it, the locker gives it back.

The game has 66 standard weapons: 53 guns, 9 melee weapons and 4 explosives. Right now 64 of them have known names. You'll find all of them here, with the store price and what it takes to unlock each one. Prices are in in-game dollars, from Dexerto and PowerPyx launch-week guides as collected by Leonipedia. We left out damage and accuracy figures, because only one outlet has published them so far.

## Where to buy

There are 24 Ammu-Nation stores on the map, marked with an orange gun icon. Hover over one to see its type:

| Store type | How many | What's inside |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 15 | Most of the basic guns |
| Expanded | 6 | The widest choice, launchers included after the story: Big Shots, Black Eagle, Iron & Oak, Phil's Ammu-Nation, Prestige Firearms and Woodhatch |
| Hunting | 2 | Hunting stores |
| Limited | 1 | Chuck's Tackle & Shot in Key Lento, near Jason's first home |

Not every gun is on sale from the start. Some need a bank account, some unlock only after you've bought other guns from the same store, one rifle needs a gold medal at every shooting range, and the heavy weapons open only after the story ends.

## Free guns

- **Two shipwrecks:** early on, on the small islands south-west of Jason's first home, a suppressed pistol and a shotgun are waiting. Pick them up and they're yours for good.
- **Police pickups:** the bed crate on a police Bison has no alarm. Climb up, open it, and you'll find guns, ammo and sometimes a stun gun.
- **Cruisers:** a police car's trunk is alarmed. With the slim jim, which you get in a Moses mission in Chapter 2, you get in and open it quietly.
- **Unique variants:** the 39 gang operations hide special versions of guns, like the Kingfishers Laird 350 with a red dot sight, or the Gutterball Moreland 850 at the Kingpin Bowling alley in Port Gellhorn.

## What the Ultimate Edition adds

![The two Morgan revolvers, labelled: high-performance scope, the J. Duval and L. Caminos engravings, and the palm-tree grips](img/posts/gta6-weap-morgan-annotated-en.webp "Two Morgans: one for Jason, one for Lucia. Image: Rockstar Games. Labels: TechSite")

Rockstar describes the Hawk & Little Morgan pair as "his and hers": a powerful revolver with classic Vice City styling sourced from the Vercetti Estate, with palm-tree-etched grips, engraving and a high-performance scope. You also get engraved versions of Jason's Girardi ES9 and Lucia's Klose K17.

| Chapter | Weapon | Where to get it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jason's engraved Girardi ES9 (Duval Signature) | Weapon locker, Variants section, free |
| 2 | Lucia's engraved Klose K17 (Caminos Signature) | Same locker |
| 2 | The Morgan pair, one per character | Weapon locker, or your car's trunk |

![An engraved Girardi ES9 and an engraved Klose K17 lying on the edge of a pool](img/posts/gta6-weap-signature-pistols.webp "Jason's and Lucia's engraved pistols. Screenshot: Rockstar Games")

Pre-order buyers also get a weapon pattern based on Tommy Vercetti's palm-tree shirt. It unlocks in Chapter 3, after you collect the '55 Vapid Stanier, in the customization menu at any Ammu-Nation.

![A submachine gun with a palm-tree pattern lying on a car's back seat next to a bag](img/posts/gta6-weap-palm-pattern.webp "The palm-tree pattern from the pre-order pack. Screenshot: Rockstar Games")

## Cheats, trophies and 100%

- **Cheats:** weapon cheats open only after the story. Blow up 100 vehicles, for example, and you get a cheat for the H-AT launcher. While a cheat is on you can't save and trophies are disabled.
- **Shooting ranges:** 25 medals unlock the ammo cheats, and gold at every range unlocks the M306 TMR.
- **100%:** you need to hold 50 of the 66 weapons at least once. A gun picked up from an enemy counts.
