Beko may be the biggest white-goods company in Europe that most Israelis do not realise they already own. The group owns or licenses 22 brands, including Beko, Grundig, Whirlpool, Hotpoint, Indesit, Blomberg and Bauknecht, and turned over €10.7 billion in 2025. At IFA it chose to talk in numbers.

## 40 minutes for a full load

The Ultimate40' programme washes a full load in 40 minutes. Beko says an improved drum paddle and a new algorithm dissolve detergent faster and spread water evenly, saving up to 35% in time and energy on daily programmes.

## 59 minutes to a clean oven

PyroPro heats the oven to 420°C and burns grease down to fine ash. A full clean takes 59 minutes and uses up to 47% less energy than a standard pyrolytic cycle.

## 30% fresher

AeroFlow keeps the temperature even across the fridge, and Beko says food stays fresh 30% longer. There is also a new range of 91cm fridges over 600 litres, four-door or side-by-side, with an ice and water dispenser making up to 2kg of ice a day.

## From the other brands

- **Whirlpool**: a 9kg washer 60% more efficient than the A requirement, and matte glass hobs with 3x the scratch resistance
- **Hotpoint**: a FabricCare dryer that is 2x gentler, and an oven with a 3-minute pizza function

## For Israel

Beko, Whirlpool and Hotpoint appliances are sold in the big Israeli chains. When you see Ultimate40 or AeroFlow on a shelf tag, you are looking at the 2026 generation.
