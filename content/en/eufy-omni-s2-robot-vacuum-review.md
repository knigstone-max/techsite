Robot vacuums in Israel have split into two groups. There are the small, cheap ones that sweep up crumbs and dog hair, and there are the big ones with a station that cleans the mop, empties the bin and sometimes looks like a piece of furniture. The eufy Omni S2 belongs firmly in the second group. It's eufy's top model, and the way it mops is quite different from most of the competition.

In early November I found it at Touch Store for 4,499 ₪ (about $1,425), down from 4,699 ₪ (about $1,488), and at Big Electric for 4,599 ₪ (about $1,457). In the US it lists at $1,599.99 (about ₪5,051), so for once the Israeli price isn't higher.

![The eufy Omni S2 station with the robot docked, with callouts for the station's 38.7×47.6×67 cm size, its 12 automatic tasks and the robot's 30,000Pa of suction](img/posts/eufy-omni-s2-annotated-en.webp "The robot and its station. Image: eufy / Graphic: TechSite")

## The mop is the main story

Most premium robots mop with two spinning pads. The S2 uses a different approach eufy calls HydroJet 2.0: a 29 cm roller that spins at 240 rpm and presses on the floor with 1.5 kg of force. Thirty-two small outlets keep feeding it clean water while the robot works, so in theory it isn't spreading dirty water around the house. eufy also uses electrolyzed water, which it says helps break down dirt.

For Israeli homes with tiled floors, this is the right kind of mop. Tile grout traps dirt, and a roller that keeps getting fresh water and presses down hard should handle it better than a pad that dries out by the end of the room. The mop also extends 1.5 cm sideways and gets to within 5 mm of the wall, and eufy has a feature called CornerRover for corners. Edges are where most robots leave a grey line, so this matters.

When the robot reaches a rug, it lifts the mop by 15 mm. That's fine for thin rugs. For a thick shag rug, it's better to mark it in the app as a no-mop zone.

## Suction and carpets

On paper, the suction is very strong: 30,000Pa, which eufy also calls 100AW. The AeroTurbo 2.0 system is supposed to handle carpets with pile up to 5 cm deep. The DuoSpiral brushes are designed so long hair doesn't wrap around them, which is good news if you have long-haired family members or a dog that sheds.

The robot can climb a 3.5 cm single threshold and a 4.2 cm double one. Most Israeli flats have low thresholds between rooms, but some older ones have higher steps at the balcony door, so it's worth measuring.

## The station does almost everything

The station handles 12 tasks. It washes the mop with water heated to 60°C and dries it with 55°C air, which helps avoid the musty smell that wet mops pick up in a humid Israeli summer. It empties the bin into a 3-litre dust bag, which eufy says lasts up to 68 days. It adds detergent automatically, runs ozone sterilisation that eufy says kills 99.99% of germs for up to 5 days, and has a removable base for cleaning.

Then there's the feature you either love or ignore: a scent diffuser with 3 fragrances, each capsule lasting up to 120 hours. It's a gimmick, but a pleasant one. Refill capsules cost 120 ₪ (about $38).

The tanks hold 2.5 litres of clean water and 1.8 litres of dirty water. eufy also lists automatic refilling and draining. That needs a connection to the water supply and a spot near a water point, so ask the store what's included before you count on it.

The station is big: 38.7×47.6×67 cm. The robot itself is 32.6×35.8×9.8 cm, which is a bit tall, so check the gap under your sofa and beds.

## Navigation and app

The S2 uses LiDAR, two RGB cameras and infrared, which eufy calls 3D Matrix Eye 2.0, along with an AI system it calls CleanMind. It's meant to recognise more than 200 types of objects and avoid things as small as about 1 cm, like socks and cables. It can also detect stains and go back over them. The app stores up to 5 maps, which is enough for a two-storey house.

The battery is 4,600mAh, for up to 2.5 hours of cleaning, and takes 4 hours to charge. It works with Alexa, Siri, Google Assistant, Apple Watch and Matter. One thing to note: Wi-Fi is 2.4GHz only, so if your router broadcasts only on 5GHz, you'll need to change a setting.

## Warranty and running costs

The two stores state different warranty terms. Touch Store lists 24 months on the device and 12 months on the battery, while Big Electric lists one year. Make sure you know what you're getting before you pay. An accessories kit with spare side brushes, filters, a dust bag and a new roller mop costs 499 ₪ (about $158).

## Who should consider the cheaper models

eufy sells several Omni models in Israel: the E20 Omni for 1,999 ₪, the E25 Omni for 2,899 ₪ and the E28 Omni for 3,237 ₪. If your home is small and you don't need hot-water washing, a scent diffuser or automatic refilling, one of those could cover what you need for a lot less.

## The bottom line

8/10

The eufy Omni S2 is a very well-equipped robot. The roller mop with constant clean water is a good fit for Israeli tiled floors, and the station takes care of nearly everything on its own, including drying with hot air. It loses points for the large station, the 2.4GHz-only Wi-Fi and the price. If you have a large home with lots of tiles and want to forget about mopping, it's worth a look. For a small flat, one of eufy's cheaper Omni models will do.
:::

**What I liked**

- Roller mop with a constant supply of clean water
- Hot-water mop washing and hot-air drying
- Very strong suction, with anti-tangle brushes
- Mop that extends to the edges of the room
- Up to 68 days between emptying the dust bag
- Israeli price lower than the US list price

**What I didn't**

- Large station that needs space
- Wi-Fi 2.4GHz only
- Warranty terms differ between stores
- The robot is a bit tall for low furniture
- Expensive compared to eufy's other Omni models
