Anker is trying again to convince Israelis to spend close to ₪800 on earbuds without a Samsung or Apple logo. This time it is the Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro, at a recommended ₪799 (about $250) through official importer Hamilton, with a 24-month warranty. There is also a Pro Max at ₪999 with a larger case screen and meeting recording. I focused on the base model, the one most people will actually buy.

I did not live with them in a lab for a month. I went through Anker's and Hamilton's official specs, what is sold here, and how the package sits against what already fills the shelves. Short answer: if you want strong ANC, long battery and a case screen without paying Apple flagship money, these are serious contenders. If ecosystem lock-in and service in every mall matter more, think twice.

![Liberty 5 Pro specs: ₪799, up to 12/50 hours of battery, Bluetooth 6.1 and IP55](img/posts/liberty-5-pro-specs-en.webp "The numbers in one place. Graphic: TechSite")

## Design and a case with a screen

The buds themselves are bean-shaped, no long stem, with the soundcore "d" on the outer face. They come in four colors: black, white, blue and pink. The case is the visual story: a pebble shape with a 0.96-inch TFT touchscreen on the front. From there you can switch ANC modes, toggle Dolby Audio, pick sound effects and pair Bluetooth without unlocking the phone.

That is handy on a bus or at the gym when the phone is in a bag. On the flip side, the small screen needs a precise finger, and in a crowded pocket it can take accidental taps if you do not lock it.

![Liberty 5 Pro case annotated: 0.96-inch screen, 10 call sensors and Anker Thus chip](img/posts/liberty-5-pro-annotated-en.webp "The case is part of the product, not just a box. Graphic: TechSite")

## Sound and noise cancelling

At the core sits Anker Thus, an AI chip that processes audio in a CPU-in-Memory design. Adaptive ANC 4.0 samples noise at a high rate and reshapes the curve in real time. Anker says it goes up to twice as deep as the Liberty 4 Pro. Day to day, that means cancellation that can hang with pricier buds on road noise, AC hum and nearby chatter. Sharp, sudden noises still get through, as they do on most TWS sets.

The default sound leans on emphasized bass, with loud volume before it falls apart. HearID 5.0 in the app builds a personal hearing profile, and LDAC is there for Android phones that support it. If you want a flat, precise stage you will need to tune the EQ. If you want gym and commute punch, you will feel at home quickly.

## Calls, connection and battery

This is where Anker pushes hard: ten sensors, eight microphones plus two bone-conduction sensors, meant to isolate your voice on a noisy street. The company cites a Guinness World Records mark for TWS call quality in an objective test. In practice, two-way calls are one of the better reasons to pick these if you live on conference audio.

Bluetooth 6.1 with multipoint for three devices at once is a real edge over models still stuck at two. There is Apple Find My and Google Fast Pair support. Durability is IP55: rain and sweat are fine, swimming is not.

Battery: up to 12 hours in the buds and 50 with the case with ANC off, or 6.5 and 28 with ANC on. With all AI features running, those drop to about 4 and 17 hours. A five-minute quick charge adds about four hours of use, and wireless charging is supported.

## Price and service in Israel

₪799 puts the Liberty 5 Pro against higher-end Galaxy Buds and against AirPods, not against ₪200 no-name buds. Store prices sometimes dip under the RRP, but even at ₪799 you get an official importer and two years from Hamilton. The Pro Max at ₪999 adds a 1.78-inch AMOLED case screen, up to 12 hours of meeting recording and in-app transcription. For most people the regular model is enough.

## Bottom line

8/10

The Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro bring a package usually reserved for flagships to an ₪800 spend: sharp adaptive ANC, strong calls, long battery and a useful case screen. They lose points for bass-heavy defaults, IP55-only toughness and a brand that still trails Apple and Samsung for walk-in service. If you want performance without the logo tax, this is a strong buy.
:::

**What I liked**

- Adaptive ANC 4.0 that handles everyday Israeli noise
- Call quality with ten sensors
- A case screen that saves unlocking the phone
- Up to 50 hours of battery with the case
- Bluetooth 6.1 with three-device multipoint
- Official import and 24-month Hamilton warranty

**What I liked less**

- Bass-forward tuning that needs EQ for accuracy fans
- IP55 only, not for washing or long heavy rain
- The small case screen can get pocket taps
- Some AI features are weaker in Hebrew
- At ₪799 you still compete with brands that have service on every street
