Apple took its time with this one. Samsung, Google and OPPO are several generations into foldables, and only now does iPhone Duo show up. My question wasn't whether Apple can build a foldable. It was whether there's a reason to pay over ₪8,000 for one here. I went through everything Apple published, the prices at the Israeli chains, and what it all means if you use your phone in Hebrew. Here's where I landed.

## Two screens, one shape

The smartest idea here, as far as I'm concerned, is that both screens share the same aspect ratio. The outer one is 5.4 inches, which Apple says gives you 90% of iPhone 18 Pro's screen area. The inner one is 7.6 inches, 50% bigger than the 18 Pro Max. Open it and the app just gets bigger instead of jumping to a different layout, which is what happens on a lot of foldables. Sounds minor, but it's the difference between a phone that turns into a small tablet and a phone that simply gets bigger.

![Two iPhone Duo phones in hand, one closed and one open, with labels for the outer display, inner display, front camera and shared aspect ratio](img/posts/iphone-duo-screens-annotated-en.webp "Closed and open: 5.4 inches outside, 7.6 inside, same shape")

The inner screen has a matte nano-texture finish that Apple says cuts reflections and makes the crease less visible. Both screens hit 3,000 nits outdoors and support ProMotion and Always On, which matters under the Israeli sun. The inner FaceTime camera sits under the display, so there's no hole in the middle of the picture.

## Durability, the part I actually care about

A foldable is a long-term buy, so the first thing I looked at was what Apple did to make it last. The frame is grade 5 titanium, the hinge has more than 100 parts, and it's rated IP68 (up to 6 meters for 30 minutes). What a spec sheet can't tell me is how the crease will look after a year of opening and closing, and that's the real test.

## Performance and battery

Inside is the A20 Pro, the same chip as iPhone 18 Pro, with a vapor chamber for cooling. Apple promises up to 35% better sustained performance than the 17 Pro. The battery setup is what caught my eye: two batteries, one on each side, and Apple quotes up to 24 hours of use when you split time evenly between the screens. Wired charging gets you to 50% in about 20 minutes, but only with a 60W or higher charger, which isn't in the box.

## Cameras

A 48MP main camera with an optical-quality 2x zoom, and a 48MP ultra wide, the same one as on the 18 Pro. There's no real telephoto, and that's the clearest trade-off against the Pro. On the other hand, the folding design does things a regular iPhone can't. You can take selfies with the rear cameras while watching yourself on the outer screen, show people the frame in real time, and prop the phone half open without a tripod.

## Two caveats for Israeli buyers

**Hebrew.** Siri AI and Apple Intelligence don't support Hebrew. Set the phone to Hebrew and you get an excellent device, but not the AI package Apple builds around it, and some of what makes the big screen useful leans on exactly that.

**eSIM only.** There's no SIM tray, anywhere in the world. That's a non-issue with the big Israeli carriers, but if you're on a smaller one or have a work line, check before you buy.

## What it costs here

Pelephone, Partner and KSP all list the same prices, VAT included:

- **256GB:** ₪8,199 (about $2,621)
- **512GB:** ₪8,799 (about $2,813)
- **1TB:** ₪9,999 (about $3,197)
- **2TB:** ₪12,199 (about $3,900)

In the US the base model is $1,999 before tax, about ₪6,253. VAT explains part of the gap, but it's still close to ₪2,000 more. Apple's case is $79 in the US (about ₪247), and the Folio with a kickstand is $129 (about ₪404).

## The bottom line

8/10

On paper, iPhone Duo is the most thought-through foldable I've seen: two screens with the same shape, serious durability and a battery that doesn't compromise. But in Israel it's very expensive, and the AI Apple built around it doesn't speak Hebrew. If you live in Apple's ecosystem and have been waiting for a foldable, this is it. If price matters to you, there's no rush.
:::

**What I loved**

- Both screens share an aspect ratio, so apps just get bigger when you open it
- Titanium, a 100-plus-part hinge and IP68
- Two batteries and up to 24 hours of use, per Apple
- Front camera under the inner screen, no hole in the middle
- 3,000 nits outdoors, exactly what an Israeli summer calls for

**What I didn't**

- ₪8,199 for the base model, well above the US price
- No Hebrew support for Siri AI or Apple Intelligence
- No real telephoto, just 2x from the main sensor
- eSIM only, and the fast charger is sold separately
