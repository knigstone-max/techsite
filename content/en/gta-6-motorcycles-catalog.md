A bike in GTA VI is more than a quick way through Vice City traffic. It squeezes into alleys, hops onto sidewalks, flies over the dunes of Mount Kalaga, and in some missions it's simply what the game hands you. Ambrosia's biker clubs are part of the story, and the trailers already showed Jason riding at night and a dirt bike sailing over rooftops.

The game counts 29 models in its bike category, but that includes bicycles, kick scooters and the like. This catalog covers the motorbikes first, then the small off-roaders and the bicycles. As with the [vehicle catalog](/techsite/en/posts/gta-6-vehicles-catalog/), a name only gets in if it was seen in the game and published in post-launch guides.

### Which one is fastest?

There's no answer that several sources agree on yet. DamnModz published in-game speed ratings that put the Nagasaki SFB 1000 and the Principe Alvino V1 on top, with the Carbon RS just behind. We haven't found a second source to back the numbers, so we're not printing them here.

## ATVs, buggies and bicycles

It's not just motorbikes. There are small machines for the trails and the beach too:

- **Nagasaki Street Blazer** (ATV): in the Chapter 2 mission Ambulance Chaser, from a barn near Frogswater.
- **Dinka Verus** (ATV): one sits locked inside the ranger outpost in south-west Mount Kalaga National Park.
- **Mer-Can Blast** (side-by-side): an unlocked one waits under the awning at the corn maze in eastern Capri.
- **Blazer** and **Blazer Lifeguard** (an ATV and its lifeguard version).

And bicycles, for a slow ride along the beach: the Walter Lombike, Vice City's bike-share you rent from a dock, the Walter Cruiser, Del Perro Scorcher, Del Perro Erode, Endurex Aerobike and Tuff BMX (a cheat after five fitness challenges). There's even an electric kick scooter, the Shift-E FAN-E, the slowest thing in the game.

## Quick tips

- **A bike is a gun platform too:** while riding you can only fire pistols and one-handed SMGs. Details in the [weapons catalog](/techsite/en/posts/gta-6-weapons-catalog/).
- **Scan everything that moves:** every bike you scan with WAiNK counts toward the 200 models for 100%, bicycles and scooters included.
- **Dirt bikes up north:** Mount Kalaga and the Grassrivers swamps are Manchez and Sanchez country. A sport bike just gets stuck there.

## What we don't know yet

- Some of the 29 models still have no published name.
- A police Western Sovereign 107 has been reported but not confirmed by two sources.
- There are dozens of Standout bikes, like the Tropical Manchez and Cultist Nightblade, parked at fixed spots. Most were reported by a single source, so we didn't list them.
