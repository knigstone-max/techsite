Samsung Italy announced on October 22 that its Smart Repair service, already offered for Galaxy phones, now covers home appliances. It's meant for products that are no longer under warranty.

## How it works

Samsung describes three steps:

1. You go to the Smart Repair portal on Samsung.com, pick your appliance and pay for an out-of-warranty repair package.
2. Samsung takes the request and assigns it to one of its Authorized Service Centers.
3. A technician comes to your home, checks the appliance and repairs it when that's technically possible.

The visit is normally handled within five working days of payment. If the product turns out to be beyond repair, Samsung says the customer isn't charged.

## Which appliances

The service covers several Samsung categories, including washing machines, dryers, refrigerators, dishwashers, ovens, cooktops and the Air Dresser clothing care cabinet.

## Why it matters

Paying up front online for a fixed repair package removes some of the back-and-forth of finding a technician after the warranty ends. Samsung's announcement covers Italy only and doesn't list package prices. If you own a Samsung appliance elsewhere, check your local Samsung site for a similar option.
