In Israel, Dreame is mostly known for robot vacuums and cordless stick vacs. Now it wants a spot in the laundry room. Crystalino, its official importer, has launched the L9 WashTower AI Set, a washer and dryer pair sold together that can be stacked or placed side by side. The ad campaign, fronted by Naama Kasry, started on October 12.

## What's in the box

- **An 11 kg washer** with a Twin Inverter motor and spin speeds up to 1,400 rpm
- **A 9 kg heat-pump dryer** with AI Dry sensors that stop the cycle once clothes are dry
- **Double auto-dosing** for detergent and softener, so you fill up once and forget about it for a while
- **PressFree Steam**, which is meant to cut down on wrinkles
- **30-minute quick programs**, plus AI programs that pick settings based on the load
- **A round TFT screen and a rotary knob** on each machine, and Wi-Fi with an app
- The stacking kit is included

The washer measures 60×63×85 cm and the dryer 60×69.5×85 cm. Stacked, that's roughly 1.70 m of height. Side by side, you need 1.20 m of width.

![The energy labels for the Dreame washer and dryer, with each figure explained](img/posts/dreame-l9-energy-labels-en.webp "What the energy labels on both machines say")

## Reading the labels

The washer is rated class A, the top of the scale. It uses 32 kWh per 100 cycles on the eco program and 54 litres of water per wash, and that program takes 4 hours. That's normal for eco cycles, which save power and water by running slow. If you're in a hurry, the 30-minute program gets it done, but you lose some of the savings.

The dryer is class B, at 92 kWh per 100 cycles with 88% condensation efficiency. It runs at 68 dB (class C), and the washer reaches 76 dB on spin (class B). Worth keeping in mind if your laundry corner shares a wall with a bedroom.

## Price and launch offer

The full set costs ₪8,990 (about $2,874). You can buy it from Dreame Israel's site, Shekem Electric (delivery there is ₪300), Bezeq Store and KSP.

![Price table for the Dreame L9 WashTower AI set and the launch bundle value, in shekels and dollars](img/posts/dreame-l9-price-deal-en.webp "Price and launch offer")

Through November 30, 2026, buyers get a Dreame E3 compact steam iron, the stacking kit and a warranty extended to 5 years (the standard 3 plus 2 more). Shekem and Bezeq Store value the bundle at ₪1,200 (about $384). Dreame and Shekem also list a 20-year warranty on the motor. Service runs through Crystalino at *8959.

## Who it's for

On paper the package looks good. Dreame packs in an 11 kg drum, a heat-pump dryer and auto-dosing without making you go up to a flagship model. The open question is reliability. This is Dreame's first laundry product in Israel, so there's no track record yet for how the machines hold up or how service handles repairs. If you're willing to take a chance on a new name, you get a lot of hardware for the price, and the launch bundle through November sweetens it a bit.
