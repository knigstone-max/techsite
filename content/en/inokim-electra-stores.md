Few Israeli electronics brands sell in thirty countries. INOKIM is one of them, yet in Israel most of its sales have been through its own website. That is about to change.

## The deal

According to ice and ynet (27.9.2026), INOKIM scooters will go into about 200 Electra Consumer Products branches, meaning Machsanei Hashmal and Shekem Electric. Globes put the deal at tens of millions of shekels. The models on sale: Mini2, Light2 and Quick4.

## The numbers

- INOKIM sells about 7,500 scooters a year in Israel, about 25% of the market by its own count
- The goal: five times the sales through the chains
- About 55,000 e-scooters worth about $16.6 million (about ₪51 million) were imported into Israel last year
- Industry estimates put more than 150,000 private and shared scooters on the roads

## Why the standard matters

The company says it is the only one in Israel with an official Israeli standards mark for e-scooters, and all models also meet the US UL 2272 standard for electrical systems and batteries. Anyone who has read about lithium battery fires in apartment buildings knows this is more than a line on a spec sheet.

## A bit of history

Nimrod Sapir founded the company in 2009 as MyWay, and it became INOKIM in 2014. His partner is Kfir Ben Shushan. The brand sells in about 30 countries.

## Before you buy

A scooter bought in a store gives you a physical address for repairs and battery replacement, which is a real plus. Still, look beyond the shelf price: check weight, real-world range and what the warranty says about the battery.
