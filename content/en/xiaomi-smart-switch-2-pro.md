Xiaomi has opened a crowdfunding campaign on its Youpin platform in China for the Smart Switch 2 Pro. It is a wall switch that goes in place of your existing one, so you can control lights from your phone or by voice without changing the bulbs.

## Installation

The switch fits the 86-type junction box, the standard wall box in China. It detects on its own whether the home has a single live wire or live plus neutral, so older homes without a neutral wire don't need new wiring. On the two- and three-button models you can choose in the Mi Home app which light each button controls, which helps when the original wiring is mixed up.

The front panel is 3.5 mm thick and sits on a steel bracket meant to keep it flat against the wall. The glass surface has anti-glare and anti-fingerprint coatings, and there are two colors.

## Control and automation

The switch uses Xiaomi Mesh 2.0, which Xiaomi says responds 50% faster and halves network setup time. It gets firmware updates over the air. You can use the physical buttons, the Mi Home app or Xiao Ai voice commands. It needs a Bluetooth Mesh gateway.

A few examples Xiaomi gives:

- Lights turn on when a door opens, if you have a compatible smart lock, gateway or curtains
- One button press when you leave turns off the lights, opens the curtains and starts the robot vacuum
- In wireless mode a button can control other devices, such as a vacuum or an air purifier, not just lights

## Safety and price

It is rated for up to 10A and 2,200W, with protection against overheating and overload.

Crowdfunding prices:

- One button: 114 yuan
- Two buttons: 133 yuan
- Three buttons: 152 yuan

Gizmochina puts these at roughly $17, $20 and $23. The campaign is in China only. The report says nothing about a global version, and the 86-type box is not the standard wall box everywhere, so check before you consider importing one.
