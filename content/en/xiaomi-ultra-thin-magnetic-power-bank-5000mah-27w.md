Xiaomi has released an updated version of its ultra-thin 5,000 mAh magnetic power bank in China. It comes less than two weeks after a 10,000 mAh model in the same line.

## What changed

The new model is 6.75 mm thick and weighs 105 g, a little thicker than the 6 mm version Xiaomi released earlier this year. In return you get faster charging both ways:

- Wireless charging goes from 15W to 20W on Xiaomi phones
- Wired charging over USB-C goes from 22.5W to 27W
- iPhones still top out at 7.5W wirelessly

Like the earlier model, it uses a silicon-carbon battery, which is what keeps it this thin. It snaps magnetically onto the back of the phone.

## Safety

Xiaomi says the new power bank meets China's GB 47372-2026 safety standard, which adds stricter requirements aimed at cutting the risk of battery fires and explosions. That matters for a pack that sits against your phone in a pocket or bag.

## Compatibility

Besides Xiaomi's 17 and 18 series, it should work with recent iPhones and Android phones such as the Google Pixel 11 series.

## Price and availability

It's on sale in China for 299 yuan, in silver, black and a new burgundy color. Xiaomi hasn't announced a global launch, though the previous version is sold outside China, including in the UK.
