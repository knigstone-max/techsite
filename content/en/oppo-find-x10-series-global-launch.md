Oppo announced the global launch of two flagship phones on October 21 at an event in Mallorca: the Find X10 and the Find X10 Pro Max. Both use a camera system developed with Hasselblad and ship with ColorOS 17.

## Cameras

The Find X10 has two 200MP rear cameras. The Find X10 Pro Max has three: a 200MP ultra-wide with a 1/1.56-inch sensor and a 124° lens, a 200MP main camera with a 1/1.28-inch ISOCELL HPC sensor that Oppo developed with Samsung, and a 200MP telephoto with an f/2.1 aperture and a 10cm minimum focus distance, so it also works for close-ups.

By default the phones save 26MP photos from ultra-wide up to 9.9x zoom. A Hi-Res mode switches to 50MP or 200MP. An optional Hasselblad Teleconverter adds 3.28x optical magnification to the telephoto camera on both models.

For video, all three rear cameras on the Pro Max record 4K at 60 and 120fps and 8K at 30fps. Oppo says the Pro Max main camera reaches up to 17 stops of dynamic range. Both phones support Dolby Vision, O-Log2 and, for the first time on an Oppo phone, Open Gate recording, which uses the full sensor area.

## Screens, battery and chips

- Find X10: 6.59-inch screen, MediaTek Dimensity 9600M (3nm)
- Find X10 Pro Max: 6.78-inch screen, MediaTek Dimensity 9600 Pro (2nm)
- Both: up to 144Hz LTPO, 3,600 nits peak brightness, BT.2020 color gamut support
- Both: up to 8,000mAh silicon-carbon battery, 80W wired and 50W wireless charging
- Both: IP66, IP68 and IP69 ratings

## Colors and availability

The Find X10 comes in Glacier Blue, Glow Coral and Mist Grey. The Pro Max comes in Titanium Grey and Luna White. Oppo says both go on sale globally starting in late October through its own stores, retailers and carriers. The press release does not list prices.
