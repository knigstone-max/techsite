The eufy Omni S2 robot vacuum-mop arrived in Israel in September, sold by Hamilton for ₪4,699 (about $1,482). Anker has now launched its bigger sibling, the Omni S2 Pro, with something we haven't seen on a robot vacuum before: UV-C lights on the underside that disinfect the floor as it cleans.

The S2 Pro has been on sale in the US since November 2 for $1,699 (about ₪5,388), and since November 9 in the UK for £1,599 (about ₪6,802 or $2,145). Hamilton hasn't said whether or when it will come here.

![eufy Omni S2 Pro annotated: six UV-C LEDs under the robot, electrolyzed water, a 100°C mop wash and 40,000Pa suction](img/posts/eufy-omni-s2-pro-annotated-en.webp "What the Pro adds over the regular S2. Graphic: TechSite")

## How the disinfection works

Anker describes it as two separate paths.

**The first is light.** Six LEDs under the robot emit UV-C at 275 nanometers, covering a 120-degree strip of floor. It's the same kind of light hospitals use to sanitize surfaces and water. According to Anker, the LEDs contain no mercury, the light stays inside the cleaning path, and it switches off the moment the robot is picked up. That matters in a home with a crawling baby or a curious dog.

**The second is the water.** The robot turns tap water in its tank into a hypochlorous acid solution through electrolysis, which keeps antibacterial protection going for up to five days. This part isn't new, since the regular S2 does it too. The water reaches the floor at 55°C, warm enough to break down grease instead of spreading it around.

In lab testing, Anker says the combination hits a 99.99% disinfection rate against E. coli, S. aureus, influenza and Aspergillus niger mold. Those are the company's figures, and there's no independent testing yet.

## The rest: suction, mop and station

- **Suction:** up to 40,000Pa, up from 30,000Pa on the S2, with five-layer filtration down to 0.3 microns.
- **Mop:** HydroJet 3.0, a 29cm roller pressing down at 16 newtons. Two scrapers flush it eight times a second, so every pass lays down clean water.
- **Station:** UniClean empties, refills, washes the mop at 100°C and dries it at 55°C. The dust bag and filter have an antimicrobial treatment.
- **Navigation:** a 3D ToF laser that recognizes more than 360 obstacle types, down to 1cm, even in the dark.
- **Climbing:** 3.5cm thresholds, 7cm double thresholds and carpets up to 3.5cm deep.
- **Size:** the robot is 326×358×98mm, the same as the S2. The station is 387×476×670mm.

## Wait, or buy the S2?

In a typical Israeli home, with tiled floors throughout and few rugs, the mop does most of the work. The regular S2 already mops with electrolyzed water, which covers a good part of the benefit. The UV-C lights add another layer, and they matter most to families with babies, pets or allergies. The Pro also carries a certification from the Asthma and Allergy Foundation of America.

There's no Israeli price yet, but abroad the Pro costs a lot more than the ₪4,699 the S2 sells for here. If you need a robot now, the S2 with a local importer warranty is a safe pick. If disinfection is the main reason you're shopping, it's worth waiting to hear from Hamilton.
