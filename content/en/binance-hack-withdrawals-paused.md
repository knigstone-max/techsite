If you hold an account at Binance, you've probably noticed that you haven't been able to withdraw anything since Thursday. The world's largest exchange confirmed it's investigating a "potential security issue" and suspended deposits and withdrawals on several networks. It hasn't said when they'll come back.

## What happened

On Thursday, October 29, reports began piling up about funds leaving Binance's hot wallets, the internet-connected wallets that handle customer withdrawals. Spokesperson Ross O'Leary said withdrawals were paused temporarily as a precaution. Binance said user funds are "completely safe" and that it will cover any losses.

Founder Changpeng Zhao (CZ) posted on X that it "does look like a hack" and that he expects funds to be fully covered.

## How much money

Nobody has confirmed a final number, but the estimates are huge:

![Data cards: about $5.9 billion stolen per Chainalysis, about $3 billion frozen, about $2.4 billion in bitcoin and ether still with the attacker, and Bybit's previous record](img/posts/binance-hack-numbers-en.webp "What's known so far, and what's still an estimate")

- **Chainalysis estimate**: about $5.91 billion (around ₪18.4 billion) stolen. If confirmed, that's the largest crypto theft ever. The previous record was Bybit's $1.4 billion in 2025, blamed on North Korea.
- **Freezes**: Circle froze roughly $800 million in USDC and Tether froze about $1.56 billion in USDT. On Friday Binance put the total frozen at around $3 billion (about ₪9.3 billion).
- **Still with the attacker**: TRM Labs puts it at roughly $2.4 billion in bitcoin and ether. No company can freeze those coins remotely. They can only be traced on the blockchain.

Bitcoin dropped more than 4% on Friday, falling below $78,000.

## Why stablecoins can be frozen and bitcoin can't

USDT and USDC are each issued by a single company that controls the token contract and can block an address. Bitcoin and ether have no such issuer. Investigators have to wait for the attacker to move funds through a cooperating exchange, bridge or swap service, and try to stop them there. Even when a freeze happens fast, getting money back to its owners usually needs a court process, and that can take months or even years.

## If you have a Binance account

- **Don't click links.** Binance itself warned about scams and unofficial links. Events like this bring a wave of emails, texts and posts promising "compensation" or "fast withdrawals." Ignore them. Use the official app or type the address yourself.
- **Never share passwords or codes.** No real support agent will ask for your 2FA code or recovery phrase.
- **Keep a record.** Screenshot your balance and transaction history now. If there's a compensation process, it'll be easier to show what you had.
- **Think about spreading risk.** Once things are back up, it's a good reminder not to keep all your crypto on one exchange. A wallet where you hold the keys puts the responsibility on you, but it doesn't depend on an exchange staying up.

## One more thing

In a separate incident, prediction market Polymarket paused operations after a breach through a third-party provider, DebugBear, with about $2 million (around ₪6.2 million) stolen.
