For a few years now, Samsung's FE has been the phone I suggest to friends who want "a normal Galaxy" without paying flagship money. It looks like the expensive models, gets the same software, and sells in Israel for a price you can live with. The Galaxy S26 FE, in Israeli stores since September 11, keeps that formula going. The question is how much it actually changed from the S25 FE, and what that's worth to you.

I didn't live with it for a month. I went through Samsung's official specs, prices here and abroad, and the in-depth testing published since launch, including battery, charging and heat measurements. The picture is fairly consistent: a good phone, with one real upgrade and a higher price.

## The price in Israel

Samsung Israel's list prices are ₪2,299 for 128GB (about $719), ₪2,649 for 256GB (about $828) and ₪3,299 for 512GB (about $1,031). Every version has 8GB of RAM.

Two months after launch, street prices are already lower. On Zap I found the 256GB model from ₪2,340 as an official import, and from ₪1,947 as a grey import. In the US it's $699.99 for 128GB (about ₪2,239) and $799.99 for 256GB (about ₪2,559). So for the 256GB version, the Israeli street price is already below the US price.

## Design and screen

From the outside it's hard to tell apart from the regular S26: aluminum frame, glass back, IP68 and Gorilla Glass Victus Plus on the front. It weighs 193 g, it's 7.4 mm thick, and it comes in Blueberry, Graphite and Pistachio. The visible change from last year is a single camera island instead of three separate lenses. At least one reviewer noted the island is a cheap-feeling plastic that may scratch, and it also means the phone doesn't lie flat on a table.

The screen is the same 6.7-inch FHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X, at 120Hz with 1,900 nits peak. Testing in direct sun found it perfectly readable, even if it's a long way from the S26's 3,000 nits. What's missing is an LTPO panel: it can't drop to 1Hz like the flagships, which costs a bit of battery.

## Performance: the real upgrade

This is where Samsung finally listened. After two generations on the Exynos 2400, the S26 FE gets the Exynos 2500, a 3 nm chip that was last year's flagship. In published Geekbench tests it's about 15% faster than the S25 FE on the CPU, but still well behind the S26, which is about 48% faster in single-core and 37% faster in multi-core.

Day to day, you won't notice. Apps open fast, multitasking is smooth, and games like War Thunder Mobile run at 60 fps on high settings. Stress tests showed an average of 42°C and a peak of 45°C, with 55% to 60% stability over time, which is middling. In a very heavy game like Wuthering Waves there were reports of dropped frames and even a crash. If you're a serious mobile gamer, this isn't your phone.

8GB of RAM and 128GB of storage in the base model feel stingy on a ₪2,299 phone, especially one promised seven years of updates. If you buy, I'd go for 256GB.

## Battery and charging

The battery is still 4,900mAh, same as last year. The new chip helps a little: in testing it lasts a full day with about 20% left at bedtime, and up to two days with light use. That's fine, but in a year when Chinese phones, some of them cheaper, ship with 7,000 and 10,000mAh, it's no longer impressive.

Wired charging is 45W, and one published measurement had it going from 2% to 100% in 63 minutes. Samsung promises 65% in about half an hour. There's 15W wireless charging and reverse charging for earbuds or a watch. There's no charger in the box, so add one to the bill if you don't already own a fast one.

## Cameras: the same set, again

This is where the FE is stuck. The rear cameras are identical to last year's: a 50MP main with optical stabilization, a 12MP ultrawide, and an 8MP telephoto with 3x optical zoom.

The main camera is good. In daylight photos are sharp, colors are lively without going overboard, and Samsung doesn't oversharpen. At night there's noise, but enough detail survives. The 8MP telephoto is the weak link: fine at 3x, smeared beyond that, and the 30x digital zoom is mostly a number on the box. The ultrawide is noisy at night too, and neither it nor the selfie camera has autofocus.

Video is better: 4K at 60 fps, 8K at 30, good stabilization, and a horizon lock that keeps the frame level even if you rotate the phone. My FanCam, which follows a person in a clip and keeps them centered, also works on older videos in your gallery. For the kids' kindergarten show, that's handy.

## Software and updates

One UI 9 on Android 17 is still the best reason to buy a Galaxy. You get text and recording summaries, audio cleanup in videos, AI photo edits (with a watermark), call screening and Now Brief with custom cards. Samsung also keeps a toggle for on-device-only AI if you don't want data going to the cloud. And seven years of OS and security updates is a serious promise at this price. There's also six months of Google AI Pro for free. Remember to cancel before billing starts.

Two less pleasant things. US units came with plenty of preinstalled apps, from Facebook to Netflix. And the USB-C port dropped from USB 3.2 to USB 2.0: wired file transfers are slower, and there's no wired video out, so no wired DeX.

## The bottom line

6/10

The Galaxy S26 FE is a good phone that's hard to get excited about. The new chip and seven years of updates make it a safe pick for the long haul, and Samsung's software is still the most complete on Android. But the cameras, battery and screen are nearly identical to the S25 FE, the USB port went backward, and the price went up. At ₪2,299 list, it isn't a deal. If you're coming from an S23 FE or older, wait for a sale or buy the 256GB at street price. If you have an S25 FE, there's no reason to switch.
:::

**What I liked**

- Exynos 2500: a real jump after two generations on the same chip
- Seven years of OS and security updates
- A big, smooth screen that's readable in the sun
- A good main camera and stable video with horizon lock
- IP68 and 45W charging that really is fast

**What I didn't**

- An 8MP telephoto, the same camera for years
- 8GB of RAM and 128GB in the base model
- USB 2.0 and no wired video out
- The same battery as last year, and no charger in the box
- A plastic camera island and preinstalled apps
