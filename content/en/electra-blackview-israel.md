If you have worked on a building site, a farm or in a warehouse, you have probably seen a chunky phone with rubber corners that survives a fall from a ladder. Most of these reached Israel through AliExpress or small importers. Some will now be sold at Machsanei Hashmal and Shekem Electric.

## What was announced

According to Geektime (19.8.2026), Electra Retail became Blackview's exclusive importer for Israel and invested about ₪10 million, roughly $3.2 million. The range covers rugged phones, tablets and mini PCs, and some branches will get experience zones where you can handle the devices.

Liron Katz, Electra Retail's VP of business development, called it a "full envelope of import, distribution, marketing, service and logistics". For buyers, that means a local address for warranty and repairs.

## Why it matters

Israel's rugged-phone market is small but real: companies equipping field staff, hikers, and people tired of replacing screens. Mini PCs are also quietly growing, mostly as small office computers or TV media boxes.

A week later Electra also started selling Google's Pixel 11 Pro. Together the two moves look like an effort to build a brand portfolio of its own, with better margins than Apple and Samsung leave.

## Still unknown

Consumer prices and specific models were not in the announcement. We will update this post when the devices reach the shelves, with a comparison to prices abroad.
