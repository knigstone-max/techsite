Hamilton, Anker's official importer, has started selling eufy security cameras in Israel. eufy is Anker's smart home brand, and the lineup here has five products: an indoor camera, two solar outdoor cameras and two kits with a hub. Prices run from ₪199 to ₪1,649, and everything comes with a one-year importer warranty.

The resolution isn't the interesting part. Every recording is stored locally, either on a memory card in the camera or on a hub that sits in your home. There's no subscription, and the company says video doesn't go to the cloud by default. Anyone already paying monthly for camera storage knows how that adds up over a year or two.

![Price table for the five eufy products in Israel, in shekels and dollars](img/posts/eufy-cameras-israel-prices-en.webp "Five products, from ₪199 to ₪1,649. Graphic: TechSite")

## An indoor camera for ₪199

The Indoor Cam C220 records in 2K. Its lens pans a full 360 degrees and follows a person moving around the room. It has infrared night vision, two-way talk, custom activity zones and a privacy button. It's the one for checking on the dog, on the kids while the babysitter is over, or on the apartment while you're away. Price: ₪199 (about $62).

## Two solar outdoor cameras

- **SoloCam E30**, ₪479 (about $150): 2K, 360-degree coverage as it pans, and it can tell people, cars and pets apart. The importer says the battery lasts up to about three months without charging.
- **SoloCam E42**, ₪549 (about $172): the top camera in the range. It records in 4K, with 8x digital zoom, color night vision, a siren and a deterrent light. eufy says that with the solar panel the battery lasts up to a year.

For comparison, eufy's US store currently lists the E42 at $179.99 (about ₪576). So this time the Israeli price isn't far off, and it's even a little lower.

Israel gets a lot of sun, so a solar camera is more than a gimmick here. Two things to check before you buy: that the spot where the panel goes gets sun for most of the day, and that your Wi-Fi actually reaches it. A camera on the yard wall or at the building entrance can be further from the router than you think.

## The kits: two cameras and a hub

- **Security Cam C35 2+1**, ₪899 (about $281): two outdoor cameras and a hub. Recordings are kept locally or on the hub.
- **SoloCam E42 2+1**, ₪1,649 (about $515): two E42 cameras and a HomeBase S380. The hub adds face recognition and more local storage.

One detail to watch: the E42 kit only works on 2.4GHz Wi-Fi. Most home routers broadcast on both bands, so it's usually fine. But if you've set up a single combined network, you may need to split it during setup.

## Before you buy

All the cameras run through the eufy Security app and also work with voice assistants and smart home systems. They're sold at Anker's official Israeli store and through authorized resellers.

A one-year warranty isn't much for a camera that lives outside in the sun and rain. Still, an official import priced close to the US is a good reason not to order from abroad. If you can't decide, a single E42 at ₪549 is a sensible place to start for the front door or the yard.
