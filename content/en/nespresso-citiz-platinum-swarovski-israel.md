Nespresso's holiday drops usually amount to a few flavoured pods and a new mug. This year the company teamed up with Swarovski and put out a special CitiZ Platinum with 44 crystals set into the body. It's already on sale in Israel as a limited edition for ₪1,250 (about $401).

## What you're paying for

Under the sparkle it's the same CitiZ Platinum Nespresso already sells. According to the company, 42 crystals run in a line down the side and two more sit by the two brand logos. The rest is unchanged:

- Four drinks plus hot water: Espresso (40 ml), Lungo (110 ml), Americano and Americano XL
- A 1-litre water tank and a used-pod bin that takes 9 to 11 capsules
- A 25-second heat-up and auto-off after two minutes idle
- A descaling alert
- 13 x 37.2 x 27.8 cm, 3.25 kg

It's an Original machine, so it takes the small classic pods, not Vertuo. If your cupboard is already full of Original capsules, nothing changes.

![Price comparison between the CitiZ Platinum x Swarovski, the regular model and the collection's accessories, in shekels and dollars](img/posts/nespresso-swarovski-prices-en.webp "What the crystals cost, and what else is in the collection")

## The crystal premium

The regular CitiZ Platinum costs ₪1,099 (about $352) on Nespresso's Israeli site, so the decoration adds ₪151 (about $48). That's real money, but not outrageous for a machine that's mostly meant to be a gift and look good on the counter.

If you only care about the coffee, the standard model pulls the exact same shot. If you want a present that looks special, that gap is the thing you're buying.

## The rest of the range

The machine comes alongside new capsules for both systems:

- **Festive Crystal Spark Espresso** for Original: a 40 ml shot with a long, dark roast and notes of cereal, caramel and cocoa
- **Festive Crystal Spark Double Espresso** for Vertuo: 80 ml, fruity and biscuity with a cocoa finish
- **Hazelnut Brownie** and **Blueberry Cheesecake**, dessert-flavoured blends for both systems

A sleeve of Original pods is ₪28.70 (about $9.20) and a Vertuo sleeve is ₪32.20 (about $10.33). Accessories include a green 560 ml Travel Mug in recycled stainless steel for ₪120, a carry strap with 36 green crystals for the same price, Pixie cups at ₪65 to ₪70, and Swiss milk-chocolate cubes at ₪45 for 200 g.

## Where to buy it

The collection is sold on Nespresso's site, in its boutiques and over the phone, for a limited time. Since it's a limited run, if it's on your Hanukkah gift list, don't leave it to the last week.

*Converted at the Bank of Israel rate for Oct 30, 2026: ₪3.118 per dollar.*
