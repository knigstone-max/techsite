Clip-on earbuds have been one of the bigger audio trends of the past two years. Instead of going into the ear canal, they hook over the edge of the ear like an ear cuff, so you still hear what's around you. Bose, Huawei, Anker and OnePlus already make them. Now Samsung does too: the Galaxy Buds On are the first clip-type model in the Galaxy Buds line, and they went on sale in Korea on October 27.

## What's different

The clip is made from a shape-memory alloy, so it should adjust to your ear and spring back to shape. Samsung says inside there's a speaker setup it calls TwinBoost, meant to deliver rich bass even though nothing seals the ear.

The usual problem with open earbuds is that people nearby hear your music. Samsung says this is the first time Galaxy Buds have shipped with sound-leak reduction. How well it works is something we'll only know after listening. For calls there's Intelligent Clear Call, which is supposed to filter out background noise.

They're rated IP54, so they handle dust and splashes. The case isn't water resistant, so keep it away from that open bottle in your bag. Samsung quotes up to 9 hours of continuous use at standard audio quality, and the case supports wireless charging.

## The Galaxy tie-in

As usual with Samsung, some features need a Galaxy phone or tablet with Galaxy AI. With one, you can call up the AI assistant by voice, use head gestures and record a quick voice memo. They'll pair with other Android phones or an iPhone, just without those extras.

One nice touch: Samsung says accessories made for the Galaxy Buds4 series also fit the Buds On, so you can dress up the charging case.

## Price

In Korea they come in black only, for ₩299,000. That's about $224, or roughly ₪698. Until November 30, Samsung Korea is throwing in two 30%-off accessory coupons.

Samsung first showed the earbuds globally on October 1, but its US page still doesn't list a price. European reports point to a December release there. They aren't on Samsung Israel's site yet, and there's been no official word about an Israeli launch.

## Should you wait?

If you want open earbuds right now, Israel already has some decent options, the OnePlus Buds Clip among them. If you're on a Galaxy phone and want the head gestures and AI assistant, it's worth waiting to see the local price. Anything close to the Korean price would put them at the pricier end of the category.
