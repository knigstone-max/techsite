Roborock isn't stopping at robots. After the Saros 20 Flow Complete arrived in Israel (and we already reviewed it here), two cordless wet-dry vacs from the F25 line shown at IFA 2026 are landing too. The one that matters most is the F25 Ultra Steam Gen 2: floor cleaning with steam up to 180°C, priced at ₪3,290 (about $1,039). Below it sits the F25 Pro Turbo at ₪2,190 (about $691), without the heavy steam kit.

Both are aimed at hard floors, not deep carpet, and both claim suction of up to 25,000Pa. The real split is how they handle stubborn stains.

![F25 Ultra Steam Gen 2 labelled: handle and controls, separate tanks, FlatReach 2.0 head and accessory kit](img/posts/roborock-f25-annotated-en.webp "What's in the box. Image: Roborock / Graphic: TechSite")

## Why steam up to 180°C matters

Most wet-dry vacs work with water, sometimes heated. Here Roborock leans on VaporFlow 2.0: dense steam that softens grease and sticky messes without heavy chemicals. The company says steam is ready in 30 seconds, volume is 2.5× the previous generation, and the temperature measured at the internal heater outlet reaches 180°C.

There's also a WaveFlow mode with 90°C running water for kitchen grease. Roborock cites TÜV SÜD tests: over 99.9999% bacteria reduction in steam mode, over 99.9% allergen reduction, and a solid-wood floor durability test with no swelling or warping after a five-year simulated use cycle. As always with lab numbers, real homes depend on the floor, the mess and how you use the machine.

## Flat head, edge arm, long battery

FlatReach 2.0 lets the vac lie flat and reach under furniture only 12.5 cm high. The SensEdge arm is meant to clean along walls on three sides, down to a 0 mm gap per Roborock, reacting in 0.18 seconds. SlideTech 2.0 wheels with separate motors should make push and pull easier, with up to 70° of swivel.

Battery life: up to 100 minutes in normal ECO mode, or up to 30 minutes in steam mode. Roborock talks about covering up to 780 m² per charge in lab conditions. In a real apartment with doorways and furniture, plan for less.

After cleaning, the dock washes the roller with hot water and steam and dries it. There's a roughly five-minute fast dry and a quieter ~30-minute dry at about 45 dB(A). A module that separates solids from dirty water should make emptying less grim, and there's auto detergent dosing.

## What about the Pro Turbo?

If steam isn't the priority, the F25 Pro Turbo at ₪2,190 keeps the same 25,000Pa suction, adds HyperScrub for stronger water flow and scrubbing, SlideTech 2.0 wheels and JawScrapers against hair wrap. No Ultra steam stack, and about a thousand shekels less.

## Who it's for in Israel

If your kitchen leaves grease on the tiles, kids or pets track mud, and the robot can't reach every corner, a steam wet-dry vac is a different tool from a robot. The F25 Ultra Steam Gen 2 at ₪3,290 sits above many basic Chinese wet vacs and below some European steam flagships.

The Saros 20 Flow Complete stays at ₪4,990 for hands-off daily cleaning. For a lot of homes the sensible pair is a robot for weekdays and a steam wet vac for stains and the kitchen. Just check that your floors can take steam, especially delicate parquet or heat-sensitive laminate.
