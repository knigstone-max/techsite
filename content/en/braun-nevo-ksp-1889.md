KSP put the Braun NEVO into its November promotions: the graphite 15010C for ₪1,889 (about $591), down from ₪2,499 (about $782). That is ₪610 off a shaver Braun positions as its flagship, with a two-year official-importer warranty. Per KSP's official Telegram channel, the deal runs through November 30 or until stock runs out, with a stated minimum of 50 units.

![Braun NEVO KSP price card: ₪1,889 instead of ₪2,499, saving ₪610](img/posts/braun-nevo-ksp-prices-en.webp "November deal on the 15010C. Graphic: TechSite")

## What NEVO actually is

NEVO is Braun's new premium line. The model at KSP uses AeroTouch: two gliding foils and 250 blades meant to shave close with less skin friction. A NEVO Smart processor adjusts power to beard density in real time, and the head flexes up to 40 degrees to follow facial contours.

The body is surgical stainless steel, the unit is made in Germany, and it is fully waterproof for wet or dry use. Battery life is up to 60 minutes, with a five-minute quick charge. In the box you also get a graphite leather travel case.

![Braun NEVO annotated: AeroTouch, Smart processor and stainless-steel body](img/posts/braun-nevo-annotated-en.webp "What you are paying for beyond the blades. Graphic: TechSite")

## The NEVO Care 7-in-1 station

What separates this kit from a plain shaver is the care station. NEVO Care cleans, lubricates and charges the unit on its own. Braun says station cleaning is ten times more thorough than a rinse under the tap. If you shave every day, that removes the daily ritual of popping the head open and rinsing it in the sink.

## Who it is for

If you want a daily shaver at a mid-range price, KSP and other chains still sell cheaper Series models. NEVO is for people willing to pay for premium hardware, German manufacturing and a station that does the maintenance. ₪1,889 is still a lot of money, but against ₪2,499 it is one of the lower prices we have seen for this model in Israel since launch.
