Bosch announced on November 17 a refreshed and renamed range of fully automatic espresso machines for the North American market: **VeroCafe Series 4, 6, 8 and 10**. Prices run from **$999 to $2,999** (about ₪3,323 to ₪9,975), before US sales tax, and they're on sale now on Bosch's US site.

Bean-to-cup machines are one of the fastest-moving categories in kitchen appliances this year. [De'Longhi](/techsite/en/posts/delonghi-rivelia-start-review-israel/) and [Dreame](/techsite/en/posts/dreame-ecceluxe-coffee-machines-israel/) have both pushed cold brew and quick bean swaps, and Bosch is now answering with its own version.

![Bosch VeroCafe: Quick Cup, up to 41 drinks, an adjustable drip tray, AutoMilk Clean and what only the Series 10 gets](img/posts/bosch-verocafe-whats-new-en.webp "What's new in the range. Graphic: TechSite")

## What's new across the range

- **Quick Cup**: press and hold one of the drink icons, and brewing starts within seconds. Made for the morning when you hit snooze one time too many.
- **Up to 41 drinks** on some models, from espresso to cappuccino and **cold brew**, now with XL sizes too.
- **Adjustable drip tray** that fits travel mugs up to 6.5 inches (16.5 cm) tall, so you can brew straight into the cup you're taking to the car.
- **AutoMilk Clean**: rinses the milk system with steam after every use.
- **Calc'n Clean**: cleaning and descaling in a single programme.
- **Home Connect**: start a drink from your phone, get maintenance reminders and recipes.
- **Screens**: the Series 6, 8 and 10 have a colour touchscreen of 3.7 to 6.8 inches. The Series 4 has a touch panel.
- **Saved favourites**: 10 or 30 personalised drinks, depending on the model.

## Series 10: two hoppers, two grinders

The top model, at **$2,999** (about ₪9,975), adds three things:

- **Two bean containers, each with its own grinder.** Regular and decaf, or a dark roast for the morning and something lighter for the afternoon, without the flavours mixing. That's a real difference from the De'Longhi Rivelia, which also has two hoppers but just one grinder, so you swap a hopper by hand.
- **Automatic maintenance from cartridges**: you load cleaning and descaling solutions in advance, schedule the cycle, and the machine doses and runs it on its own.
- **Barista Mode** for fine-tuning strength, size, milk ratio, grind and brewing temperature.

Bosch hasn't given individual prices for the Series 4, 6 and 8 in the announcement, only the overall range.

## And in Israel?

On Bosch Israel's website, under coffee machines, there are currently only **built-in** fully automatic models (Series 8) and coffee grinders. The freestanding countertop machines Bosch is renewing in the US aren't on the local site, and the company hasn't said anything about Israel. Freestanding Bosch machines do turn up at some Israeli stores, so if you're thinking of buying one, check whether it's an official import with a local warranty.

Keep in mind that appliances sold in North America are built for the local 120V grid. Even if you see a VeroCafe at a good price on a US site, don't assume it will run on an Israeli 230V socket, and Bosch's warranty won't follow it here. The new features will probably reach the European Series 4, 6 and 8, but Bosch hasn't announced that, and it's worth waiting for an official word before planning on it.

## What it means for you

If you're shopping for a bean-to-cup machine for the winter, the direction is clear: cold brew and fast bean swapping are becoming standard, not luxuries. In Israel today, the [Dreame Ecceluxe Master FCM60](/techsite/en/posts/dreame-ecceluxe-master-fcm60-review-israel/) does cold brew for ₪2,110 (about $634), and the De'Longhi Rivelia Start, at about ₪3,200 (about $962), lets you swap hoppers in a second. A machine with two separate grinders like the Series 10 isn't available here yet.
