Xiaomi has launched the Smart Band 10 Pro in Spain. It replaces the Smart Band 9 Pro from 2024 and starts at €79.99, with a ceramic version at €99.99.

## Design and screen

The body is 9.7 mm thick, down from 10.8 mm on the previous model, and weighs 21.6 g. Xiaomi uses a new aluminum alloy that it says has 40% higher yield strength and 20% higher hardness. Colors are silver, black and pink with matching straps, plus the ceramic edition.

The 1.74-inch AMOLED panel has thinner, even bezels. Peak brightness is 2,000 nits, which Xiaomi says is 33% higher than last generation, and the refresh rate is 60 Hz.

## Battery

Xiaomi claims:

- Up to 21 days with light use
- 15 days with typical use
- 8 days with the always-on display turned on

## Phones and health tracking

The band pairs with iPhone as well as Android, and it can sync with two phones at once so you get notifications from both.

For health tracking there's a new optical heart-rate module with dual light sources and dual sensors, which Xiaomi says improves accuracy. A new sleep algorithm is claimed to be 11% more accurate at detecting when you fall asleep and wake up, and 14% more accurate on sleep stages. It also tracks heart rate variability overnight to estimate recovery, along with stress, blood oxygen and menstrual health. The band is rated 5 ATM and can measure heart rate underwater.

There are 150 workout modes, including a new running-track mode and a cycling mode that turns your phone into a bike computer with live data sync.

The prices above are for Spain. The report doesn't give pricing for other markets.
