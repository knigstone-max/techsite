Plenty of Israeli homes have a routine by the machine. Baby clothes go in alone, underwear alone, the football socks as far from everything else as possible. The result is load after small load, each one running a full-size washer. Xiaomi's answer is about as direct as it gets: one machine with three drums.

It's called the Mijia Tri-Drum Washer-Dryer Pro 14.5kg, and it launched in Hong Kong yesterday. At the bottom is a 12.5 kg drum, and above it sit two 1 kg mini drums. Each drum has its own water inlet and drain, and all three can wash and dry at the same time.

![Xiaomi Mijia tri-drum annotated: two 1 kg mini drums on top, a 12.5 kg main drum below, and separate water in and out for each drum](img/posts/xiaomi-tri-drum-annotated-en.webp "Three washers in one body. Graphic: TechSite")

## Why three and not two

That question came up at the launch too. According to HK01, Xiaomi's answer was about space. A machine that grows upward takes the same floor area either way, so you may as well fit two small drums up top instead of one. That way you can give one drum to baby clothes or underwear, and the other to socks or the dog's blanket.

**The big drum** washes up to 12.5 kg and dries up to 8 kg, spinning at up to 1,400 rpm. Xiaomi says it fits, for example, 5 full bedding sets or 20 pairs of jeans.

**The two mini drums** wash up to 1 kg each and dry up to 0.5 kg, spinning at 1,200 rpm. Drying there is gentle, at 35 to 40°C, which suits delicates like silk.

Note the gap between washing and drying in the big drum. Load the full 12.5 kg and you'll need to take some out before drying, since it tops out at 8 kg.

## What else it does

- **OxygenCare:** diamond-coated electrodes split the water to create active oxygen, which Xiaomi says handles 20 kinds of stains, soy sauce, juice and blood among them, and brightens yellowed whites.
- **Auto-dosing:** each drum doses its own detergent, and the big drum doses softener too. Xiaomi says one fill lasts a month.
- **Fast programs:** a 32-minute smart wash and a 12-minute quick wash, based on Xiaomi's own tests with small loads.
- **Drying in the big drum:** the gentle mode runs at 55 to 65°C, with a sensor that stops when the load is dry. There are also programs for pet hair and down jackets.
- **App:** remote control through Mi Home, including water temperature, rinse count and water and power use.
- **Motor:** an inverter direct drive. In Hong Kong, Xiaomi covers it for 12 years.

Xiaomi also cites a TÜV Rheinland test in which a specific program removed 99.99% of E. coli from the inlet pipe. That holds in lab conditions, not necessarily in an ordinary home.

## The size

This is not a small machine. It stands 117 cm tall, 63.9 cm wide and 60 cm deep, and weighs about 93 kg. A standard washer is around 85 cm tall, so this one won't slide under a counter. In a typical Israeli service balcony, with a cabinet or shelf over the machine, measure before you get excited. The plumbing is unusual too. HK01 points out it has three water inlets and three drains, which older flats can't always accommodate.

## Price, and will it come here

In Hong Kong you can pre-order it until November 15 for HK$8,999 (about ₪3,626, about $1,147). Sales start on November 16, and until December 16 it'll cost HK$9,199 (about ₪3,707, about $1,173). The official price is HK$9,999 (about ₪4,029, about $1,275), including delivery, installation and removal of the old machine.

It hasn't been announced for Israel. Hamilton, Xiaomi's official importer here, sells the brand's phones, TVs and small appliances, and for large white goods it imports Midea and Toshiba. The machine runs on 220 to 240 volts at 50 Hz, which matches the Israeli grid, but without an official importer there's no warranty and no service, and a 93 kg three-drum washer isn't something you want to bring in yourself.

Whether or not it ever arrives, the direction is interesting. We recently looked at LG's WashTower, which stacks a washer and dryer in one body. Xiaomi takes the same grow-upward idea somewhere else: rather than joining a washer and a dryer, it splits the laundry itself three ways. For families with babies, allergies or pets, that could be the more useful trick.
