Until this year, anyone looking for the cheapest music service in Israel knew the answer: YouTube Music, at ₪19.90 a month. Then the price rises came. In July Apple raised Apple Music, in August YouTube updated every plan it sells here, and suddenly the three big services cost exactly the same for one person: **₪23.90 (about $7.17) a month**.

When the price is identical, the decision comes down to the details. And the details turn out to be quite different.

![Price table: Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music and YouTube Premium in Israel, with individual, student, duo and family plans, lossless audio and free tiers](img/posts/music-streaming-israel-prices-en.webp "Every plan in one place. Graphic: TechSite")

## Individual: a tie, with different free trials

On a solo plan all three cost ₪23.90. The difference is how long you can try for free:

- **Spotify** gives new subscribers **three months for ₪0**, in an offer that runs until December 31, 2026. Only if you haven't tried Premium before.
- **YouTube** gives eligible new subscribers the first month free on any of its plans.

## Couples: only Spotify

If there are two of you living together, Spotify has a **Duo plan at ₪33.90 (about $10.17)** with two separate accounts. That's ₪16.95 each. Apple and YouTube have no couples plan, so a pair pays for either two individual plans or a family plan.

## Families: YouTube is cheapest, Apple is most generous

- **YouTube Music:** **₪38.90 (about $11.67)**, for up to five extra family members aged 13 and up in the same household. The cheapest of the three.
- **Apple Music:** **₪39.90 (about $11.97)**, shared with up to five other people.
- **Spotify:** **₪39.90**, but only **up to 4 accounts**. You can buy one or two more for an extra fee.

So for a family of five or six, Spotify is the least attractive option, while Apple and YouTube are almost level.

## Students: ₪12.90 everywhere

All three charge **₪12.90 (about $3.87)** for students, with eligibility checks. On Spotify the first month is free.

## Sound quality

Spotify Premium now includes **lossless audio up to 24-bit/44.1kHz**. Apple Music includes **lossless and Spatial Audio** at no extra cost. On ordinary Bluetooth earbuds you probably won't hear much difference, but with good wired headphones or a home stereo, it starts to matter.

## Free tier

Spotify and YouTube Music let you listen for free, with ads and limits. Apple Music has no such version: you pay or you don't listen. If there's a kid at home who's happy with the free tier, that counts.

## And YouTube Premium?

**YouTube Premium** costs **₪29.90 (about $8.97)** a month, ₪6 more than YouTube Music alone. It includes YouTube Music, plus ad-free YouTube, downloads and background play. The family plan is ₪54.90 (about $16.47) and the student plan ₪18.90 (about $5.67). If you watch a lot of YouTube, it's probably the best value of any plan here. If you only want music, there's no reason to pay more.

## So which one?

**Spotify** if you're single or a couple, you like its playlists and recommendations, and you want to listen together with friends in real time using Jam. The Duo plan is its clearest advantage.

**Apple Music** if you're on an iPhone with a big family, and sound quality matters to you.

**YouTube Music** if you're a family after the lowest price, or **YouTube Premium** if YouTube itself is the app you spend the most time in.

One tip for everyone: before you switch, check the bundles. Apple sells Apple One packages, and YouTube Premium already includes music. Sometimes a subscription you already pay for covers it.
