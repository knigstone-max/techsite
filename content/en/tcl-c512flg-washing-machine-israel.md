TCL only started selling white goods in Israel in September, through Electra Consumer Products, and it's already adding a pricier model. The C512FLG is a dark grey front-loader with a 12 kg drum, and its headline feature is automatic dosing: you fill the drawer with liquid detergent and softener once, and the machine decides how much to use each wash.

It costs ₪2,990 (about $947). The 12 kg TCL machines that arrived in September sell for roughly ₪1,800 to ₪2,300, so you're paying a few hundred shekels to about a thousand more.

![The TCL C512FLG washer, labelled: the Auto Dose drawer, a Hebrew control panel with Wi-Fi, the 540 mm Super Drum and its A energy rating](img/posts/tcl-c512flg-annotated-en.webp "What's on the machine. Image: TCL / Graphic: TechSite")

## Why auto-dosing matters

Most of us use too much detergent. It doesn't clean any better. It leaves residue in clothes and in the machine, and it costs money. Auto Dose has one tank for detergent and one for softener, and the machine measures by load and programme. If you do several loads a week, it also saves you the measuring. Bear in mind it works with liquid detergent, not powder.

There's also an Auto Stain Removal programme and JetBubble, which mixes detergent into the water before it reaches the clothes so it dissolves better.

## Drum and programmes

The stainless steel drum is 540 mm across, and TCL calls it the Super Drum. It has a wave-pattern surface and tall lifters that pick clothes up and drop them, which helps with bulky loads like duvets. The motor is a Direct Drive Inverter, attached straight to the drum with no belt, which usually means less noise and vibration. The importer lists spin speeds of up to 1,300 rpm.

There are 14 programmes, including an allergy programme, 15- and 30-minute quick washes, Eco 40-60, washes from 20°C up to 90°C, and three steam programmes. You can delay the start by up to 24 hours and add a forgotten item once the wash has started.

Two smaller touches are worth having. Active Fresh turns the clothes every so often for up to 10 hours after the cycle ends, so they don't crease or smell if you can't unload right away. And there's a light inside the drum.

## Hebrew controls and an app

The LED control panel is in Hebrew, which isn't a given for a brand that's new here. There's also Wi-Fi and an app for starting and tracking a wash and getting fault alerts. Child lock and a drum self-clean programme round it out.

## Running costs and size

It's rated A for energy, using 46 kWh per 100 cycles and 56 litres of water per wash. It's 60 cm wide, 61.8 cm deep and 85 cm tall, so it fits a standard space. It weighs 72 kg, so check that delivery includes installation.

## Worth the extra?

If you're buying a 12 kg machine anyway, it mostly comes down to how much you value auto-dosing and the direct-drive motor. For a family running lots of loads, a drawer that measures for you is a small change you'll notice every day. If you wash once or twice a week, TCL's cheaper September models will do, or you could look at the same money for a brand with a long-established service network in Israel. TCL is new here, and it's too early to say what its service will be like over the years.
