Xiaomi's fitness bands have been among the best-selling gadgets in Israel for years, mostly because they do most of what a smartwatch does for the price of a restaurant meal. This week Xiaomi took the new generation, the **Smart Band 11**, global, along with a new, smaller watch: the **41mm Watch S5**. The Band 11 launched in China back in September and is now reaching the UK and Europe.

![Xiaomi Smart Band 11 vs Xiaomi Watch S5 41mm: screen, battery, body, payments, sports modes and European price](img/posts/xiaomi-band-11-watch-s5-compare-en.webp "The two new wearables side by side. Graphic: TechSite")

## Smart Band 11: a brighter screen, the same long battery

- **A 1.72-inch AMOLED screen** with thin, even bezels and up to **2,000 nits**. That's the difference you'll notice most outdoors in the midday sun.
- **Up to 21 days of battery** on the standard model, up to 18 on the NFC version. Those are light-use figures, so continuous tracking and a few workouts a week will bring them down.
- **A new heart-rate sensor** with two LEDs and two photodiodes. Xiaomi quotes 98.2% heart-rate accuracy, based on its own testing.
- **150+ sports modes**, 5ATM water resistance and heart-rate tracking while swimming.
- **An improved sleep algorithm** (Sleep Algorithm 2.0).
- **HyperOS 4**, Xiaomi's new software.

The most useful addition, at least on paper, is the ability to **connect to a Xiaomi phone and an iPhone at the same time**. If you carry a work phone and a personal one, as plenty of people here do, you get notifications from both on one band.

The standard model is aluminium, and the NFC versions also come in ceramic and metal finishes.

## Watch S5 41mm: a watch for smaller wrists

Xiaomi's Watch S series has always been on the big side. The new model is aimed at people who want something more delicate:

- **A 9.9 mm stainless steel body** with articulated lugs that follow the wrist
- **A 1.32-inch AMOLED screen** at up to **3,000 nits**
- **A 590mAh battery** rated for up to 14 days
- **Google Wallet**, a first for the series, alongside Xiaomi Pay
- 150+ sports modes, a health summary that looks at more than 50 metrics, and women's health tracking built with the Clue app
- Colours: black and light blue, plus a white edition with a ceramic bezel and a leather strap

On payments in Israel: contactless payment support on the watch varies by market. Google Wallet works here on phones, but if paying from your wrist is the reason you're buying it, check that your card is supported on watches too.

## Prices

- **Smart Band 11**: from €59.90 (about ₪214, or $66). In the UK it's £54.99 (about ₪236, or $72).
- **Watch S5 41mm**: from €229.90 (about ₪822, or $252). In the UK it's £199.99 (about ₪859, or $263).

Neither will be sold officially in the US.

## And in Israel

On the day we wrote this, we couldn't find an official Israeli price for either one. What's already in stores:

- The **46mm Watch S5** is sold by Hamilton, Xiaomi's official importer, for ₪879 (about $270).
- The **Smart Band 11 Active**, the cheaper version with a 1.47-inch TFT screen, sells at GoMobile for ₪165 (about $51), or ₪139 (about $43) in Eilat.

## Worth waiting for?

If your Band 9 or Band 10 still works, the changes don't justify an immediate upgrade. If you're buying your first band, or you juggle two phones, the Band 11 is worth a short wait. The 41mm Watch S5 is mainly interesting for people who have skipped smartwatches so far because they were too big for their wrist.
