OPPO launched the Reno17 series in China this week, and it comes with a small twist. The Pro is the smaller phone of the two. The regular Reno17 has a 6.56-inch screen and an **8,000mAh** battery, the biggest the Reno line has ever had. The Reno17 Pro drops to a 6.32-inch screen but gets a **200MP** main camera and a faster chip.

Reno is OPPO's mid-range line, below the Find X flagships. In Israel it's sold by Ronlight, the official importer, and it usually shows up here a few months after China.

## Two phones, two ideas

The **Reno17** is the battery phone. You get 8,000mAh in a 206-gram body that's 8.28 mm thick, running Qualcomm's Snapdragon 7 Gen 4. The flat screen is 2624×1208 at 120Hz, and its local peak brightness hits 6,000 nits, which is actually higher than the Pro.

The **Reno17 Pro** is built for one-handed use. It's 151 mm tall, 72.4 mm wide and weighs 192 grams. Inside is MediaTek's Dimensity 8550 SUPER, where all eight cores are performance cores, plus a 7,250mAh battery. OPPO says that's the highest energy density the series has had. The screen also supports 3840Hz PWM dimming, meant to cut flicker. That matters mostly if your eyes are sensitive to it.

Both phones charge at 80W, carry IP66, IP68 and IP69K ratings and ship with ColorOS 17.

## Cameras

The main camera is the big difference. The Reno17 uses a 50MP Sony sensor, while the Pro has a 200MP sensor measuring 1/1.56 inches. Both have f/1.8 lenses and optical stabilization. Full 200MP shots only happen in a dedicated mode. Most of the time the phone combines pixels into a smaller image.

Everything else is shared: a 50MP ultrawide, a 50MP periscope telephoto with 3.5x optical zoom and a 50MP selfie camera with autofocus. Every camera records 4K at 60 frames per second.

## Price

In China the Reno17 starts at ¥3,599, roughly $540 or ₪1,750, for 8GB and 256GB. The Pro starts at ¥4,199, about $630 or ₪2,045. Those are Chinese prices without Israeli VAT or an importer's margin, so don't expect to see them here.

## What about Israel?

Ronlight hasn't announced the Reno17, and there's no local date or price yet. OPPO's Israeli site still lists the Reno16 series. At Ronlight, the Reno16 with 12GB and 256GB costs **₪2,699** (about $830) and the Reno16 F is **₪2,199** (about $680).

That gives a rough idea of where the Reno17 could land, probably around the Reno16 or a little above it. That's my guess, though, not anything official.

A few things worth checking when it arrives:

- **Which model comes.** Not every model always reaches Israel, and global versions sometimes differ from the Chinese ones.
- **Battery size.** Global versions of Chinese phones sometimes ship with smaller batteries because of shipping rules. Check the number on Ronlight's page.
- **Hebrew and Google services.** The Chinese version doesn't include Google services, which matters if you're thinking of ordering one from abroad.

If you need a phone now, the Reno16 is already here with an importer warranty. If you can wait, the Reno17 looks like a real step up in battery life, especially the regular model.
