Ahead of the summer Tadiran released a new wall-mounted series, AIR ONE, and this time it talks about design as much as BTU. The unit comes in white or black at the same price, which says something about its target buyer.

## The range

- Five models, from 1 to about 3.5 horsepower
- Inverter in every model, A++ energy rating
- Built-in Wi-Fi and control through the MY TADIRAN app
- Night, quiet, dry and self-clean modes
- I Feel, which measures temperature at the remote
- Shabbat mode approved by the Mishmeret HaShabbat institute
- Five-year warranty, subject to terms

## The sensor

What sets AIR ONE apart from other wall units is a built-in motion sensor. You can have the air follow you, or avoid you while still cooling the room. Leave the room with it running and after a few minutes it drops to ECO mode.

## Above-average output

Tadiran says the smallest model, AIR ONE INV 140, delivers 9,840 BTU and up to about 11,020 BTU in some conditions, while other 1hp units hover around 9,000 BTU. In a south-facing top-floor bedroom, that difference is noticeable.

## What is missing

Tadiran did not publish prices on the launch page, and the final cost depends on installation and the retailer. Ask for a quote that includes standard installation and removal of the old unit, and compare at least two retailers.
