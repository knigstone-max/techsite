Anyone who owns a mopping robot knows how this goes. There's a coffee stain from yesterday, the robot rolls over it, smears it a little and moves on. The DEEBOT X12S OmniCyclone is ECOVACS' answer to that. When it spots a stain it sprays water and cleaning solution on it first, and only then goes over it with the mop roller.

Ronlight, the official importer, has been selling it in Israel since October 18 for ₪4,999 (about $1,605). Launch buyers also get a maintenance kit worth ₪599 (about $192).

## Mopping with a roller

The main part is the OZMO ROLLER 3.0, a 27 cm roller that presses down at up to 4,000Pa and spins at 220 rpm. While it works, 32 nozzles rinse it and an internal scraper pulls the dirty water out, so it doesn't drag kitchen grime into the living room.

![Spec card for the DEEBOT X12S: 27 cm roller, FocusJet, 1.6-liter dust bin, water tanks, 9.8 cm height and quick charging](img/posts/ecovacs-x12s-key-numbers-en.webp "The figures worth knowing before you buy")

Two more features matter in an Israeli home:

- **FocusJet** targets dried-on mess, like sauce splashes near the stove or paw prints from the balcony.
- **TruEdge 3.0** uses a 3D sensor to push the roller right up to walls and skirting boards, where a dirty strip usually gets left behind.

On rugs, the robot lifts the roller by 15 mm and covers it, so the rug stays dry.

## Suction and hair

Suction comes from a 150W BLAST motor. ECOVACS claims a 60% gain over the previous model and 224% on carpet, but those are the maker's own numbers and real homes will vary. ZeroTangle 4.0 is meant to keep hair from wrapping around the brush. If you have a shedding dog or cat, that's what you'll notice day to day.

## A bagless dock with hot water

The OmniCyclone dock doesn't use dust bags. It empties the robot into a 1.6-liter cyclonic bin, and ECOVACS says you'll empty that about every 48 days. How often you actually do it depends on your floor space and how much pet hair you have.

The dock also:

- washes the roller with hot water and pressure-cleans its own tray
- dries the mop system with hot air at up to 75°C
- holds 3.5 liters of clean water and 2.4 liters of dirty water

One caveat: the heater peaks at 100°C, but the actual water temperature varies, so don't expect boiling-water washes.

The dock is 33.5 cm wide, 46.5 cm tall and 49.3 cm deep including the ramp, and weighs 10.3 kg. Measure the spot before you order.

## The robot

- 9.8 cm tall and 5.5 kg
- Climbs steps up to 4 cm, which covers most living room to balcony thresholds
- Navigates with a dToF sensor and an RGBD camera for obstacles. A physical shutter covers the camera if that makes you uneasy
- 4,000mAh battery. During roller-wash stops it gains about 13% in 3 minutes
- AGENT YIKO 3.0 voice assistant that understands Hebrew, plus a Hebrew app

## Warranty

Ronlight gives one year of full warranty plus two more years of limited coverage, as long as you register the product online. The battery is covered for 12 months. Register on day one.

## Who should buy it?

Compared with the X11 OmniCyclone it replaces, you get stain pre-treatment, a newer roller and faster top-up charging. The X11 is still on sale in Israel, and some chains sell it for a lot less. With kids, pets or a kitchen that gets messy every day, the extra money probably pays off. If your floors stay fairly clean and you mostly want daily vacuuming and mopping, the older model will do the job and save you money.
