LG's StanbyME is one of those products that's easier to show than explain. It's a battery-powered screen on a wheeled stand, so you can move it from the living room to the bedroom, the kitchen or the balcony without hunting for an outlet. The first one came out in 2021, the second was a 27-inch QHD model, and now the StanbyME 2 Max is coming to Israel with a bigger 32-inch 4K panel.

There's no official Israeli price yet. The local estimate is around ₪4,500 (about $1,425). In the US, LG sells it for $1,299.99 (about ₪4,104), so if that estimate holds, the gap with the US will be smaller than Israelis are used to.

![The LG StanbyME 2 Max on its stand, labelled: a 32-inch 4K touchscreen, a battery good for up to 4.5 hours of video, and a wheeled stand that tilts and swivels](img/posts/lg-stanbyme-2-max-annotated-en.webp "What you're looking at. Image: LG / Graphic: TechSite")

## The screen

It's a 32-inch LCD with Edge LED backlighting, 3840×2160 resolution and a 60Hz refresh rate. It handles Dolby Vision, HDR10 and HLG, and it's a touchscreen, so you can scroll, draw or play on it with a finger. The previous model packed QHD into 27 inches, so this one is both bigger and sharper.

If you want the deep blacks of an expensive TV, an Edge LED panel won't give you that. That isn't the point here. This is the second screen in the house, the one that follows you around.

Inside is LG's third-generation α8 AI Processor 4K with the usual LG extras: upscaling, automatic sound tuning, and brightness that adjusts to the light in the room.

## Battery, stand and moving it around

LG rates the built-in battery at up to 4.5 hours of video, enough for a long film or an evening of a series. It charges through the stand or over USB-C. If you want to run it from a power bank, you'll need one that delivers 65W, and LG doesn't include one.

The stand tilts the screen 25 degrees either way, swivels it 90 degrees left or right and rotates it into portrait, which is handy for recipes, video calls or phone clips. The screen also comes off the stand, so you can prop it on a table or hold it on your lap.

Mind the weight, though. The screen alone is 5.6 kg, and with the stand it's 20.3 kg. The wheels are fine around an apartment, but carrying it up the stairs is another matter. With the stand it's about 1.31 m tall.

## Software and ports

It runs webOS 26 with LG's apps, the streaming services and LG Channels. It's also the first StanbyME with LG Gallery+, which has more than 5,000 artworks, so when you're not watching anything it can pass for a picture on the wall.

For ports there's HDMI with eARC, USB-C and USB 2.0, plus Wi-Fi 5 and Bluetooth 5.3. Sound comes from a pair of 10W side-firing speakers with Dolby Atmos, and LG also talks up virtual 11.1.2-channel sound. In practice it sounds like a portable screen, and if you want big sound you'll pair a Bluetooth speaker.

## Who it's for

It's a niche product, and at this price that's obvious. For about ₪4,500 you could buy a much bigger TV, but that one stays on the wall. The StanbyME suits people who want one screen that moves between rooms: parents who want something by the kids' bed, anyone who cooks with a recipe or a show running, or people who spend their evenings on the balcony. The touchscreen also turns it into a giant tablet for kids.

If you're tempted, wait for the official Israeli price. Whether it lands at ₪4,000 or ₪5,000 changes the maths quite a bit.
