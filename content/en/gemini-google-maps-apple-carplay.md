Google started rolling out Gemini in navigation to Google Maps on Apple CarPlay on October 21. It's available now in the US, and Google says a global rollout is also underway.

## How it works

While navigating in Google Maps on the car screen, you tap the Gemini icon and talk. Google's examples are multi-step requests: ask which park has good views of the city, ask whether now is a good time to go based on crowds and weather, then ask Maps to drive you there while avoiding toll roads. You can also find stops along the way and report traffic disruptions by voice.

According to 9to5Google, this is the first time Google Maps has offered a voice assistant inside CarPlay. Before, Google Assistant only worked when you used Maps navigation on the iPhone itself. The same Gemini feature already works in Android Auto and in Google Maps on Android and iOS phones.

## What's not included

On October 20 Google announced Personal Intelligence for Gemini in Android Auto, which lets the assistant use information from Gmail, Calendar, Photos and other Google apps. The Verge reports that the CarPlay announcement does not mention that feature, and it has asked Google whether it is coming to CarPlay.

The photo shows CarPlay on a car display in general, not the new Gemini feature.
