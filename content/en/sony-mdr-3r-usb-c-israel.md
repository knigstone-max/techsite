If you owned a Walkman in the eighties, you will recognize these instantly: a thin metal band, round foam pads in orange, and far less headphone than we are used to today. On November 5 Sony announced the MDR-3R, a remake of the MDR-3 that shipped in 1979 alongside the very first Walkman, the TPS-L2. The big change is the plug. The 3.5 mm jack is gone, replaced by USB-C that goes straight into your phone.

According to Gadgety, they are due in Israel during November at a suggested ₪219 (about $69). The Stranger Things Edition will be ₪249 (about $78). In the US they sell for $59.99 and $79.99, roughly ₪192 and ₪255, so the Israeli markup is fairly modest this time.

![Sony MDR-3R annotated: light headband, 23 mm drivers, swappable pads and USB-C](img/posts/sony-mdr-3r-annotated-en.webp "What Sony kept from the original and what it added. Graphic: TechSite")

## What you are actually buying

These are simple wired headphones, and that is the point. No battery, no Bluetooth pairing, no firmware updates. Plug them into a USB-C port and they work, as long as the device supports USB Audio Class 1.0. Most recent phones, laptops and tablets do. Sony still notes that some devices may need settings tweaks, and that it does not recommend using them with a Walkman.

There is an in-line remote for playback and a microphone for calls. The drivers are 23 mm, the same size as the original, and Sony says it rebuilt them from the old design specs with light tuning for how people listen now.

## Open design, with the usual trade-off

The build is open-ear, very light and comfortable for long sessions. There is also no noise isolation of any kind. On a packed bus or train you will hear everything around you, and people nearby will hear a bit of your music. If quiet commutes are the goal, look elsewhere.

The box includes two sets of pads, blue and orange, so you can swap colors whenever you like. The packaging and manual copy the original model's look, and the product packaging is plastic-free.

## The Stranger Things Edition

Alongside the standard model there is a Netflix tie-in inspired by Max's headphones and the Upside Down. It gets red and black pads, an engraved show logo and custom packaging. Technically it is the same pair, so the extra ₪30 buys you the look.

## Who should care

At ₪219 this is not an audiophile product, and it does not compete with wireless ANC earbuds. It is a fun gadget for anyone tired of charging one more device, for a work laptop where you do not want to fight Bluetooth, or simply for Walkman nostalgia. If your phone still has a 3.5 mm jack, note that these only plug into USB-C.
