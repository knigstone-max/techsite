I haven't looked forward to a Call of Duty like this in a long time. After Modern Warfare III and Black Ops 7, which both felt like they shipped before they were ready, it was easy to give up on the series. Modern Warfare 4 wins that trust back within minutes.

We wrote this review after playing on PS5. The game launched on October 23, 2026, and it comes from Infinity Ward, the studio behind Modern Warfare from the start.

## Campaign: a story you actually want to finish

It opens on the Korean Peninsula. North Korea launches a full-scale invasion, and you play a young squad of South Korean soldiers trying to survive a collapsing front line. Meanwhile Captain Price is on the run, fighting a personal war from the shadows, and the two threads collide in the worst possible way.

It's cinematic in the good sense. The pace never lets up, some moments stay with you after the console is off, and the story ties up threads from almost a decade of Modern Warfare. If you played the earlier games, it's an ending that feels earned.

## Multiplayer: lots of maps, a steady hand

Launch brings 20 multiplayer maps: 12 new 6v6 maps, four Gunfight maps, three Ground War maps and a new modular training facility called Kill Block. Combat feels grounded and precise, with fluid movement and good control in every fight. It isn't Black Ops 7's frantic speed, and that's the point: there's room to think, and it's never dull.

## DMZ is back, and better than ever

The extraction mode returns with a map called Hajin. You go in as an off-the-books asset behind enemy lines, and every run forces choices about what to grab, who to fight and when to get out. This is the mode that kept us saying "one more round" at 2am.

## It feels right on PS5

The PS5 version supports DualSense vibration and trigger effects, and you feel the difference with every weapon. There's a PS5 Pro Enhanced version, and online matches go up to 60 players. Online play needs PS Plus.

## Bottom line

Modern Warfare 4 is the most complete Call of Duty in years: a strong campaign, tight multiplayer and addictive DMZ in one package. If you skipped the last few and were waiting for the series to come back, now's the time.
