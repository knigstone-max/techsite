Huawei unveiled its new flagship line, the Mate 90 series, on October 20. There are four models: Mate 90, Mate 90 Pro, Mate 90 Pro Max and Mate 90 RS Ultimate Design. All of them use chips Huawei designs itself, and the product pages list HarmonyOS 7 as the software.

## Prices in China

Huawei's Chinese store lists these starting prices:

- Mate 90: 5,999 yuan
- Mate 90 Pro: 6,999 yuan
- Mate 90 Pro Max: 9,499 yuan
- Mate 90 RS Ultimate Design: the top of the range, which EFE puts at 12,999 yuan

## Chips

Each model gets a different Kirin chip. The base Mate 90 has the Kirin 9030 and the Mate 90 Pro has the Kirin 9035. The Pro Max and the RS use the Kirin 9050 family. According to EFE, the Kirin 9050 Pro first appeared last month in the Mate XT 2 tri-fold, and Huawei says it is up to 31% faster than the previous generation. On the base Mate 90 page Huawei claims a 27% overall performance gain.

## Screen, body and battery

The Pro and Pro Max pages list a peak screen brightness of 12,000 nits. The Mate 90, Pro and Pro Max all carry IP68 water resistance (down to 6 meters) and IP69 protection against high-pressure, high-temperature water jets. The base Mate 90 has a 6,600 mAh battery and 66W wired charging.

## Cameras

Huawei says the whole line uses a new color system, True-to-Color 3.0, with an infrared coating on every lens. The company claims a 37% improvement in color accuracy and 62% in skin tones. The Pro Max adds a 200-megapixel telephoto camera with a large sensor and hardware stabilization, which Huawei says stays sharp beyond 20x zoom.

## Availability

So far the phones are listed only on Huawei's Chinese store. Huawei hasn't announced international pricing or dates.
