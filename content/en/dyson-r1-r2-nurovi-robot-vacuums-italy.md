Dyson first announced two robot vacuums, the R1 Nurovi Dry and the R2 Nurovi Wash+Dry, in London on September 3. On October 20 the company started selling both in Italy through its own channels, according to notizie.it, with authorized retailers to follow in the coming months.

## Two models

- **R1 Nurovi Dry** (549 euros): vacuum only.
- **R2 Nurovi Wash+Dry** (849 euros): vacuums and also washes hard floors.

## What they share

Both robots carry the piezo dust sensor from Dyson's cordless vacuums. It measures how much dust is on the floor and raises suction where it's dirtier. Dyson calls the pickup method Twin-capture: the brush bar sweeps up larger debris while the motor handles fine dust. On its UK product page Dyson lists 21,000 Pa of suction for the R2.

Both have an extending side sweeper built to reach edges and corners without tangling in hair, including pet hair. For the R2, Dyson lists a sweeper reach of 27 mm, LiDAR room mapping, and the ability to climb thresholds up to 40 mm high. Scheduling, cleaning zones and no-go zones are set in the MyDyson app.

## The dock

After each clean the robot returns to a bagless dock with eight cyclones that empty its bin into a sealed container. Dyson says that container holds up to 90 days of dry debris.

## Mopping on the R2

The R2's mop pads are shaped like a Reuleaux triangle, a curved triangle built from three arcs that keeps the same width as it turns. Dyson says the shape lets the pads push into corners. After every clean the R2's dock washes the pads in 75°C water and dries them with 45°C air, which Dyson says prevents mold and odors.
