Dyson launched the first PencilWash early this year: a stick-thin floor washer with no serious suction, cleaning with a wet roller. Nine months later there's already a second generation. The **W1 PencilWash** was announced in September at Dyson's Berlin event and went on sale in October in the UK, the US and Japan. The headline changes are a magnetic dock that cleans the roller at the press of a button, and a head that keeps wet and dry waste apart.

It isn't sold here yet. We checked Dyson Israel's site this morning: the wet cleaner section has the Wash G1 and the [Clean+Wash Hygiene we reviewed on Monday](/techsite/en/posts/dyson-clean-wash-hygiene-review-israel/), and no PencilWash at all, old or new.

![Dyson's official cutaway of the W1 PencilWash head, labelled: 84,000-filament roller, wet and dry waste kept apart, 12 water points](img/posts/dyson-w1-pencilwash-annotated-en.webp "What's in the head, per Dyson. Graphic: TechSite")

## What changed from the first one

**A denser roller.** 84,000 microfibre filaments, 16% more than before. Dyson calls it the most absorbent roller it has put in a wet cleaner and says floors are left 50% drier than with the previous model. The US product page says 30%, so the figure seems to depend on how it was measured.

**Clean water from 12 points,** up from eight. Water is spread across the full width of the roller, and dirty water is scraped off on every turn, so only clean water touches the floor.

**Hair that doesn't wrap.** Nylon bristles inside the microfibre lift hair, wet hair included, and a comb behind the roller stops it tangling. Dyson says it works on strands up to 35 cm. If you have a dog or someone with long hair at home, you know why that matters.

**Wet and dry waste kept apart.** This is the interesting bit of engineering. Dirt stays down in the head instead of being pulled up into the machine. No internal pipes to clog, no filter. Dyson says the dirty-water tank and debris tray come out together and take 20 seconds to empty.

## The dock does half the job

The Dok is an open magnetic stand. Drop the cleaner on, it charges, and pressing MAX starts a 4-minute-10-second self-clean that rinses residue off the roller.

What it doesn't do is **dry** the roller. Dyson says so plainly in its US FAQ: take the washed roller out and leave it somewhere warm and ventilated. As our [wet-dry vacuum buying guide](/techsite/en/posts/wet-dry-vacuum-buying-guide-israel/) explains, hot-air drying is what stops the wet-mop smell, and on machines above ₪2,000 it's close to standard. Here you get something much lighter and smaller, and give that up.

## The numbers

- **Weight:** 2.4 kg per the UK announcement, and just 470 g in the hand. Dyson's Japanese and US pages list slightly different weights, probably depending on what's included.
- **Battery:** up to 45 minutes, 3.5 hours to charge. Dyson UK says the battery is swappable.
- **Clean-water tank:** about 300 ml (290 ml on the Japanese spec sheet), good for 320 m² in standard mode, per Dyson.
- **Lies flat to 75 mm,** a full 180°, to get under sofas and beds.
- **38 mm handle.**
- **Two modes:** standard, and MAX for stains. A tap gives five seconds of extra water; holding the button runs it for a full minute.

## Price, and what to do in the meantime

![Price table: W1 PencilWash in the US and UK, Clean+Wash Hygiene in Israel, plus spec cards for weight, battery, tank, lie-flat height, self-clean and drying](img/posts/dyson-w1-pencilwash-prices-en.webp "Prices abroad, and what Dyson sells here. Graphic: TechSite")

The US list price is **$349.99 (about ₪1,171)**, and Dyson's own store has it for $249.99 (about ₪836) right now. In the UK it's **£299.99 (about ₪1,313, or $392)**. Dyson Israel hasn't announced a price or a date, and there's no reason to assume the US sale price will carry over as is.

If you want a Dyson wet cleaner today, the Clean+Wash Hygiene was ₪1,979 (about $592) when we reviewed it. It's bigger and heavier. If what drew you to the PencilWash was the weight and the low profile, but you still want suction and heated drying, there are [other wet-dry vacuums we tested this month](/techsite/en/posts/bissell-crosswave-reach-plus-select-review-israel/) that are already on sale here.

## The bottom line

The W1 PencilWash fixes two real problems with the original: dirt pulled up into the machine, and a roller you had to rinse by hand. It still doesn't replace a vacuum, it doesn't dry its roller, and 300 ml is a small tank. For a tiled flat, kids who drop things and no patience for dragging a big machine out of the cupboard, it's a good idea. We'll update when there's an Israeli price.
