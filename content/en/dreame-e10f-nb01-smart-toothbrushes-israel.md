In Israel, Dreame is mostly known for robot vacuums, and more recently for hair dryers and cordless stick vacs. Now it's stepping into oral care here with two electric toothbrushes: the higher-end E10F and the more basic NB01. Both are already on sale in Dreame's stores and on its Israeli website.

## E10F: a brush with a screen and tracking

The flagship lists at ₪449 (about $144), and as of November 1 Dreame Israel's site has it for ₪404 (about $130). It comes in silver and purple.

What you get:

- A sonic motor rated up to 84,000 movements a minute, adjusting its power as you brush
- AI tracking of 6 mouth zones and 16 tooth surfaces, with real-time feedback
- Seven brushing modes
- A pressure sensor that warns you when you push too hard
- A small screen on the handle
- An antibacterial brush head
- DREAME HOME app support
- Up to 180 days of battery, according to Dreame

180 days is a very big number for a toothbrush, and in practice it depends on how often you brush and which mode you use. Either way, this is a brush you can take on a long trip and leave the charger at home.

## NB01: the simple one

The NB01 costs ₪349 (about $112) and comes in black or white. It skips the tracking and the screen and gets a light indicator instead, which shows whether your brushing pressure is right. Battery life is up to 40 days.

## What the extra money gets you

For ₪100 more (about $32) at list price, the E10F adds two main things: tracking that shows which spots you missed, and much longer battery life. If you already brush for exactly two minutes in the same order every time, the tracking won't change much. If you have a kid who needs convincing to brush properly, or your dentist keeps pointing at the same corner, that's where it earns its keep.

Pressure feedback, on the other hand, is in both in some form, and it may be the most useful thing an electric brush does. Pressing too hard damages gums, and most people don't notice they're doing it.

## The competition

The Israeli market belongs mostly to Oral-B and Philips Sonicare, with a big spread of models at every price. Their advantage is replacement heads you can pick up at any Super-Pharm. With Dreame, at least early on, heads will probably be sold mainly through the company's own stores and site, so check what they cost before buying. The brush is a one-time purchase, but you'll buy heads every few months.

The E10F comes with a 12-month warranty.
