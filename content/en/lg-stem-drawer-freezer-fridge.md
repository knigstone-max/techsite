LG launched a new version of STEM in Korea on October 26. STEM is LG's line of fridges plumbed straight into the water supply, and it makes several kinds of ice: round Craft Ice, cubes and crushed. This model's news is at the bottom.

## Drawers instead of doors

The usual bottom freezer with two side-opening doors is gone. In its place are two rows of drawers. LG says it measured how far people bend at the waist and knees and how much muscle effort that takes, and found that a pull-out drawer you look into from above saves a lot of crouching.

- **The middle drawer** works as either a fridge or a freezer. In the LG ThinQ app you pick one of 14 storage modes, including baby food, meal kits, dairy, wine, kimchi, meat and fish.
- **The bottom drawer** is a freezer with an automatic ice maker.

## What the AI does

- **AI Fresh Care** learns when and how often the doors get opened and cools harder ahead of meal prep.
- **AI Saving Mode** tunes the compressor during hours when the fridge is barely used, to save power.
- **Voice control:** say you want to store beef, and the fridge suggests a compartment and mode based on how long you plan to keep it, then sets it once you choose. You can also control other ThinQ appliances from the fridge.

## Other details

There are now two compartments with food-specific temperature settings instead of one, air filters against odors and bacteria, and doors that close on their own past a certain angle. A 6.8-inch display sits on the front. It's designed for a flush built-in look: it needs just 4 mm on each side and 9 mm on top, 40% less top clearance than before.

## Price and availability

For now this is a Korea-only launch. LG's release gives no price and says nothing about other markets, Israel included.
