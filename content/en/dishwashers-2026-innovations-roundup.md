After [the laundry room](/techsite/en/posts/washers-dryers-2026-innovations-roundup/), [fridges](/techsite/en/posts/fridges-2026-innovations-roundup/) and [the kitchen](/techsite/en/posts/kitchen-appliances-2026-innovations-roundup/), the dishwasher gets a chapter of its own. We touched on it briefly in the kitchen roundup, but 2026 was a more interesting year for it than that section had room for.

The direction is clear: the dishwasher wants to take the small decisions off our hands. Which programme, how much detergent, when to start, and whether the plastic boxes will come out wet again. Here's what we saw this year, from whom, and what has already reached Israeli shops.

![Dishwashers in 2026: a camera inside, a one-hour wash and dry, automatic dosing, a self-opening door and zeolite drying, and what's already in Israel](img/posts/dishwashers-2026-trends-en.webp "Five ideas that kept coming back this year. Graphic: TechSite")

## 1. A camera inside: Miele G8 Diamond

The most intriguing idea of the year came from Miele, which unveiled the G8 Diamond in Berlin in early September as the flagship of its new dishwasher generation. It has a **built-in camera** that looks at what you've loaded. Miele calls it AutoVision: the machine works out how full it is and what's inside, and changes the cycle to match. Spotted pots? It turns up the cleaning intensity. Bottles? It adjusts the wash for their insides. Plastics? It picks the right drying.

The drying itself, ExcellentDry, pushes heated air through the cabin. You choose once between three levels, and from then on it's automatic. Miele says you can get cupboard-dry plastic boxes, or energy efficiency up to 25% better than the A threshold, depending on the setting. On top sits AutoDos, the automatic dosing system Miele introduced in 2018.

The clever part is the app: you can tell it to start only when it's 80% full, but not before 10 PM. Or to start when your solar panels are producing enough power. In Israel, with [time-of-use electricity rates](/techsite/en/posts/winter-tou-electricity-peak-hours-appliances/) kicking in for winter, that's exactly the setting you'd want.

The catch: it reaches Germany, Austria and Switzerland in April 2027, with other countries later. There's no Israeli date yet.

## 2. Wash and dry in an hour: LG

In April, at EuroCucina in Milan, LG showed what it calls the first full redesign of its built-in dishwashers in nine years, engineered specifically for Europe. The headline: **a complete wash and dry cycle in one hour**.

How? QuadWash Pro makes micro-bubbles that help detergent get into food residue, so less pre-rinsing. Dynamic Heat Dry+ uses a material that heats up as it absorbs moisture, which shortens drying. And in the AI SenseClean cycle, a turbidity sensor checks how dirty the water is at each stage and adjusts temperature, rinse count and detergent, with up to 32 combinations. We saw the range again [at IFA](/techsite/en/posts/lg-ifa-2026-seven-appliances/).

For a family of four that runs the dishwasher almost every evening, one hour instead of three is a difference you feel. But LG hasn't announced an Israeli price or date.

## 3. Automatic dosing: Hisense S7 and ATAG

Fill a reservoir once and forget it for weeks. On the **Hisense S7**, launched in Dubai in October, the reservoir lasts 20 cycles. It also has AI Super Wash, which reads load size and soil and adjusts water, heat, detergent and time, a tilted 6.86-inch touchscreen, 16 place settings, drying with a door fan and automatic opening, and 42 dB according to Hisense.

Dutch brand ATAG launched three new series in October. The **9 Series** gets AutoDose, push-to-open and 38 dB. The largest model across the range holds 17 place settings in four baskets. The 8 Series projects a three-colour light onto the floor to show how far along the cycle is, without opening the door.

Why it matters: most people use too much detergent, or a whole tablet when the machine is half empty. Dosing to the load saves money and avoids residue on the dishes.

## 4. A door that opens itself

This isn't a luxury feature any more. Haier, ATAG and Hisense all build in a door that opens at the end of the cycle to let steam out and let the dishes air-dry. On the Haier I-Pro Shine Series 7 it opens 10 cm. On the ATAG 8 Series, a sensor decides when to open.

As Israel Hayom noted last week, dishwashers with soil sensors and a door that opens at the end are already sold in Israel. If you're buying a new one now, insist on it. It saves electricity on drying, and it's the simplest win on this list.

## 5. Zeolite drying, already in Israel: Bosch Serie 6

If you want something from this year that you can order today, the **Bosch Serie 6 SMV6ZCX24E** appeared on Zap in July and now sells for **₪4,793 to ₪5,390 (about $1,418 to $1,595)**, with some sellers offering official import. It's a 60 cm fully integrated dishwasher with 14 place settings.

Its trick is **zeolite drying**: a mineral that absorbs moisture and releases heat, so dishes dry faster on less power, plastics included. It also connects to the Home Connect app, so you can start it remotely during cheaper hours. It's not a camera, but it's a proven fix for the most common dishwasher complaint: wet plastic.

## Two more worth knowing

- **Electrolux QuickSelect 2.0:** instead of choosing a programme, you slide to how much time you have, and the screen shows the effect on energy use. We covered it in our [Electrolux and AEG IFA roundup](/techsite/en/posts/electrolux-aeg-ifa-2026/)
- **Teka:** according to Israel Hayom, Teka has developed a dishwasher that lifts the lower basket to a comfortable height, so you don't have to bend down to load and unload. If you have back trouble, it might be the most useful idea on this list

## What to look for when buying a dishwasher now

- **A door that opens at the end of the cycle.** Simplest and best value
- **Scheduling or an app.** This winter, running the dishwasher after 10 PM instead of 7 PM is a real saving if you've switched to time-of-use rates
- **A soil sensor** that adjusts the cycle. Most mid-range machines already have one
- **Automatic dosing** if you want to forget about tablets. For now it's mostly on expensive models
- **A camera and a one-hour full cycle** are what's next. If you don't have to buy today, it may be worth waiting to see what reaches Israel in 2027
