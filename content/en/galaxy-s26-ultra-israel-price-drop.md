Eight months after launch, the Galaxy S26 Ultra is where many buyers like to catch a phone: still current, and noticeably cheaper.

## What it cost and what it costs now

At preorder in late February, according to ice (26.2.2026):

- Galaxy S26 512GB: ₪3,199 (about $1,040)
- Galaxy S26+ 512GB: ₪3,799 (about $1,235)
- Galaxy S26 Ultra 512GB: ₪4,549 (about $1,475), with a free storage upgrade

In late October, on the site of official importer Mobile Online, the Ultra sells for:

- 256GB: ₪3,500 (about $1,135)
- 512GB: ₪3,999 (about $1,300)
- 1TB: ₪4,950 (about $1,605)

At iTech Mobile we found the 256GB model in a time-limited sale for ₪3,299 (about $1,070). On the 512GB model you save about ₪550 against the preorder price, and on the 256GB model the gap to the full launch price is close to ₪1,000.

## What you pay for

The Ultra is still the best-equipped model in the series: a 200MP wide camera and a 50MP 5x optical telephoto. Samsung says the two sensors take in 47% and 37% more light than last year. If you do not need long zoom, the S26+ saves more.

## Check before you buy

- **Official versus parallel import**: the price gap is small, and official import means a local lab and Israeli software updates.
- **Extended warranty**: at launch Partner offered three years. Ask whether that still applies.
- **Trade-in**: in February up to ₪4,000 (about $1,300) was offered for an old phone. Offers are lower now, but still worth comparing.

The S27 will probably be announced around February. The lowest price on the S26 will likely come after that, on a phone that is a year old.

*Dollar figures are our conversion at the Bank of Israel representative rate of about ₪3.08 per $1 (26.10.2026), rounded.*
