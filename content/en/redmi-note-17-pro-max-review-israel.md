Redmi Note phones have been among Israel's best sellers for years. They never try to be the most advanced thing on the shelf. They try to give you as much as possible for as little as possible. The Redmi Note 17 Pro Max sits at the top of the new range, and Hamilton, the official importer, sells it for ₪2,199 (about $705) with 8GB of RAM and 512GB of storage. In Eilat it's ₪1,864.

I wanted to answer two things: is it worth paying extra over the regular Note 17 Pro, and how does it stack up against pricier phones?

## The battery is the reason to buy it

The headline spec is a 10,000mAh battery. Two years ago that number belonged to tablets and power banks. Xiaomi uses silicon-carbon cells, which pack more capacity without making the phone a brick. It's 8.65 mm thick (8.57 mm in white), which is slimmer than I'd expect with that much battery inside.

Xiaomi claims up to three days of use, 21 hours of gaming or 40 hours of video. Those are the maker's numbers and your mileage will depend on how you use it. Even at half that, this is a phone you won't worry about at the end of the day. Xiaomi also says the battery keeps 80% of its capacity after 1,600 charge cycles, so it should age well.

Charging is 100W, and the charger is in the box, which isn't a given anymore. There's also 27W reverse wired charging for topping up earbuds or a friend's phone. No wireless charging, though.

## A big, bright screen

The display is a 6.83-inch AMOLED at 2772 x 1280 and 120Hz. On paper it gets very bright: 3,500 nits at peak and 2,000 in high-brightness mode. For an Israeli summer, that's exactly what you want.

If flicker bothers you, there's 3,840Hz PWM dimming plus DC dimming. You also get HDR10+ and Dolby Vision, Gorilla Glass Victus 2, and Wet Touch 2.0 for when your hands or the screen are wet. The fingerprint reader sits under the display.

## Toughness is the surprise

It has an aluminium frame and both IP68 and IP69 ratings. The difference matters: IP68 covers being dunked in water, IP69 covers high-pressure, high-temperature water jets. Xiaomi also says it passed SGS certification for 3-metre drops onto granite. A case and a pre-applied screen protector come in the box.

The price is weight: 229.5 grams. This is a heavy phone, and if you're used to something small, your pocket will notice.

## Performance and software

Inside is a Snapdragon 6 Gen 5, a mid-range Qualcomm chip. Social apps, navigation, photos and light games are no problem. If you play heavy games at high settings, you'll notice this isn't a flagship chip. Storage is UFS 4.1 and RAM is LPDDR5X, both quick, and 512GB is a lot of room at this price.

It runs Android 16 with HyperOS 3, and Xiaomi promises up to six years of security updates. That's a big deal, since it means the phone can stay safe to use until around 2032. Just remember that security patches aren't the same as major Android upgrades.

The rest of the list is complete: Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 6.0, NFC for Google Wallet, eSIM, stereo speakers with Dolby Atmos, and an IR blaster so it can work as a remote for the air conditioner or TV. In Israel, people use that more than you'd think.

## Cameras: a decent base, no surprises

The main camera is 50MP with a 1/1.95-inch sensor, f/1.5 and OIS. Xiaomi advertises 2x "optical-quality" zoom, which is really a crop from the main sensor. There's no true telephoto. Next to it is an 8MP ultrawide, and that's the weak spot. 8MP isn't much, and I wouldn't expect much from it in low light. The selfie camera is 32MP, and video tops out at 4K30.

## Price and competition

At the importer, the range climbs in steps: the Redmi Note 17 is ₪1,219, the Note 17 Pro ₪1,549 and the Pro Max ₪2,199. Right above it is the Xiaomi 17T at ₪2,299.

That's its biggest problem. Only ₪100 separates the Pro Max from the 17T, which is a step up in class, with 12GB of RAM. On the other hand, the Pro Max gives you 512GB, the huge battery and IP69. So it comes down to what you care about.

Launch buyers get extras that soften the price: Redmi Headphones Neo (worth ₪299), a one-year screen-break warranty (worth ₪380), two months of YouTube Premium, six months of Google One with 200GB, and an accessory kit. Hamilton's warranty runs 24 months. Colours are black, white and purple.

## The bottom line

7.5/10

The Redmi Note 17 Pro Max is for people who don't want to think about battery life and don't want to panic over a drop or a splash. Battery, screen and toughness are excellent for the money. It loses points for the weak ultrawide, the weight, and a price that sits too close to the Xiaomi 17T. Catch it on sale and it becomes a much easier recommendation.
:::

**What I loved**

- 10,000mAh battery and 100W charging, with the charger in the box
- A very bright screen with high-frequency PWM dimming
- IP68 and IP69 ratings and an aluminium frame
- 512GB of storage and six years of security updates
- IR blaster, eSIM and NFC

**What I liked less**

- The ultrawide is only 8MP
- Heavy at 229.5 g
- Just ₪100 cheaper than the Xiaomi 17T
- No wireless charging
