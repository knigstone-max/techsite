Honor has taken the **Watch 6 Pro** out of China. The watch launched there at the end of September, and on November 19 it went on pre-sale in Europe. Its predecessor, the Watch 5 Pro, never got a broad international release, so this is the first chance in a while to buy Honor's round flagship in Europe without importing from China.

## What you get

- A **1.5-inch LTPO AMOLED screen** at 466×466, peaking at **5,500 nits** when only part of the screen is lit
- A **46.3 mm** case that's 11.6 mm thick and weighs 50 g without the strap
- A stainless steel front frame and a ceramic bezel
- **5ATM** water resistance plus IP68 and IP69, so water, dust and even pressure washing
- **Dual-band GPS**, NFC and Bluetooth 5.4
- Over 120 sports modes, including a running coach and a badminton mode that recognises different swings
- **Quick Health Scan**, which checks several health readings at once, alongside heart rate, blood oxygen, sleep and HRV

It works with **both Android and iPhone**, which matters: you don't need an Honor phone to use it.

## Battery: 18 days on paper

The watch has a 515mAh battery. Honor quotes **up to 18 days** in its ultra-long battery mode, **up to 10 days** with regular use, and up to a week with the always-on display. As usual, GPS workouts will bring those numbers down.

## The feature still waiting for approval

In China, Honor made a point of **cardiac arrest detection**. It wasn't in the European materials, and at first it looked like it wouldn't make the trip. Honor then told Android Authority that the European version does support it, but the feature is going through EU medical certification, and until that's done Honor can't market it. In other words, it's there, but don't buy the watch for it until it's approved.

## European prices

These prices apply in France, Germany, Italy and Spain:

- **Black with a sport strap**: €279 (about ₪1,006 or $307) at launch, down from €379 (about ₪1,367 or $416)
- **Silver with a steel strap**: €329 (about ₪1,187 or $362) at launch, down from €429 (about ₪1,547 or $471)

Launch prices last **until December 31, 2026** or while stock lasts. Early buyers also get a 22 mm leather strap, a pair of Honor clip earbuds and a 12-month warranty extension, subject to stock.

## What about Israel?

Honor hasn't announced an Israeli launch. Earlier Honor watches did make it here, the Watch GS 3 for one, so this one may follow. If you're thinking of ordering from Europe, check shipping to Israel and taxes first, and remember the warranty will be with the European seller, not a local importer. We'll update when there's news of an official import.
