GTA VI doesn't unlock everywhere at once. Rockstar launches it on 19 November at local midnight in each region, and the first place to hit midnight is New Zealand. The New Zealand PlayStation Store lists the release as 18 November at 11:00 UTC, which is midnight on Thursday in Auckland and, for us, **Wednesday 18 November at 13:00 Israel time**.

An Israeli account gets the game at midnight between Wednesday and Thursday, 00:00 Israel time. So on paper the New Zealand trick buys you 11 hours of Vice City before everyone else here. Whether that's worth the money and the hassle is your call. Here's what you need to know.

![GTA VI unlock timeline in Israel time: New Zealand Wednesday 13:00, Australia 15:00, Japan 17:00, Israel Thursday 00:00, US Thursday 07:00](img/posts/gta-6-unlock-timeline-en.webp "Every region opens at its own midnight. New Zealand first, Israel at night")

## How it works

On PS5, what decides when the game unlocks is the country of the account that bought it, not where the console sits. Changing the console clock does nothing. And you can't change the country of an existing PSN account at all. So on PS5 the trick needs a new account created as a New Zealand one, and a second copy of the game bought on it.

Xbox is simpler, and we'll get to it below.

## On PS5, step by step

![Six-step infographic: add a New Zealand user, load the wallet with a gift card, buy the game, turn on console sharing, pre-load and switch back to your main profile](img/posts/gta-6-nz-steps-en.webp "The six steps at a glance")

1. **Add a new user.** On the PS5 sign-in screen pick Add User and create a new PSN account with a different email. When it asks for a country, choose New Zealand. That's permanent for this account.
2. **Load the wallet in NZ dollars.** The New Zealand store only takes New Zealand payment methods, so an Israeli credit card won't go through. The practical route is a PlayStation Store gift card in New Zealand dollars (NZD), bought as a digital code from a well-known seller and redeemed in the store through the three-dot menu and Redeem Code. Stick to reputable digital stores with a clear refund policy, and make sure the card is specifically for New Zealand.
3. **Buy the game.** Standard costs NZ$139.95 on the New Zealand store (about $78, roughly ₪243), and Ultimate costs NZ$174.95 (about $97, roughly ₪304), plus whatever fee the gift-card seller adds. To be clear, this is a second purchase. If you already pre-ordered on the Israeli store for ₪319, that order doesn't move to the New Zealand account.
4. **Turn on console sharing.** While signed in to the New Zealand account: Settings, then Users and Accounts, Other, Console Sharing and Offline Play, and choose Enable. That lets anyone on this console, including your main profile, play that account's games. It can only be on for one PS5 at a time.
5. **Pre-load.** PS5 pre-loading has been open since 10 November. Download from the New Zealand account and clear some space. It's a huge install, so don't leave it to Wednesday lunchtime.
6. **Switch back to your profile.** On Wednesday at 13:00 the game unlocks. Sign in to your usual profile and play from there.

## Trophies, saves and online

When you play from your main profile through console sharing, trophies and saves land on the profile that's playing, which is your own account, not the New Zealand one. If you play straight from the New Zealand account, the progress stays there.

As for GTA Online, Rockstar hasn't detailed how it will work at launch, so we can't promise where online progress will live. What we do know: online play on PS5 needs PS Plus, and console sharing carries over the online multiplayer part of the sharing account's PS Plus. Cloud storage and automatic updates stay with the subscriber.

Two smaller things. Money left in the New Zealand wallet stays there; you can't move it to your Israeli account. And add-ons will most likely have to come from that same New Zealand store, since on PlayStation add-ons need to match the game's region. On languages, we couldn't find anything official comparing the New Zealand and Israeli versions, so we won't guess.

## What about the boxed copy?

It won't help. Physical GTA VI copies on PS5 hold a download code, not the full game on a disc, and the codes are region-locked. A box bought in Israel works on an Israeli account, not a New Zealand one.

## On Xbox it's much easier

On Xbox Series X|S you don't need a new account or a second purchase. Buy or pre-order the game as usual on your own account, finish the pre-load (on Xbox it opened on 10 November at 00:00 GMT), then go to Settings, System, Language & location, set Location to New Zealand and restart. According to player reports, the game should then unlock at New Zealand time. You can switch the location back to Israel afterwards.

This is a method players report, not something Microsoft or Rockstar officially supports. It has worked for past launches, but there's no guarantee.

## The risk, plainly

The part worth saying out loud: Sony's terms ask you to pick your real country of residence when you create an account, and for all details to be accurate. Sony can restrict, suspend or close an account opened with inaccurate details, and anything bought on it goes with it. We're not aware of mass bans over this trick, but it isn't an approved method, and anyone using it takes that risk on themselves. Your main account isn't tied to the purchase, but the terms also mention the console, so know that going in.

## The boring, smart option

Wait 11 hours. If you pre-ordered on your Israeli account and pre-loaded, you'll get the game at midnight between Wednesday and Thursday without paying twice, without gift cards and without a second account. If you weren't planning to take Wednesday afternoon off anyway, that's probably the right call. If you were, now you know exactly what it takes.
