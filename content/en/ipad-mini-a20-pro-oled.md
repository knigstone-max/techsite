Apple announced a new iPad mini on October 26. The headline change is the display: LCD is out and OLED is in, and the screen grows from 8.3 to 8.5 inches while the tablet keeps the same footprint, thanks to bezels that are 24% thinner.

## The display

- Super Retina XDR OLED with a 2,000,000:1 contrast ratio and P3 wide color
- Up to 1,200 nits peak brightness, more than double the previous model, and 600 nits full-screen
- A 1-nit minimum for reading in the dark
- A nano-texture glass option, new to the mini, to cut glare

Israeli site Poenta notes that Apple doesn't list ProMotion in the specs, so the refresh rate most likely stays at 60Hz. If you're used to 120Hz scrolling on an iPhone or iPad Pro, you'll notice.

## Thinner, and water resistant

It's now 5.4 mm thick. Gadgety lists the weight at 255 g for Wi-Fi and 263 g for cellular. The tablet is rated IP67 against water and dust, tested in the lab at up to 1 meter for 30 minutes. That's a first for the iPad mini.

## Performance

Inside is the A20 Pro from the iPhone 18 Pro line, built on a 2-nanometer process. Apple quotes up to a 6-core CPU, a 7-core GPU with a neural accelerator in each core, up to 4.5x faster AI performance and up to 2.7x faster graphics than the previous generation. The 512GB model gets a dual 16-core Neural Engine.

## Cameras, audio and wireless

- 12MP Center Stage front camera, moved to the long edge for landscape video calls
- 12MP rear camera with 4K video at 60 fps and a new flash
- What Apple calls an Acoustic Display, with woofers that work through the screen
- Apple's N1 chip for Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6 and Thread; cellular models use the C2 modem, which Apple says is up to 50% faster than before

It ships with iPadOS 27 and works with both Apple Pencil Pro and Apple Pencil (USB-C).

## Price and availability

In the US, the Wi-Fi model starts at $699 (about ₪2,150) and the cellular model at $849 (about ₪2,615). The shekel figures are rough conversions, not Israeli prices. Colors are midnight and starlight, with 128GB, 256GB and 512GB options. The new Smart Folio costs $79.

Pre-orders open October 28 in 36 countries, with deliveries from November 6. Israel isn't on that list, and Apple hasn't announced a local price or date. For now the previous A17 Pro model sells in Israel from about ₪2,260, according to Poenta.
