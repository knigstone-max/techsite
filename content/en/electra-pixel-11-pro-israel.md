Until now, buying a new Pixel in Israel meant choosing between a small shop selling parallel imports and ordering from abroad. In late August Electra Retail, which owns the Machsanei Hashmal and Shekem Electric chains, started selling the Pixel 11 Pro series in stores and online.

## What is on sale and for how much

According to Gadgety (30.8.2026), Electra carries only the Pro models:

- Pixel 11 Pro in 512GB and 1TB
- Pixel 11 Pro XL in 256GB and 512GB

Prices run from ₪4,799 to ₪5,449, roughly $1,560 to $1,770. In the US, Google charges $1,099 for the Pro and $1,299 for the XL in base configurations, before sales tax. The regular Pixel 11 is not part of the deal for now.

## Not the official importer, and that is fine if you know it

Electra is not Google's official importer. The warranty is two years through Electra's lab, and warranty repairs are sent to Google's lab in Europe. In practice a screen or battery job may take longer than you are used to with Samsung or Apple, so ask how long a typical repair takes before you buy.

The phones are eSIM only. All the big Israeli carriers support eSIM, but if you are moving from a phone with a physical SIM you will need to ask for a conversion.

## What you get

- Tensor G6 and Android 17, with seven years of updates
- 50MP main, 48MP ultrawide and 48MP 5x telephoto, with up to 120x digital zoom
- 8K video through Video Boost
- 204g for the Pro and 227g for the XL

Electra also sells the Google TV Streamer and Nest products. The move came a week after the group announced a roughly ₪10 million (about $3.2 million) investment in exclusive Blackview imports, a sign it wants a phone portfolio of its own beyond Apple and Samsung.

## Bottom line

If you want a Pixel with a receipt from a big chain and a warranty you can walk into a branch with, this is the easiest route today. If repair turnaround matters to you, get the terms in writing first.

*Dollar figures are our conversion at the Bank of Israel representative rate of about ₪3.08 per $1 (26.10.2026), rounded.*
