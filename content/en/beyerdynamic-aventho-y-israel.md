Most wireless headphones are done the day the battery wears out. beyerdynamic, the German brand best known for studio gear, wants to change that. On its new AVENTHO Y, the battery sits in the left ear cup under a magnetic pad, and you can swap it yourself. Biconnect, the Israeli importer, launched them on October 22.

## The specs

- **A 650 mAh user-replaceable battery** in a small hinged compartment under the pad
- **90+ hours** of playback with ANC off, 60+ hours with it on
- A full charge takes under an hour, and 5 minutes on the cable gets you 6 hours
- **Active noise cancelling** and a transparency mode, with 5 microphones
- 45 mm dynamic drivers, closed back, 20–20,000 Hz
- Bluetooth 6.0 with SBC and AAC, multipoint, 20 m range
- USB-C for wired listening, EQ in the app
- 316 g, aluminium yokes, folds flat into a soft case

![AVENTHO Y with callouts for the magnetic pads, aluminium yoke, swappable battery, battery life and Bluetooth 6.0](img/posts/beyerdynamic-aventho-y-annotated-en.webp "The headphones at a glance")

## Pads, colours and add-ons

The pads attach with magnets, so getting to the battery or replacing worn pads is easy. beyerdynamic also sells velour pads in grey or black. There are four colours: Liquid Black, Blue Fusion, Soft Jade and Peach Blush.

In the next few weeks an upgrade pack is due at ₪199 (about $64). It adds a boom mic and a low-latency USB-C dongle, which turns them into a gaming headset for a console or PC.

![Price comparison of AVENTHO Y in Israel and the US, plus the boom mic pack](img/posts/beyerdynamic-aventho-y-prices-en.webp "What they cost here and there")

## Israel vs. US pricing

In Israel they cost ₪599 (about $191). beyerdynamic's US store lists them at $149.99, roughly ₪469. That's a gap of around ₪130, but the US price doesn't include sales tax, and buying locally gets you an importer warranty. For headphones you plan to keep for years, that counts.

## Our take

At ₪599 these aren't flagship headphones, and they don't pretend to be. What you get is very long battery life, a brand that knows sound, and the idea that headphones shouldn't end up in the bin after three years. If you want the strongest noise cancelling money can buy, wait for proper testing. If you want an everyday pair built to last, AVENTHO Y look like a sensible pick.
