LG showed only seven products at IFA this year, and nearly all of them tackle the same problem: small homes, high power bills and not much time.

## 1. A fridge that understands full sentences

The next-generation LG SIGNATURE refrigerator gets an LLM-based voice assistant you can ask, for example, how to store a vegetable. The door has a 6.8-inch LCD, and AI Fresh learns when you open the fridge and pre-cools up to two hours ahead.

## 2. A washer that beats the A threshold

The new Fit & Max washer uses less energy than the minimum for an EU A rating and pairs with an A-rated heat-pump dryer. Its Direct Drive motor is certified by TÜV Rheinland for up to 30 years of operation.

## 3. A 25-inch WashTower

LG's single-body washer-dryer comes in a new size between 24 and 27 inches, better suited to European cabinetry. More than 3.2 million WashTowers have been sold worldwide.

## 4. A French-door fridge that sits flush

A Zero Clearance design lets the fridge sit flush with cabinets, and one drawer converts between fridge and freezer. It won iF and Red Dot design awards in 2026.

## 5. A dishwasher that finishes in an hour

The Fit & Max dishwasher washes and dries in a one-hour cycle. QuadWash Pro uses micro-bubbles, and AI SenseClean measures how dirty the water is and adjusts temperature, time and detergent.

## 6. LG's first hair dryer

LG is moving into personal care. The dryer uses Triple Airfluidics and, LG says, weighs about as much as a cup of coffee.

## What matters for Israel

LG has not announced Israeli prices or dates. Of the list, the fast dishwasher and the efficient washer are the ones worth waiting for: in a family of four they run almost daily, and every percent saved adds up on the electricity bill.
