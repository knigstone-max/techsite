Anyone who has cleaned a coffee stain off a fabric sofa knows the second half of the story: the stain is gone, and the cushion stays damp until the next day. Tineco's new machine is built around that moment. The **CARPET ONE Spot DualHeat**, announced on November 16, is a compact spot cleaner for carpets, upholstery and car seats, and it's the first we've seen in this category with both steam and a built-in dryer.

## Four ways to clean

The DualHeat name refers to two kinds of heat: hot water up to 70°C and steam up to 100°C. Together they give four modes:

- **Cool Clean**: cold water, for fresh spills and delicate fabrics
- **Warm Clean**: warm water, for grease and tougher stains
- **Steam Clean**: steam, for old stains that have set in
- **Boost Steam**: more concentrated steam for stubborn spots

The machine sprays clean water continuously and sucks the dirty water back up at the same time, with 17kPa of suction. There are two separate cleaning solutions: a pre-treat formula you apply straight onto a greasy stain, and a carpet cleaning solution that goes into the clean water tank. The wide brush tool got a bigger suction opening, which Tineco says picks up more water.

## And then it dries

This is the part that's actually new. Tineco calls it **FlipDry**: a plate on the machine flips out and blows air at the spot you just cleaned.

- **Max Dry**: hot air up to 70°C with fast airflow. Tineco says it dries the area in as little as three minutes, under its own lab conditions
- **Soft Dry**: a gentler mode for heat-sensitive fabrics

The plate can be angled to aim the air where it's needed. Three minutes is the best case in a lab. On a thick sofa cushion in a damp Israeli winter, expect it to take longer.

## Sofas, rugs and cars

Most Israeli homes have tiles, not wall-to-wall carpet, so for us this is really a sofa, mattress, rug and car-seat machine. Tineco ships interchangeable brush covers for upholstery and some smooth surfaces, and a crevice tool for narrow gaps. It weighs about 5 kg, has a carry handle, a 3.5-metre cord and a 1.5-metre hose, so it reaches stairs and the inside of a car. After use, a separate attachment flushes the hose with clean water so dirt doesn't sit in it.

## Price and Israel

In the US it costs **$249** (about ₪828) on Amazon. The announcement only covers the US, and the machine isn't listed on Tineco's Israeli website. Locally, Tineco sells the **Carpet One Cruiser**, a full upright carpet washer, for ₪3,990 (about $1,200) on its official site. That's a very different machine, for people with a lot of carpet.

If you're shopping for a spot cleaner here today, check whether the model you're looking at heats the water, or just sprays cold water at a stain. That's the main difference this machine adds, along with the dryer.
