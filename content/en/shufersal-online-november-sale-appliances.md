November is sale season in Israel, and Shufersal Online kicked it off with a "November Sale" promising discounts of up to 60%. The sale covers everything from perfume to furniture, but we were interested in the appliances, so we went through what's on offer and worked out the real discount.

The sale runs until November 30 or while stocks last, and some small-appliance deals continue until December 4. Delivery is charged separately.

## The big brands: 15% to 25% off

Here's what stands out:

- **Dyson V15 Fluffy**: ₪1,699 (about $542), down from ₪1,999. That's 15% off.
- **Ecovacs Deebot X11 Pro** robot vacuum: ₪1,999 (about $637), down from ₪2,499. 20% off.
- **Nespresso Essenza Mini**: ₪299 (about $95), down from ₪399. 25% off.
- **Ninja Crispi Pro XL** air fryer: ₪899 (about $287), down from ₪1,099. 18% off.

Those are reasonable discounts, but a long way from 60%. The deepest cuts in the sale are in other categories, like perfume, and on appliances from lesser-known brands.

## Where the cuts go deeper

The Urevo walking pad drops to ₪999 (about $319) from ₪1,499, and a JVC soundbar to ₪599 (about $191) from ₪899. Both are roughly a third off. Among Bauer's small appliances, an electric kettle is ₪29.90 (about $9.50) instead of ₪79.90, and a toaster is ₪39.90 (about $12.70) instead of ₪79.90. Those are the biggest appliance discounts we found.

## How to tell if it's really a bargain

The "was" price is Shufersal's own regular price, not necessarily the lowest price on the market. Before you order, do three things:

1. **Compare the sale price** with other stores and price-comparison sites, not just with the original price shown next to it.
2. **Check the exact model.** Vacuums and coffee machines come in lots of similarly named versions, and the differences in performance and accessories can be big.
3. **Find out who provides the warranty.** For brands like Dyson and Nespresso, make sure the product comes from the official importer.

One more thing: factor in delivery. On a ₪29.90 kettle it can be a big chunk of the total.
