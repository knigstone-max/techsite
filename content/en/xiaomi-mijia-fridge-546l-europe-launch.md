Xiaomi keeps bringing big appliances to Europe, not just phones and vacuums. At an event in Paris on November 17 it showed four products from Mijia, its smart home brand, none of which had been sold outside China before. We already covered the three-drum washer-dryer in [our 2026 laundry roundup](/techsite/en/posts/washers-dryers-2026-innovations-roundup/). This time the focus is the fridge: the **Mijia Refrigerator Pro Multi-Door 546L**.

## Two layouts, same capacity

It comes in two versions, both in Graphite Grey:

- **French Door** (model MRF55ESGPDAAEU): two doors on top and the freezer at the bottom
- **Cross Door** (model MRC55ESGPDAAEU): doors in a cross layout with 18 compartments, six of them in the freezer

Xiaomi's spec sheets list the same numbers for both: **546 litres** in total, with **351 litres** of fridge and **195 litres** of freezer, an inverter compressor, a dual cooling system and **34dB** of noise. Freezing capacity is 14 kg per day.

![The Xiaomi Mijia Refrigerator Pro Multi-Door 546L French Door with callouts: fridge capacity, freezer capacity and dimensions](img/posts/xiaomi-mijia-fridge-546l-annotated-en.webp "How the space is split, per Xiaomi. Graphic: TechSite")

## Only 60 cm deep

For anyone planning a kitchen, this may be the most interesting figure. It measures **89.2 cm wide, 60 cm deep and 191.2 cm tall**. Heat vents from the bottom, so Xiaomi says 4 mm of clearance on each side and 8 mm on top is enough. The cabinet opening needs to be at least 90 cm wide, 192 cm tall and 60 cm deep.

Put simply, it sits flush with the cabinets instead of sticking out, without paying for a true built-in model. Anyone who has renovated a kitchen knows how annoying a fridge that juts 10 cm past the counter can be.

## An ice maker with an app

The headline feature is an **automatic ice maker**. The bin holds up to 180 cubes on the French Door version and up to 140 on the Cross Door. Fast mode makes a batch in 55 minutes, according to a TÜV Rheinland test at 25°C.

The Xiaomi Home app lets you schedule ice, change temperatures remotely and get alerts when the bin is full or the water runs low. There's also a food inventory with expiry reminders, but you have to type in every item yourself, so it only helps if you keep it up.

## Energy class: E

Xiaomi rates the fridge **Class E** on the European label. On the A-to-G scale that's middle of the pack, not one of the efficient ones. Keep it in mind when comparing large fridges, because a fridge runs 24 hours a day, all year.

## Price and timing

In France:

- **Cross Door**: €1,299.99 (about ₪4,688 or $1,428)
- **French Door**: €1,399.99 (about ₪5,049 or $1,538)

Both arrive in stores **at the end of December**. Anyone who registers on a dedicated page on Xiaomi's site gets a €100 coupon.

## What about Israel?

Hamilton, Xiaomi's Israeli importer, brings in the company's vacuums, including the [Robot Vacuum 6 series](/techsite/en/posts/xiaomi-robot-vacuum-6-series-israel/), but we found no announcement of Mijia fridges or washers for Israel. If this fridge does come, it will join a market that already has plenty of large fridges with ice makers from the established brands. What would set it apart is mainly the 60 cm depth and the app. We'll update if there's news.
