Nobody replaces a washing machine every year, so it's easy to miss how much the category moves. In 2026 it moved quite a bit. Manufacturers stopped competing only on one more kilo of drum and another 100 rpm, and started asking different questions. What do you do with a small load of baby clothes? How do you dry without burning electricity? And how do you fit a machine into an Israeli service balcony without it sticking out?

This is our year-end look at the laundry room. For each idea we've added where things stand in Israel: whether it's in stores, what it costs, and if it isn't here, whether it's worth waiting for.

![Eight washer and dryer innovations from 2026: Xiaomi, Haier, LG, Bosch, Samsung, Beko, Dreame and TCL, with Israeli availability for each](img/posts/laundry-innovations-2026-en.webp "The year in laundry at a glance. Graphic: TechSite")

## 1. More than one drum

The most interesting idea this year was splitting the laundry itself. In November **Xiaomi** launched the Mijia Tri-Drum Washer-Dryer Pro, with a big drum at the bottom and two small ones on top. Each drum has its own water inlet and drain, and all three can wash and dry at the same time. Baby clothes, underwear and the football socks never meet, and you don't run three full-size cycles for three small piles.

[We covered it in detail](/techsite/en/posts/xiaomi-mijia-tri-drum-washer-dryer-pro/) when it launched in Hong Kong. It has since reached Europe: in France the 12 kg version will cost €1,499.99 (about ₪5,364, or $1,645), arriving in January 2027. Xiaomi's own tests say running all three drums at once uses 48% less water and 49% less energy than three back-to-back loads on its previous washer-dryer.

**Haier** went further, at least on the show floor. At IFA in Berlin it showed the Multi Care Laundry Studio, with four drums in one body.

**In Israel:** neither has been announced. The version Xiaomi launched in Hong Kong is 117 cm tall and needs three water inlets and three drains, so even if it comes, it won't suit every service balcony.

## 2. A camera in the drum

Haier's Vision 15 washers have an internal camera called AI Vision Sense. Haier says it recognises load size, garment colours, foam level and even items left behind or stuck in the drum. Nine more sensors measure weight, balance, water level and temperature, and the machine adjusts the cycle from there.

It sounds like a gimmick, but the problem is real. Most people pick one programme and run it on everything. A machine that notices a white shirt among dark jeans could save a few ruined clothes.

**In Israel:** Haier and Candy are sold here, but the Vision models haven't arrived. Of everything Haier showed, they're the closest to a shelf.

## 3. A wider laundry tower

Laundry towers, a washer and dryer in one body, make life easier in Israeli flats with narrow service balconies. This year **LG** added a third WashTower size: a 25-inch (about 63.5 cm) model with 20 kg of washing and 20 kg of heat-pump drying, both running at once. [Full specs here](/techsite/en/posts/lg-washtower-25-inch-israel/).

**In Israel:** no date or price yet. What's sold today is the 60 cm WT40, with a 12 kg washer and a 10 kg dryer. When we checked earlier this month it started at ₪8,427 (about $2,584). Watch the width: a niche built for 60 cm won't take the new one.

## 4. Heat pumps come to washer-dryers

Washer-dryers, which wash and dry in the same drum, have always been a compromise. They dried by condensation and used a lot of power and water. At IFA **Bosch** showed a Series 8 washer-dryer with a heat pump, rated A for both washing and drying according to Bosch. It washes up to 9 kg, and washes and dries up to 6 kg in one go.

**Electrolux** also announced a new 900 series heat-pump washer-dryer, along with 3DSense sensors in its dryers that check moisture deep inside the fabric so duvets and down coats come out fully dry.

**In Israel:** nothing announced yet. But if there's no room for a separate dryer, as in plenty of Israeli flats, this is the one to watch.

## 5. A flat front

In Berlin **Samsung** showed a new line of front-loaders with a fully flat front and a bigger door, so bedding goes in and out more easily. There's also Bubble Shot, which sprays water across the drum to shorten cycles. [We covered it in September](/techsite/en/posts/samsung-ifa-2026-flat-washers/).

**In Israel:** Samsung itself called these development-stage display models, with no sales decision. It's a design direction, not a product to wait for.

## 6. A full load in 40 minutes

**Beko** brought numbers rather than slogans. Its Ultimate40 programme washes a full load in 40 minutes, and Beko says it saves up to 35% in time and energy on daily programmes. The group also owns Whirlpool, Hotpoint, Grundig and Blomberg, so the tech spreads across several brands at once.

**In Israel:** Beko and Whirlpool are in the big chains. When you see Ultimate40 on a price tag, that's the 2026 generation.

## 7. Vacuum brands move into laundry

One of the more interesting local trends this year: brands that made their name on robot vacuums now selling washing machines. **Dreame** launched its L9 WashTower AI set here, an 11 kg washer and a 9 kg heat-pump dryer with double auto-dosing for detergent and softener. [Details here](/techsite/en/posts/dreame-l9-washtower-ai-israel/).

**In Israel:** ₪8,990 (about $2,757) for the set, with a launch offer through the end of November. The open question is long-term reliability and service, since it's the brand's first laundry product here.

## 8. Auto-dosing gets cheaper

Not long ago, a drawer that measures detergent and softener for you was a premium feature. This year it reached the middle of the market. **TCL**, which only started selling large appliances in Israel in September, sells the 12 kg C512FLG with auto-dosing, a direct-drive motor and app control. [We wrote about it here](/techsite/en/posts/tcl-c512flg-washing-machine-israel/).

**In Israel:** ₪2,990 (about $917). It works with liquid detergent only, not powder.

## What to take from all this

- **If your washer works:** no reason to replace it. Most of the big ideas this year aren't here yet.
- **If you're buying now:** auto-dosing and a direct-drive motor have reached sensible prices and are worth a small premium. If you dry a lot, a heat-pump dryer saves electricity over the years.
- **If there's no room for a dryer:** see whether heat-pump washer-dryers reach Israel in 2027.
- **Before you buy anything:** measure the niche. Wider towers and taller machines are this year's direction, and they don't fit everywhere.
