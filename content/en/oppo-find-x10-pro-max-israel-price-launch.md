A week after its global debut in Mallorca, the Find X10 Pro Max has an Israeli price. Ronlight, OPPO's official importer, is selling it for ₪6,499 (about $2,078) in a single 12GB RAM / 512GB storage configuration, in Titanium Grey or Luna White. According to ynet, the regular Find X10 won't be sold here.

## What ₪6,499 gets you

The cameras come first. There are three 200MP rear cameras: an f/1.5 main camera, a telephoto that doubles as a macro camera from 10cm, and a 124° ultra-wide. By default the phone saves 26MP photos, and you can switch to 50MP or 200MP when you want every detail. All three shoot 8K at 30fps, and there's also 4K at 120fps, Dolby Vision and Open Gate for anyone who edits video seriously.

![The back of a white Find X10 Pro Max, with arrows pointing to the three cameras, the orange ring around the module and the two-tone finish](img/posts/oppo-find-x10-pro-max-israel-annotated-en.webp "What's on the back: three 200MP cameras, an orange ring and a two-tone finish")

Beyond that:

- **Display:** 6.78-inch AMOLED at 2772x1272. It refreshes at up to 120Hz, or up to 144Hz in supported games.
- **Chip:** MediaTek's 2nm Dimensity 9600 Pro.
- **Battery:** 8,000mAh, with 80W wired and 50W wireless charging.
- **Durability:** IP68 and IP69.

## The launch bundle

The first 150 buyers get Enco Buds 3 Pro earbuds, a 50W magnetic wireless charger, an aramid fiber case and a two-year importer warranty. If you want the Hasselblad Teleconverter, a clip-on lens that sits over the telephoto camera for extra reach, it's ₪499 (about $160) instead of ₪1,499 (about $479). The phone is sold on the importer's site and through authorized retailers such as KSP, and the importer quotes delivery within 14 business days.

![The Israeli price, the European price, the launch bundle and the main specs of the Find X10 Pro Max](img/posts/oppo-find-x10-pro-max-israel-price-en.webp "Price, freebies and key specs on one card")

## Check the battery

ynet found that the version sold in Europe has a smaller 7,150mAh battery. Ronlight lists 8,000mAh for the local model, so if you're buying from anywhere else, check the box to make sure it's the global version.

## Compared with Europe

ynet puts the European price at around €1,799, which is about ₪6,261 at the official rate. So the Israeli price is very close to Europe's, and here you get the bigger battery too. That puts it up against the most expensive Apple and Samsung models, which is probably where most buyers will be weighing it up.

*Conversions use the Bank of Israel rate for October 28: ₪3.128 per dollar and ₪3.4801 per euro.*
