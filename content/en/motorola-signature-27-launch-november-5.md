Motorola is unveiling the Signature 27 tomorrow, November 5. The company confirmed the date on X last week, after first teasing the phone in September at Qualcomm's Snapdragon Summit. This is the second phone in the Signature line, and it also brings a new naming scheme: the number is simply the last two digits of the year.

Motorola hasn't said a word about price yet. What it has shared is enough to show it's going after the Galaxy and Pixel flagships this time, not the mid-range.

## What Motorola has confirmed

The phone runs on Qualcomm's **Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6**, the top chip in the new lineup. To keep it from throttling, Motorola built a cooling system it calls ArcticMesh. It combines diamond-infused thermal gel, liquid metal, a copper mesh and a 3D vapor chamber, and Motorola says the whole thing is 40% larger than in the previous generation. There are also six antennas, which should help with reception in elevators and car parks.

On the software side, Motorola is promising **7 years of OS upgrades**. That's the same commitment Samsung and Google make on their flagships, and a big step for a brand that used to be stingy with updates. Gemini is built in, including Gemini Omni for video and Lyria for music.

## Cameras and sound

The main camera is a 50MP Sony LYTIA 910, paired with a **200MP periscope** telephoto. Motorola talks about a Video Enhancement Engine and Qualcomm stabilisation with 3.5° of optical image stabilisation, so video is clearly a focus. It's also the first Motorola phone with audio tuned by Bang & Olufsen.

The body is aluminium, and the camera housing mixes ceramic with brushed aluminium. There are two colours, both from Pantone: Coal Smoke with a woven Tactical Weave texture, and the olive Capulet Olive you see in the photo, with a brushed finish that looks like fabric.

## What's still only reported

A hands-on video by YouTuber Danny Winget, covered by 9to5Google, adds more details that Motorola hasn't officially published yet. Take them as likely, not final:

- A 6.8-inch "Extreme AMOLED Duo" display using Tandem OLED, with LTPO, 120Hz and a peak brightness of 12,000 nits
- 16GB of RAM and up to 1TB of UFS 4.1 storage
- Android 17 out of the box
- A 50MP ultrawide on top of the main and periscope cameras, plus a "dedicated action sensor" for motion

The battery size hasn't come up anywhere reliable. Winget only calls it "sizable." We'll know tomorrow.

## What about Israel?

There's no Israeli launch date or price yet. The best hint is last year's model. The first Motorola Signature sells through storemobile, the official importer, with a one-year warranty: 2,790 ₪ (about $884) for 12GB/256GB and 2,990 ₪ (about $947) for 16GB/512GB. GoMobile lists the 16GB/512GB version at 3,199 ₪ (about $1,013).

The Signature 27 has a newer chip, a bigger cooling system and, if the reports are right, a brighter screen, so it's hard to see it coming in cheaper. Our guess is that the Israeli price will start above last year's model. That's a guess, not a leak, and we'll update once there's an official number.

## Is it worth waiting for?

If you're shopping for a flagship right now and Motorola is on your list, waiting a few days costs you nothing. Tomorrow we'll get the full specs and price, and after that the Israeli importer's plans. If the price lands close to last year's, this could be one of the more interesting alternatives to a Galaxy S at the high end.
