Wyze announced the Bulb Cam Pan Color on October 22, as part of its Wyze Week launches. It's a security camera and a color smart light in one body, and it gets its power from an ordinary light socket.

## Installation

You screw it into a standard E26 socket and pair it over Bluetooth in the Wyze app. Wyze says setup takes about a minute, with no batteries, drilling or wiring. A cover for the wall switch comes with it, so nobody turns off the power by accident. A telescopic design lets the lens drop below deep fixtures and lampshades.

## The camera

- 2.5K video with color night vision
- 360-degree pan and 180-degree tilt
- AI tracking that follows people, pets and vehicles, free of charge
- 24/7 recording to a microSD card up to 512GB (card not included)
- Dual-band Wi-Fi 6, plus Alexa, Google Home and IFTTT support

AI notifications and cloud clips need a Cam Plus subscription. For privacy, the lens can rotate into the housing, and audio recording stops while it's tucked away.

## The light

The bulb reaches 1100 lumens, with 16 million colors and white from 1800K to 6500K. Ambient and Motion modes each get their own schedule, color and brightness, so it can glow softly all night and brighten when it detects motion. It can also sync with other Wyze color bulbs.

## Price and availability

The Bulb Cam Pan Color costs $99.98 and went on sale at Wyze.com on October 22. E26 is the North American socket size, so it won't fit the E27 sockets common in Israel without an adapter.
