Sweden's Electrolux Group came to IFA with two brands and two approaches. Electrolux talked about looking after clothes, and AEG tried to surprise.

## Electrolux: less wear on clothes

The company's research found that more than half of Europeans feel their clothes wear out too fast. Its answer:

- **Electrolux 900** with UltraQuick, removing stains at 30°C in a 39-minute cycle, plus PureWash SoftWater to protect colours
- **3DSense** in 800 and 900 series dryers, scanning moisture deep in the fabric so duvets and down coats dry evenly
- **SmartSelect**, which recommends the most efficient settings for each load
- A new 900 series heat-pump washer-dryer, and slim models for small kitchens and balconies

## A dishwasher without menus

Fully integrated 600 to 900 series dishwashers get QuickSelect 2.0: instead of picking a programme, you slide to the time you have, and it shows the effect on energy use. Electrolux says it is the only slider interface on fully integrated dishwashers in Europe.

## AEG: pasta in one pot

The AEG 7000 OnePot Pasta hob is the brand's first with a pasta programme. Choose the portions, put everything in the pot, close the lid, and 15 minutes later you have pasta al dente with sauce. AEG also showed:

- **ErgoMini**, a 0.55kg handheld vacuum
- **Trilobite 8000 GEN2**, a vacuum-mop robot just 7.89cm tall, lower than a drinks can, so it fits under most sofas

## In Israel

Electrolux and AEG are sold in Israel, but no local dates were announced. If you are planning a new kitchen, ask the retailer about OnePot Pasta availability.
