Microsoft has started emailing Xbox owners who haven't turned on their console for a while, suggesting they trade it in at Best Buy. The offer is up to $375 in Best Buy gift cards. The email was first shared on X by the deals account Nintendeal and then reported by Notebookcheck and XDA Developers.

## Who gets it

According to the reports, the email goes to people whose console has been sitting unused, including those who moved to playing on PC or another device. XDA describes it as an offer for the Series X. Notebookcheck says it covers the Series X and Series S.

## How much it is worth

$375 is the maximum, not a fixed amount. Notebookcheck expects the models with the most storage and a disc drive to get the top value, but that is the site's estimate. For comparison, XDA puts the price of a new Series X in the US at $799. Notebookcheck says the offer is about as good as, or better than, what GameStop pays.

## Why now

Microsoft hasn't explained the email. Both sites think the goal is to put more used consoles on store shelves. Memory and storage shortages have pushed up costs, and Microsoft has raised Series X|S prices several times. Notebookcheck points out that when Microsoft announced its June price increase it said it was working with retailers on programs to sell previously played consoles at lower prices.

The offer goes through Best Buy, a US chain. The reports don't say whether anything similar exists in other countries.
