Some phones you buy for the camera. Others you buy for two hours of Genshin without your hand getting cooked. The **REDMAGIC 12 Pro** is the second kind, and proud of it: there's a fan inside, liquid cooling you can see through the clear back, an RGB ring and shoulder triggers. In November it went on sale on REDMAGIC's global store, and it's already showing up in Israel as a parallel import.

## What's inside

- **Chip:** Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, built on a 2 nm process and clocked up to 5GHz, alongside REDMAGIC's RedCore R5 gaming chip and its G1 display chip
- **Screen:** 6.85-inch AMOLED at 2688 by 1216, refreshing at up to 185Hz, with 0.96 mm bezels. No notch and no hole: the 16MP selfie camera sits under the display
- **Battery:** 8,400mAh with up to 80W charging both wired and wireless, plus reverse charging for accessories. There's also Charge Separation, which REDMAGIC says lets you play while plugged in without wearing out the battery or overheating
- **Cooling:** AquaCore Cooling 3.0, a dual-loop liquid system, a 25,500 rpm fan rated IPX8, a vapour chamber and liquid metal
- **Cameras:** a 50MP main camera with optical stabilisation and a 50MP ultrawide
- **Protection:** IP68 and IP69, per REDMAGIC
- **Also:** Wi-Fi 7, NFC, an ultrasonic fingerprint reader, USB-C with display out, 520Hz shoulder triggers, and REDMAGIC OS 12 on Android 17

According to the Israeli parallel importer, it weighs 223 g and is 8.55 mm thick. That's heavy, and it's the price of the big battery and the fan.

## A remote for the AC, in your pocket

One small detail that matters more here than you'd think: it has an infrared blaster, which REDMAGIC pitches for controlling devices around the home. So you can run the AC, the TV or the fan from your phone when the remote has vanished into the sofa. Apple never had one and Samsung dropped it years ago, so for anyone after one phone for gaming and the house, it's a genuine plus.

## What it costs

![REDMAGIC 12 Pro prices: global store vs parallel import in Israel across three memory versions, plus screen, battery, cooling, chip, protection and IR blaster](img/posts/redmagic-12-pro-prices-en.webp "Two ways to buy. Graphic: TechSite")

On REDMAGIC's global store, which is the store the site lists for Israel:

- 12GB and 256GB: $899 (about ₪3,005)
- 16GB and 512GB: $999 (about ₪3,340)
- 16GB and 1TB: $1,199 (about ₪4,008)

The black translucent Nightfreeze comes in all three. The silver Subzero only comes in the 512GB and 1TB versions.

At iTech Mobile, which sells it as a parallel import with a 12-month store warranty, prices are ₪4,899 (about $1,465), ₪5,399 (about $1,615) and ₪6,199 (about $1,854). We found no official importer here.

That's a big gap: about ₪1,900 on the base model. Part of it is VAT, shipping, and a local warranty you can reach without posting a phone abroad. Even so, ordering from the global store will probably work out cheaper. Global store prices don't include shipping, and you may owe VAT and import charges when it lands, so check the final total before you pay.

## Before you order

- **No eSIM.** According to iTech Mobile's product page, it has two Nano SIM slots and no eSIM support. If you've moved to eSIM, that changes things
- **No charger in the box,** per the same shop. You'll need to buy an 80W charger separately
- **Warranty:** REDMAGIC's global store offers 15-day returns. A fault after that means shipping abroad. With the parallel import, the warranty is the shop's, not the maker's

## Who it's for

People who game a lot on their phone and care more about steady performance over long sessions than a thin body and a top camera. If you want a good all-round phone, this money buys a better camera and an official importer's warranty. But if liquid cooling and a 185Hz screen are what you're after, there aren't many alternatives you can buy in Israel.
