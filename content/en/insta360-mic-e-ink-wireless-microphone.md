Insta360 launched the Insta360 Mic worldwide on October 22. It's a wireless microphone for video creators and fills the middle of the company's lineup, between the entry-level Mic Air and the flagship Mic Pro.

## The screen

The clip-on transmitter has an E-Ink display you can personalize with a logo, an emoji, a pet photo or anything else. Insta360 says it's the company's first customizable E-Ink mic with expandable storage.

## Recording

- A microSD slot for optional internal recording, in 32-bit float when a card is inserted
- Without a card, it still records through Bluetooth or the receiver
- AI noise canceling handled by a dedicated audio chip
- One receiver works with up to four transmitters, including Mic Pro transmitters

## Connections and battery

The receiver plugs into phones, DSLRs and mirrorless cameras through the included USB-C phone adapter or a 3.5mm cable. Six Insta360 cameras connect to the transmitter directly over Bluetooth with no receiver: X6, X5, Luna Ultra, Luna Pro, GO Ultra and Ace Pro 2. The X6 and Luna can take two transmitters at once.

Each transmitter runs up to 13 hours, or up to 34 hours with the charging case.

## Prices

- Two transmitters and one receiver: $249.99
- One transmitter and one receiver: $139.99
- A single transmitter: $79.99

It's sold through the Insta360 online store, Amazon and authorized retailers.
