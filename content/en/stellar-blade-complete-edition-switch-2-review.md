Stellar Blade waited two years to reach Nintendo, and it arrives the way a late-platform launch should: Complete Edition, everything released so far, on a full cartridge, no Game-Key Card. The global launch was November 5, 2026 at 17:00 Israel time. MSRP is $59.99 (about ₪190 at the representative rate). In Israeli shops I've seen ₪299 at MediaGame and ₪309 at Player1, down from a ₪329 list.

If you missed it on PS5 in April 2024 or on PC in June 2025, this is a rare chance to get one of the generation's sharper action games on a handheld. If you already finished it, what's new is mainly portability and outfits, not a new story.

![Stellar Blade Switch 2 facts: Complete Edition, free Bayonetta gear, full cartridge and Israel prices](img/posts/stellar-blade-switch-2-facts-en.webp "What's included and what it costs. Graphic: TechSite")

## Stellar Blade in brief

You play Eve of the 7th Airborne Squad, sent from a space colony back to a ruined Earth. Creatures called Naytiba have taken the planet, and the mission looks simple until it isn't. In between you get a wrecked city, semi-open areas and big bosses that demand pattern learning.

Combat is timing-first: attack, dodge, and a precise parry that opens a punish window. It isn't a Soulslike that crushes you in every hallway, but it also doesn't play itself. If you like fights that feel like a sharp dance, you'll feel at home. If you want calm Zelda-style exploration, less so.

## What's in the Complete Edition

The Switch 2 version bundles the full game with the DLC packs already out: the NieR:Automata collab (outfits for Eve, Lily and Adam, Emil's shop and more) and the GODDESS OF VICTORY: NIKKE pack (boss, outfit, mini-games). On top sit Complete Edition bonuses such as the Stargazer set, extra cosmetics, 2,000 SP EXP and 5,000 Gold.

Launch day also brings four Bayonetta-inspired outfits for Eve: Bayonetta, Jeanne, Nun and Black Tresses. Those are free on PS5 and PC too, not just Switch 2. Pre-orders unlocked early access to a white diving suit, round glasses and earrings. A free eShop demo covers the opening so you can try the combat before you pay.

## Why the full cartridge matters

Switch 2 has seen too many Game-Key Card releases: buy a box, then download the whole game. Here SHIFT UP put the full game on a 32GB cartridge. You can start without a mandatory download, which matters on a flight, on the train, or when home internet is down. There's also a limited SteelBook at $69.99 through participating retailers and the Panax store.

SHIFT UP is publishing this version itself. On PS5 and PC that role belonged to Sony. The rating is PEGI 18 / ESRB Mature, mainly for violence and character design aimed at adults. If the console is shared with kids, take note.

## Performance, Joy-Con 2 and what's still unclear

The game supports TV, tabletop and handheld play. SHIFT UP calls out Joy-Con 2 motion support for non-combat activities. What still isn't published in dry numbers: resolution and frame rate in handheld versus docked. Until stable measurements land, I treat that as a question mark, not a promise.

On PS5 the game ran sharp and steady. On Switch 2 the reasonable expectation is some lighting and density trade-offs while keeping combat feel intact. If timing breaks because of frame dips on bosses, that would be a problem. In the first days after launch, watch for a day-one patch.

## Israel pricing: is it worth it

$59.99 is full price for a game that already includes its DLC, which is generous in a generation that likes selling expansions separately. Locally, ₪299–309 for a physical copy is about what you expect from a new Switch 2 title on launch promo. If you already own it on PS5 or PC and only want the Bayonetta outfits, those arrive free without buying again.

If you don't have another console, and you like sharp action with big bosses, this is one of the clearer Switch 2 recommendations this quarter. The free demo helps you decide without gambling the full amount.

## Bottom line

8/10

Stellar Blade Complete Edition on Switch 2 does the late launch right: all the content at one price, a full cartridge, and Bayonetta outfits even if you stay on PS5. It loses a point for still-unpublished handheld performance numbers, and for offering mainly portability rather than a new story to people who already finished it. If you haven't played it yet, it's one of the stronger Switch 2 buys this year.

**What I liked**

- A real Complete Edition with NieR and NIKKE included
- Full cartridge, not a Game-Key Card
- Free Bayonetta outfits across platforms
- Fair Israel launch pricing around ₪299–309
- Free eShop demo before you buy
- Timing-based combat that still feels sharp years on

**What I liked less**

- No official handheld-vs-TV performance figures yet
- No new story content for PS5/PC veterans
- Mature rating limits shared-family play
- SteelBook sold separately at a higher price
- Possible dependence on a day-one patch for stability
