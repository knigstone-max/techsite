Open-ear earbuds have one well-known weakness. They're great for running outside or taking calls at the office, but on a bus or a plane you can barely hear a thing. Anker's soundcore AeroFit 2 Pro takes a fairly simple approach to fixing that. The hook around your ear has five positions. On the low settings the bud sits outside your ear canal. On the high ones it moves slightly inward and noise cancelling switches on.

The earbuds reached Israel about ten days ago. KSP, the official importer, sells them for ₪730 (about $234) with a one-year warranty, in black, white, blue and purple. On soundcore's US store the list price is $179.99 (around ₪561), so Israeli buyers pay a fair bit more. They also picked up a CES 2026 Innovation Award.

![How the AeroFit 2 Pro modes map to the hook positions, battery figures, and a price comparison in shekels and dollars against the regular model](img/posts/soundcore-aerofit-2-pro-modes-en.webp "The two modes, and what the earbuds cost in Israel")

## How it works

- **Positions 1 and 2:** open-ear. Your ear canal stays clear, so you hear traffic, a horn behind you or someone talking to you.
- **Positions 4 and 5:** what Anker calls Semi-in-Ear. The bud presses against the opening of your ear and Adaptive ANC 3.0 kicks in. Anker says it adjusts up to 180 times a minute to match the noise around you. Both hooks need to be at 4 or 5, or noise cancelling won't run.

There's a transparency mode too, if you want to hear what's around you while they're snug. One caveat: Anker's fit chart says your ear needs to measure more than 16 mm between its two reference points for the snug mode to sit right. If your ears are on the small side, measure first.

## Battery

In open mode Anker quotes up to 7 hours, or 34 with the case. With ANC on that drops to 5 hours, or 24 with the case. Turn on LDAC, the high-quality codec, and you get about 6 hours open and about 3 with ANC. Calls last about 4 hours. Ten minutes of charging gets you roughly 3.5 hours, and a full charge takes about an hour. The case charges over USB-C or wirelessly.

## What else is inside

- 11.8 mm drivers, Hi-Res and LDAC support (Android only, iPhones don't support LDAC)
- Spatial audio with head tracking
- Four mics with AI noise reduction for calls
- Bluetooth 6.1 with multipoint for two devices at once
- IP55 on the earbuds, not the case
- 10.4 g per earbud, 88 g for the case
- Translation in 100 languages through the soundcore app, Hebrew included

## Against the regular model and the competition

The regular AeroFit 2 costs ₪380 at KSP and ₪470 at Bug (about $122–151). It has no ANC, but the battery lasts longer: 10 hours, or 42 with the case. If you only wear open-ear buds for runs and walks, it's all you need.

Around the Pro's price there are a few other options. The Shokz OpenFit 2, also open-ear, goes for about ₪600 at Bug (about $192). Samsung's Galaxy Buds 4 (about ₪550 at Bug) and AirPods 5 (about ₪510–590) are regular in-ear buds. None of them offers this particular combination: open-ear by default, sealed with ANC when you need it.

## Bottom line

If you like open-ear buds but gave up on them on the train, the AeroFit 2 Pro is the first pair that seriously tries to do both. The Israeli price is well above the US one, so wait for a sale or shop around before you buy.
