After years of waiting and two delays, there's a date that looks final: GTA VI launches November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Pre-orders have been open since June 25, and there are about three weeks left. If you haven't ordered yet, here's what to know before you pay.

## What it costs

In the US, the standard edition is $79.99 (about ₪250) before tax, and there's an Ultimate Edition at $99.99 (about ₪313). In Israel the digital price is ₪319 (about $102) on both PS Store and Microsoft Store, the same as FC 27 here.

![Price table for GTA VI in the digital stores, at Ivory and at Bug, plus the pre-order, pre-load and launch dates](img/posts/gta-6-israel-prices-en.webp "Where pre-ordering is cheapest, and when everything happens")

If you'd rather have a boxed copy:

- **Ivory**: ₪345 (about $110), or ₪292 (about $93) at the VAT-free Eilat branch. Shipping starts November 19.
- **Bug**: ₪349 (about $112), on both PS5 and Xbox.

So digital is ₪26–30 cheaper than the box. On the other hand, a physical copy can be resold once you're done.

## The box is really a code

Collectors, take note: According to the published details, the physical copies going out from November 12 contain a download code rather than the full game on disc. If you were buying the box to avoid a huge download, know that going in.

## The bonuses

- **Vintage Vice City Pack** for purchases made before November 20. On Xbox the cutoff is November 19 at 23:59:59.
- **One month of GTA+** with digital pre-orders.
- **Pre-load** from November 12, so you won't be stuck waiting on a download on launch night.

## Worth knowing

The game is rated PEGI 18, so it's not one for the kids. There's no PC version at launch, and Rockstar hasn't announced a date for one. If you own a PS5 or Xbox Series console and you're buying anyway, the digital pre-order is the cheapest and simplest route. Heading to Eilat in the next few weeks? ₪292 at Ivory there is the lowest price we found.
