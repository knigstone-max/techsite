iPhone owners have had the AirTag for five years. Android users had trackers from Samsung, Chipolo and others, but nothing from Google itself. As of November 11 that's changed: the Pixel Tag, Google's first tracker, costs $29.99 (about ₪96) for one or $99.99 (about ₪319) for a four-pack.

## What it is

It's a small, flat tag for your keys, wallet or suitcase. It pairs with the Find Hub app, where you can see it on a map and make it beep. When it's out of range, the Find Hub network, which Google puts at more than a billion Android devices, helps locate it. Google says location data on the network is end-to-end encrypted.

A few details from the official spec page:

- 46.1 by 28 mm, and 5.4 mm thick
- 11.8 g with the battery
- IP67, up to 1 metre of water for 30 minutes
- A CR2032 coin cell you replace yourself, rated for over a year
- A built-in speaker, plus a button that rings your phone, so it works in reverse too
- One colour, a light grey Google calls Fog

You can share a tag with up to 10 people, which is handy for the family car keys. There are left-behind alerts, and your phone warns you if an unknown tag is travelling with you.

## Not every Android phone gets everything

This is the part to read carefully. Pairing, the map and the beep work on any phone running Android 9 or later. Precision Finding, though, with the on-screen arrow showing direction and distance when your keys are under a sofa cushion, needs a phone with an Ultra Wideband chip. Most budget phones don't have one.

There's a second way to measure distance, Bluetooth Channel Sounding, which Google says needs Android 16 or later and Bluetooth 6.0. So a recent Pixel or Galaxy gets the full package. A three-year-old mid-ranger mostly gets the map and the beep.

And for iPhone owners: the Pixel Tag doesn't support iPhones at all. Stick with the AirTag.

## What about Israel

Google doesn't sell the Pixel Tag in Israel, and Israel isn't on Google's list of warranty countries for it. Even the Pixel 11 Pro phones Electra sells here come in without Google acting as the official importer. If you want a Pixel Tag, you'll either wait for someone to bring it in or order it from abroad.

The good news is that at this price, a personal order of one tag or even a four-pack stays under the $75 threshold, so there's no VAT to pay. Just make sure the seller actually ships to Israel, and keep in mind that if it breaks, there's no one here to swap it.

## Is it worth it?

If you have a recent Android phone with UWB and want a tracker that plugs straight into the system without another app, the Pixel Tag is the obvious pick. The replaceable battery is a real advantage over tags you throw away after a year. If your phone is older, any other Find Hub-compatible tag will do almost the same job, sometimes for less.
