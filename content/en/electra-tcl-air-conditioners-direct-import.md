If you bought a TCL air conditioner in Israel in recent years, it came through official importer SG, whichever chain you walked into. That is about to change. According to Calcalist and Yedioth Ahronoth, Electra Consumer Products agreed with TCL in recent weeks to buy air conditioners directly from the manufacturer and sell them in its own chains, without SG in the middle.

This is not a first. Back in 2022 Electra persuaded TCL to sell it televisions directly in the same way. Now the air conditioners follow.

## Where they will be sold

The units will land in Electra's retail chains: Machsanei Hashmal, Shekem Electric and Shekem Duty Free. That is 98 branches in total, and the group says they hold about 30% of Israel's electrical appliance market. SG will keep distributing TCL to the rest of the market, so the brand will effectively have two parallel channels here.

No models, prices or warranty and service terms have been published yet for the units coming through Electra. When they are, check who actually provides service. With an air conditioner, installation and the technician matter almost as much as the hardware.

![Electra's direct import figures: 57% in 2024, 65% in 2025 and about 70% today, plus market share by category](img/posts/electra-direct-import-numbers-en.webp "The numbers Electra is putting forward. Graphic: TechSite")

## Why Electra is doing this

Short version: margins. With no importer in between, more money stays with the retailer, and sometimes there is room to cut prices. By the company's figures, direct imports now make up about 70% of white goods revenue in its chains, up from 65% in 2025 and 57% in 2024.

At the same time, Electra keeps pushing its house brand. According to data it presented, based on research by an international market-research firm, the Electra brand led unit sales in four categories from January to August 2026: fridges at 22% (ahead of Sharp at 14% and Samsung at 9.7%), freezers at 33%, washing machines at 24.4% and dryers at 18%. In dishwashers it is second with 12%. These are company figures, not an independent audit.

## What it means if you are shopping

Probably not much right away. Winter is here, and real air-conditioner season starts in spring. But if the TV experience is any guide, a second import channel can push TCL prices down, and indirectly the competition's too. On the other hand, putting one more brand under a group that already controls a large share of retail is not necessarily good for competition over time.

For now, if you are eyeing a TCL unit for next summer, it is worth waiting to compare prices across both channels before you buy.
