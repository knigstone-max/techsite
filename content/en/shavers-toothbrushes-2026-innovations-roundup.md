Our year-end roundups by category continue. After [fridges](/techsite/en/posts/fridges-2026-innovations-roundup/), [air conditioners](/techsite/en/posts/air-conditioners-2026-innovations-roundup/), [dishwashers](/techsite/en/posts/dishwashers-2026-innovations-roundup/) and [hair care](/techsite/en/posts/hair-care-2026-innovations-roundup/), it's the turn of the small gadgets in the bathroom cabinet: electric shavers and toothbrushes.

This isn't a category that reinvents itself every year, which is exactly why 2026 stands out. The three big shaver makers, Philips, Braun and Panasonic, all launched new flagships at almost the same time, and in toothbrushes the sensors moved from the app onto the handle itself.

## 1. Braun NEVO: a new series, the first in about 12 years

Most years Braun refreshes Series 9. This year it launched an entirely new line called **NEVO**, its first new series in about 12 years according to Japanese grooming magazine OTOKOMAE. The big idea is less friction: a foil with a finish inspired by shark skin, which Braun says, for the Japanese model, cuts skin friction by 24% compared with the previous Series 9 Pro+.

The rest: a cutting system with 250 inner blades, a head that swings 40 degrees forwards and back, a one-piece stainless steel body and a "7-in-1" station that cleans, maintains, dries and charges.

**In Israel:** yes. [KSP put model 15010C in its November sale](/techsite/en/posts/braun-nevo-ksp-1889/) at **₪1,889 (about $574)** instead of ₪2,499 (about $759), with a two-year official importer warranty. The chain says the deal runs until November 30 or while stock lasts.

## 2. Philips i9000 Prestige Ultra: a shaver that's also a grooming kit

Philips went the other way. Rather than focusing on the shave alone, the **XP9406/40** released in June turns its rotary shaver into the base of a kit: click-on heads for body grooming, nose and ear hair, beard styling from 0.5 to 5 mm, and a facial massager.

For the shave itself, a **SkinIQ Pro** sensor checks beard density 500 times a second and adjusts power, and lights tell you if you're pressing too hard and whether your circular motion is right. That's genuinely useful if you're switching from foil to rotary and aren't sure how to hold it.

**In Israel:** partly. The i9000 Prestige Ultra family is sold here, and model XP9402/31 starts today on Zap at **₪1,591 (about $483)** and runs up to ₪2,388 (about $725). We couldn't find the XP9406 kit with all the new heads.

## 3. Panasonic: six blades, twice

Panasonic made two moves this year. The first has already arrived: the **Arc6**, the first six-blade foil shaver, [which we reviewed in November](/techsite/en/posts/panasonic-arc6-es-ls9a-review-israel/). At the time, the ES-LS9A version with the cleaning station was selling at iCell for **₪2,479 (about $753)** on promotion, through official importer Elias.

The second is staying in Japan, at least for now: the **Palm-In PRO 6-Blade ES-P690U**, which packs the six-blade system into a body 5.9 cm tall and about 180 g. You hold it in your palm right next to the blades and steer the angle with your fingers. Panasonic says it cuts long stubble twice as efficiently as the five-blade model in the same line. It's aimed at frequent flyers who want a full-power shaver in a pocket.

## 4. Philips Sonicare 9900 Prestige: a mouth map on the handle

The most interesting bathroom idea this year has nothing to do with shaving. Philips' new **Sonicare DiamondClean 9900 Prestige** is the brand's first toothbrush with AI running on the device itself.

Instead of opening an app after you brush, the feedback appears on the handle in real time: a **12-zone mouth map** that shows what you missed, and a light ring that flags coverage, pressure or over-scrubbing. The pressure sensor dials the intensity down by itself if you press too hard. There are 8 modes and 3 intensities, and the battery is designed to be replaceable.

**In Israel:** not yet. Philips says it reaches Europe and the US this autumn, with more countries later in the year and through 2027. If you want a brush with brushing feedback right now, you'll have to settle for older models or a newcomer.

## 5. Dreame moves into the bathroom

That newcomer is Dreame. The brand best known here for robot vacuums and hair dryers [launched two electric toothbrushes in Israel in November](/techsite/en/posts/dreame-e10f-nb01-smart-toothbrushes-israel/): the **E10F** with a screen on the handle and brushing tracking at **₪449 (about $136)** list, and the simpler **NB01** at **₪349 (about $106)**, with a pressure indicator and up to 40 days of battery.

It's part of a wider trend we also saw in our [hair care roundup](/techsite/en/posts/hair-care-2026-innovations-roundup/): Chinese vacuum brands moving into Philips and Braun territory with lower prices and fewer years of track record.

## What to take from this when buying

- **Shavers:** if you shave daily and have sensitive skin, the NEVO on promotion is currently the most interesting flagship price in Israel. If your beard grows in several directions, especially on the neck, a rotary like the i9000 will suit you better. If you shave twice a week, you don't need a ₪2,000 (about $607) shaver at all.
- **Toothbrushes:** a pressure sensor matters more than an app. You can wait for the 9900 Prestige, but it'll take a while, and check what replacement heads cost before you fall for the handle.
- **Official import:** both categories are full of grey imports. With a battery that isn't always replaceable, a two-year importer warranty is worth a few dozen shekels.
