Apple rarely cuts the price of a model that has not been replaced yet. On 20 August, almost a month before the new generation, it took $90 off the iPhone 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max. In Israel that became ₪320 (about $104), and about ₪270 in VAT-free Eilat.

## New prices at Machsanei Hashmal

**iPhone 17 Pro Max**
- 256GB: ₪4,999 → ₪4,679 (about $1,520)
- 512GB: ₪5,859 → ₪5,539 (about $1,800)
- 1TB: ₪6,649 → ₪6,329 (about $2,055)
- 2TB: ₪8,299 → ₪7,979 (about $2,590)

**iPhone 17 Pro**
- 256GB: ₪4,599 → ₪4,279 (about $1,390)
- 512GB: ₪5,439 → ₪5,119 (about $1,660)
- 1TB: ₪6,249 → ₪5,929 (about $1,925)

KSP, iDigital and iStore followed, with small differences.

## Why now

Walla and Geektime tied the move to "RAMmageddon", the memory shortage pushing up component costs across the industry. Apple needs to clear stock before the 18 Pro while keeping a price that looks reasonable next to it.

## Buy now or wait?

If the 17 Pro's camera and battery are enough for you, the gap to the 18 Pro at launch (from ₪5,029 for 256GB) is about ₪750 (about $245). That is a lot to pay for a faster chip and a mechanical aperture. If your phone is two or more years old and you want a good deal, this is a reasonable time to buy. Just make sure it is official import with at least a one-year warranty.

*Dollar figures are our conversion at the Bank of Israel representative rate of about ₪3.08 per $1 (26.10.2026), rounded.*
