TCL brought three Mini LED lines to Israel this fall. The Q7D PRO is the cheapest of them and, on paper, the brightest. It starts at ₪3,990 (about $1,281) for the 55-inch, a price that usually buys a plain LED set with basic backlighting. I went through the full spec sheet and the local extras, and here's where I landed.

## SQD-Mini LED in plain terms

Behind the screen sit thousands of tiny blue LEDs grouped into dimming zones. Each zone brightens or dims with the picture, so blacks stay black while an explosion in one corner glows. A Super Quantum Dot layer on top turns that blue light into color. The panel itself is an HVA 2.0 Pro type, designed for high contrast.

The thing to understand is that the zone count grows with screen size, but more slowly than the screen does.

![TCL Q7D PRO table showing dimming zones and brightness for each size from 55 to 98 inches, plus price, refresh rate and audio](img/posts/tcl-q7d-pro-sizes-zones-en.webp "How many dimming zones each size gets")

The 55-inch has 480 zones, which is reasonable for that size. The 98-inch has 1,152, spread over roughly three times the area, so each zone covers more of the picture. White subtitles on a black movie scene are where you might see a faint halo. For value, I think the 55 and 65-inch models are the sweet spot.

## Brightness and HDR

TCL Israel quotes up to 2,200 nits for every size except the 98-inch, which tops out at 2,000. TCL's global product page says 2,000-nit HDR, so I'd treat 2,200 as a best-case peak, not something you'll see every evening. Even 2,000 nits is plenty for a bright Israeli living room with a big window.

Every format that matters is covered: Dolby Vision IQ, HDR10+, HLG and IMAX Enhanced. The IQ part adjusts the picture to the room's lighting, which helps if the TV gets used at midday and late at night.

## Gaming is its strong suit

- 4K at 144Hz, and up to 288Hz in DLG mode (at a lower resolution)
- VRR up to 144Hz plus FreeSync Premium Pro
- ALLM, which switches to game mode automatically
- Two of the four HDMI ports are HDMI 2.1

That's more than enough for a PS5 or Xbox Series X. If you hook up a gaming PC, 144Hz is a real perk at this price.

![TCL Game Bar overlay on a game scene: crosshair, magnifier, shadow enhancement and an info bar showing FPS, HDR and VRR](img/posts/tcl-q7d-pro-game-bar.webp "The Game Bar menu shows frame rate and gaming aids. Image: TCL")

One thing to plan around: only two ports are HDMI 2.1, and one of them doubles as the eARC port for a soundbar. With a console, a PC and a soundbar, you'll need to think about what goes where.

## Software and Israeli apps

It runs Google TV, and the key detail for Israel is the app list: yes+, Partner TV, HOT and Cellcom TV. In most homes that means no set-top box. iPhone users get AirPlay 2 and HomeKit, and there's built-in Chromecast and Miracast too.

## Sound

The audio is an ONKYO 2.1 system with 40W, a built-in subwoofer, Dolby Atmos and DTS Virtual:X. On paper that beats the thin speakers in most TVs. Still, if you go for a 75-inch or larger in a big room, budget for a soundbar later.

## Ports

- 4 HDMI, two of them 2.1, one with eARC
- Wi-Fi 5 and Bluetooth 5.4
- 2 USB ports and an optical output

Wi-Fi 5 rather than 6 is a letdown in 2026. If your router is far away, run a network cable.

## The pricier siblings

At the same launch TCL introduced the Q7D RGB through Sidata and the RM7L RGB, the same hardware sold through Electra. They use red, green and blue LEDs instead of blue only, with 1,344 to 2,880 zones and 100% BT.2020 coverage. They start at ₪4,990 (about $1,602), but only from 65 inches. If you want a 55-inch, the Q7D PRO is your pick.

## The bottom line

8/10

On paper the Q7D PRO is one of the best-value TVs in Israel this year: bright Mini LED, 144Hz, every HDR format and apps from all the local TV providers. At 55 and 65 inches it looks like a great deal. In the bigger sizes, zone density drops, so see it in a store before you buy.
:::

**What I loved**

- A ₪3,990 starting price for Mini LED with up to 2,200 nits
- 144Hz, VRR and FreeSync Premium Pro at this price
- Google TV with yes+, HOT, Partner TV and Cellcom TV
- Both Dolby Vision IQ and HDR10+
- ONKYO 2.1 sound with a built-in sub

**What I liked less**

- In the larger sizes each dimming zone covers more screen
- Only two HDMI 2.1 ports, and one is also the eARC port
- Wi-Fi 5 only
- The gap between the 2,200 nits quoted locally and 2,000 on the global page
