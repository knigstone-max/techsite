For the first time, a new iPhone reached Israel on the same day as the US and Europe. Deliveries began on 18 September, and instead of a second-wave wait, retailers published price lists during launch week.

## Launch prices (256GB / 512GB / 1TB / 2TB)

From the lists published by Channel 14 on 10.9:

- **iStore**, Pro: ₪5,399 / 6,299 / 7,999 / 10,699. Pro Max: ₪5,899 / 6,699 / 8,499 / 10,999
- **Pelephone**, Pro: ₪5,399 / 6,399 / 7,699 / 9,499. Pro Max: ₪5,999 / 6,999 / 8,099 / 9,999
- **Machsanei Hashmal**, Pro: ₪5,299 / 6,199 / 7,899 / 10,599. Pro Max: ₪5,799 / 6,599 / 8,399 / 10,899

Within days prices moved. Gadgety reported on 18.9 that Machsanei Hashmal cut the 256GB Pro to ₪5,029 (about $1,630), the Pro Max to ₪5,679 (about $1,845) and the 2TB model to ₪10,579 (about $3,435). A benefits pack costs ₪349 (about $113) extra.

In the US Apple asks $1,199 for the Pro and $1,299 for the Pro Max before sales tax. Most of the gap is Israel's 18% VAT.

## What is new

- A20 Pro chip on a 2nm process
- A vapor chamber three times larger, and Apple quotes up to 40% faster performance
- 48MP main camera with a mechanical aperture from f/1.48 to f/4.0
- 5,391mAh battery in the Pro Max and 4,056mAh in the Pro
- Black, silver, Glacier and burgundy. iOS 27

Apple Intelligence still does not speak Hebrew, so anyone buying for the AI features will be using it in English.

## Trade-in and iPhone Duo

Partner said it would match KSP prices, and trade-in offers go up to ₪4,300 (about $1,395). The foldable iPhone Duo opened for preorder on 16.10 and launched on 23.10; at Pelephone it starts at ₪8,199 (about $2,660).

## Where to buy

The gap between the cheapest and priciest seller for the same storage is a few hundred shekels. Before you pay, check that it is official import, who gives the warranty, and whether the price depends on buying an accessory or joining a plan.

*Dollar figures are our conversion at the Bank of Israel representative rate of about ₪3.08 per $1 (26.10.2026), rounded.*
