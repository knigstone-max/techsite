In Israel, Haier mostly means fridges, washing machines and air conditioners. In Europe it's now moving into floorcare. After showing the range at IFA, it started selling three new cleaning machines in the UK, T3 reported on November 9. The interesting one is the **I-Pro Clean Z5**, a cordless hard-floor wet-dry vacuum built a little differently from most of what's sold here.

## Two rollers instead of one

Most of the wet-dry vacuums we've reviewed have a single roller spinning one way. The Z5 has **two motorised rollers turning in opposite directions**. Haier says that lets it clean on both the forward push and the backward pull, so you don't have to go over the same stain again and again.

The rest is familiar from the category:

- **25,000Pa** of suction, measured at the motor inlet according to Haier.
- A **dirt sensor** that raises the water flow by itself on dirtier floors.
- A **foam trigger** that sprays detergent foam straight onto greasy or sticky marks.
- Edge cleaning on **all four sides** of the head, plus LED headlights.
- Lies flat to **180 degrees** under sofas, with water still flowing while it's down.
- A **1-litre** clean tank and a 0.75-litre dirty tank.
- **Up to 50 minutes** of runtime (in the middle setting) and up to 400 m² per charge by Haier's calculation. A full charge takes four hours.
- 5.9 kg, 113.6 cm tall, self-propelled, with voice prompts.

## Five-minute drying

Back on its base, it washes the rollers in **75°C** water and dries them with **85°C** air. Haier says the whole cycle takes **five minutes**. That's where the whole category is heading this year: a dry roller is a roller that doesn't smell. [Here's why that matters so much](/techsite/en/posts/wet-dry-vacuum-care-guide-smell/).

## What it doesn't have

**No steam.** While Roborock, Dreame, Tineco and Xiaomi all sell steam models in Israel, Haier stuck with hot water in the dock. **No app**: the spec sheet lists no connectivity, just an LED display. The voice prompts come in English, French, German, Spanish and Italian. Haier also says it isn't suitable for carpets.

## What it costs

Haier's UK store lists it today at **£339.99 (about ₪1,470, $446)**, down from £439.99 (about ₪1,902, $578). T3 quoted a lower £239 when the range went on sale, so the UK price is clearly moving with promotions.

## And in Israel?

We couldn't find the Z5 at any Israeli retailer, and we haven't seen a local launch announcement. Haier's large appliances are already sold here, so the floorcare line could follow. We'll update if it does.

Meanwhile, here's what's already on sale in the same territory:

- **[Xiaomi Wet Dry Vacuum 5 Pro](/techsite/en/posts/xiaomi-wet-dry-vacuum-5-pro-review-israel/)** at ₪2,490 (about $756) from Hamilton: 26,000Pa, 160°C steam, up to 40 minutes.
- **[Roborock F25 Pro Turbo](/techsite/en/posts/roborock-f25-pro-turbo-review-israel/)**, launched at ₪2,190 (about $665) and now selling for around ₪1,529 to ₪1,690 (about $464 to $513): 25,000Pa, one roller, no steam.

If the Z5 reaches Israel close to a straight conversion of the UK price, its real rival is the F25 Pro Turbo: same suction, no steam, but two rollers and foam for stains. If it lands at ₪2,500 (about $759), the Xiaomi with steam looks like better value.
