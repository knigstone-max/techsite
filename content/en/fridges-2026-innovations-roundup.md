After the [laundry room](/techsite/en/posts/washers-dryers-2026-innovations-roundup/) and [floor cleaning](/techsite/en/posts/floor-cleaning-2026-innovations-roundup/), it's time for the appliance that runs around the clock. Fridges don't change fast. Most people replace one about once a decade, and manufacturers know it. Still, a few ideas showed up at almost every brand in 2026, from the Shanghai show in March to IFA in Berlin in September.

Here's what changed, what's actually useful, and what you can already buy in Israel.

![Six fridge trends of 2026: camera food recognition, plain-language voice, pre-cooling, flush installation, switchable compartments and freshness sensors](img/posts/fridges-2026-trends-en.webp "Ideas that came up at several brands this year. Graphic: TechSite")

## 1. The fridge sees what's inside

The biggest theme of the year. Instead of you typing in what you bought, cameras inside the fridge identify it.

- **Samsung** pushed a software update in May to US Bespoke AI Family Hub fridges with the 32-inch screen. Recognition, built with Google's Gemini, now goes well beyond the 40 items the fridge knew out of the box, including packaged goods read from the label. It also flags when something you buy often is running low. Two things from the fine print: it doesn't work in the freezer, and you may need to fix the list by hand. More markets get the update in stages through 2026.
- **Hisense** put three cameras in the [PureSmart Duo](/techsite/en/posts/hisense-puresmart-duo-21-inch-screen-fridge/), the 21-inch-screen fridge on sale in the UK.
- **Haier** showed its Seeker series at AWE in Shanghai: ask "where's the steak?" and the screen and the light in the right compartment show you.

Useful? When it works on its own, yes. The "do we still have milk?" call from the supermarket could disappear. But any system that needs you to enter or correct things by hand only works for people who stick with it.

## 2. You talk to it like a person

- **LG** showed the next LG SIGNATURE fridge at IFA with voice recognition built on a large language model. You can ask it, for example, how best to store a particular ingredient.
- **Samsung** upgraded Bixby on the fridge to follow normal sentences. In the company's example, you say "make the fridge cooler when it gets hot out", it asks at what temperature, and sets up a routine.
- On the [LG STEM](/techsite/en/posts/lg-stem-drawer-freezer-fridge/) launched in Korea, you say you want to store meat and it suggests a spot and a storage mode.

The Israeli catch is obvious: Bixby on the fridge understands a limited list of languages, and Hebrew isn't on it. We found nothing about Hebrew from LG either.

## 3. Cooling before you open the door

One of the quieter, more useful ideas. On the new LG SIGNATURE, AI Fresh learns when you open the fridge and can **pre-cool up to two hours ahead** of an expected opening. The logic: if everyone raids the fridge at six to make dinner, it's better to drop the temperature first than to fight the warm air afterwards. The STEM has a version of the same idea, plus a saving mode that eases off the compressor when nobody's opening the door.

## 4. Flush with the cabinets

Anyone who has redone a kitchen knows the fridge that sticks out 10 cm past the counter. Several brands tackled it this year:

- **LG Fit & Max**, a French-door model with a Zero Clearance design for a flush fit next to cabinets. It won iF and Red Dot design awards this year.
- **Xiaomi** is bringing a [546-litre fridge only 60 cm deep](/techsite/en/posts/xiaomi-mijia-fridge-546l-europe-launch/) to Europe, thanks to venting heat from the bottom. In France it will cost from €1,299.99 (about ₪4,745, about $1,427) at the end of December.
- And in Israel: **Frigidaire** returned in September through Miniline, and one of its four models is a **544-litre** four-door fridge (347 litres fridge, 197 freezer) designed for flush and built-in installation.

## 5. Compartments that switch roles

- **LG Fit & Max** has a drawer that can switch entirely between fridge and freezer, plus two compartments you can set separately by food type.
- On the **LG STEM**, the middle drawer switches between fridge and freezer, with 14 storage modes in the app.
- **Haier Seeker** in China takes it to the extreme: seven doors, nine compartments and 12 zones.

For an Israeli family that shops big for Shabbat or the holidays and suddenly needs more freezer space, a flexible drawer may be the most practical idea on this list.

## 6. Freshness sensors

- **Bosch** showed its VitaFresh Pro series in Shanghai with sensitive odour sensors that monitor the air inside and warn before food goes off.
- **Hisense** showed a fridge there with a vacuum compartment for keeping meat.

We haven't seen these outside China yet, but they're worth watching.

## And during a power cut?

Not a fridge feature as such, but very relevant in Israel: the [Jackery FridgeGuard](/techsite/en/posts/jackery-fridgeguard-fridge-backup-battery/), a slim battery that sits on top of the fridge and keeps it running for up to 15 hours. It launched in Canada and hasn't arrived here.

## What reached Israel

Honestly, not much. Most of this year's ideas launched in the US, Korea, China or Europe, and we found no official word on the new LG, Samsung or Hisense models in Israel. Frigidaire is the clear local example: four models from 499 to 812 litres, starting at **₪4,660 (about $1,401)**. Some of them have a Shabbat mode.

If you're replacing a fridge now, three questions will help more than any camera: the exact depth of your kitchen niche, how much freezer space you really use, and the energy rating. A fridge runs all year, and that's where most of the money goes.

*Conversions at the Bank of Israel rate for Nov 20, 2026: ₪3.326 per dollar and ₪3.650 per euro.*
