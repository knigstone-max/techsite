Not everyone needs ₪1,000-plus headphones. For the train, working from home or a run with a podcast, Sony's CH line has always been the safe pick at a sane price. The new **WH-CH730N** was announced in the US in September, and it's now on sale in Israel too.

## What it costs

- **Israel**: ₪360 to ₪410 (about $108 to $123), depending on the store and the deal. The official importer is Ishpar.
- **US**: a list price of $179.99 (about ₪599), before sales tax.

This is one of those rare cases where the Israeli price is well below the US list price. No need to wait for a friend flying back from New York.

![Sony WH-CH730N by the numbers: battery, connectivity and weight, plus Israeli and US prices in shekels and dollars](img/posts/sony-wh-ch730n-specs-en.webp "The key figures. Graphic: TechSite")

## What's inside

- **Noise cancelling** with two microphones per ear cup, which Sony calls Dual Noise Sensor
- **Up to 50 hours** of battery with noise cancelling on. Three minutes of charging gives an hour of playback
- **Bluetooth 6.0**, two devices at once (multipoint), LE Audio and Fast Pair/Swift Pair for Android and Windows
- **207 g** and a folding design, which Sony says is new on this model
- **Calls**: beamforming microphones with AI noise reduction
- Five colours: black, white, pink, blue and oat

## What you give up

At this price there are trade-offs. According to Maariv's review:

- **No LDAC**, Sony's high-quality audio codec. You get SBC, AAC and LC3. If you listen on Spotify or YouTube, it won't matter
- **No wear sensor** to pause music when you take them off
- **No case** in the box
- Noise cancelling handles low, steady noise like an air conditioner or a bus engine well, but it's nowhere near Sony's expensive models

Controls are physical buttons, including a dedicated one for noise cancelling, not touch panels. Some will see that as a plus: no accidental taps when you adjust them on your head.

## The app

They work with Sony Sound Connect: EQ, firmware updates and a mode that detects whether you're walking, sitting or on public transport and adjusts noise cancelling to match. You can also save places like home and work with different settings.

## Who it's for

Anyone who wants over-ears with real noise cancelling, a battery that lasts a week of commuting and a brand with local service, without paying for premium. If you want the noise cancelling of the expensive models, budget a few hundred shekels more.

Sony also announced the **WH-CH530**, basic on-ears without noise cancelling, with up to 55 hours of battery and a US list price of $69.99 (about ₪233). We haven't found an official Israeli price for them yet.
