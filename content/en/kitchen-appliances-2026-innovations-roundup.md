After [the laundry room](/techsite/en/posts/washers-dryers-2026-innovations-roundup/), [floor cleaning](/techsite/en/posts/floor-cleaning-2026-innovations-roundup/) and [fridges](/techsite/en/posts/fridges-2026-innovations-roundup/), our year-end series reaches the kitchen. Here, more than anywhere else in the home, 2026 was a year of small appliances: coffee machines, air fryers and mixers that moved from "nice to have" to "every home has one". On the built-in side, ovens, hobs and dishwashers tried to take a few decisions off our hands.

Here are the ideas that kept coming back this year, what's already on sale in Israel, and what's still only at trade shows.

![The kitchen in 2026: five ideas that kept coming back, from cold brew and two kinds of beans to faster dishwashers](img/posts/kitchen-2026-trends-en.webp "This year's kitchen in five lines. Graphic: TechSite")

## 1. Coffee: cold brew, two kinds of beans and a narrow body

Bean-to-cup machines were the hottest kitchen category of the year, and three ideas kept showing up.

**Cold brew at the press of a button.** Proper cold brew usually means a jar in the fridge for a whole night. This year it became a button. The [Dreame Ecceluxe Master FCM60](/techsite/en/posts/dreame-ecceluxe-master-fcm60-review-israel/), Dreame's first espresso machine, does it for ₪2,110 (about $634) in Israel, and the [De'Longhi Rivelia Start](/techsite/en/posts/delonghi-rivelia-start-review-israel/) promises cold brew in under five minutes. In an Israeli summer that feels like it never ends, that's not a gimmick.

**Two kinds of beans in one machine.** The Rivelia Start comes with two 250 g hoppers that you swap in a second, one grinder for both. In November Bosch went a step further with the [VeroCafe Series 10](/techsite/en/posts/bosch-verocafe-espresso-lineup-2026/): two hoppers, each with its own grinder, at $2,999 (about ₪9,975) in the US. Regular and decaf without mixing.

**Narrow machines for Israeli kitchens.** Counter space is short in most Israeli apartments. The [Dreame Ecceluxe Slim FCM30](/techsite/en/posts/dreame-ecceluxe-coffee-machines-israel/) is only 18 cm wide, which Dreame says is about 35% narrower than a typical bean-to-cup machine, and it costs ₪1,686 (about $507).

Also worth mentioning: Bosch showed its first portafilter machine at IFA, the HomeCafé Series 4, with 30 grind settings. And at the other end of the scale, the Nespresso CitiZ Platinum with 44 Swarovski crystals arrived in Israel at ₪1,250 (about $376). Same coffee, a lot more sparkle.

## 2. Air fryers: glass instead of coating, two levels instead of one

The air fryer doesn't need an introduction anymore. In many Israeli kitchens it works harder than the oven. This year two problems got answers.

**The coating.** Most air fryers have a non-stick drawer that starts to flake after a year or two. The [Ninja CRISPi PRO XL](/techsite/en/posts/ninja-crispi-pro-xl-israel/) replaces it with glass: a 2,050 W heating head that sits on 5.7- and 2.3-litre glass dishes, which go into the dishwasher, the fridge and the table. Sarig sells it in Israel for ₪1,099 (about $330).

**Capacity.** Bosch showed a whole family of air fryers at IFA: a Series 6 Double Stack with two drawers stacked on top of each other and 12 litres in total, a Series 4 Double Drawer with two 4.5-litre drawers side by side, and a Double Flex with a 10-litre drawer you can split. For a family that makes chicken and chips at the same time, two separate drawers are the difference between one dinner and two rounds.

## 3. Ovens and hobs that do some of the work

**Pasta on the hob.** AEG's 7000 OnePot Pasta hob is the brand's first with a pasta programme. You choose the number of portions, put everything into one pot, close the lid, and after 15 minutes there's pasta al dente with sauce. AEG is sold in Israel, but no local date has been announced.

**An oven with a camera.** Haier's ID Ultimate Series 6 recognises the food and stops cooking when it's done. Haier also put a camera in a washer drum this year, so the idea is spreading.

**Self-cleaning in an hour.** Beko's PyroPro heats the oven to 420°C and burns grease down to fine ash. A full clean takes 59 minutes and uses up to 47% less energy than a standard pyrolytic cycle, according to Beko. In the same group, a Hotpoint oven got a 3-minute pizza function.

## 4. Dishwashers: faster, with fewer menus

- **LG Fit & Max**: washes and dries in a single one-hour cycle, with micro-bubbles and a sensor that measures how dirty the water is and adjusts temperature, time and detergent.
- **Electrolux QuickSelect 2.0**: instead of choosing a programme, you slide to how much time you have, and the screen shows how it affects energy use. On the fully integrated 600 to 900 series dishwashers.
- **Haier I-Pro Shine A-70%**: reads the load, the soil level and the temperature, and picks the cycle itself.

None of the three has an Israeli price yet. For a family of four that runs the dishwasher almost every day, a one-hour wash-and-dry cycle is the feature to wait for.

## 5. Small appliances, lower prices

Not every innovation came from the big shows. Electra sells a countertop water and ice bar that does cold water, hot water and ice for ₪1,290 (about $388). And at the high end of baking, the [Bosch Series 8 MUMS8ZS00](/techsite/en/posts/bosch-series-8-mums8zs00-stand-mixer-review/) brings an 1,800 W motor, a 6-litre bowl and a built-in scale that weighs the flour for you, for about ₪3,550 (about $1,067).

## What's on sale in Israel, and what isn't

**Already here**: the Ninja CRISPi PRO XL, the two Dreame Ecceluxe machines, the De'Longhi Rivelia Start, the Bosch Series 8 mixer, the Nespresso CitiZ Swarovski and Electra's water bar. Prices are in the table above, as listed when we covered each one. Check again before you buy, since prices move.

**Not yet**: the AEG pasta hob, the Haier oven with a camera, the Beko PyroPro oven, the LG and Electrolux dishwashers, Bosch's new air fryers and the VeroCafe range. Most of these brands are sold in Israel, so if you're planning a new kitchen, ask the retailer whether the specific model is coming and when.

## How to read this when you buy

- **Coffee**: if more than one person drinks coffee at home, and each wants different beans, look for two hoppers. If you drink cold coffee all summer, a cold brew button is worth paying for.
- **Air fryer**: glass or ceramic dishes last longer than a coating. Two drawers are worth it for families of four and up.
- **Built-in**: a camera in the oven or a pasta programme is a nice extra. A one-hour dishwasher cycle, or pyrolytic cleaning that uses less power, is something you'll feel every week.
