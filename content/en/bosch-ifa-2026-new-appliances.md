Bosch called its IFA booth the "Bosch Factory of Quality", and it had far less science fiction than its rivals. Ordinary appliances, each with one clear improvement.

## Series 8 heat-pump washer-dryer

This is the product most relevant to Israeli homes. Combo washer-dryers have always been a compromise, drying by condensation with a lot of power and water. Bosch now puts a heat pump in a combo machine. According to Bosch:

- Up to 20 litres of water saved
- 54% less energy than a condenser combo
- Class A for both washing and drying
- Wash and dry up to 6kg in one go, or wash up to 9kg

If you have no room for a separate dryer, and many Israeli flats do not, this is real news.

## OneFlow wet-dry vacuum

It vacuums and mops in one pass, cleans up to 150 m² per charge, and the station washes the roller with 70°C water and dries it with 75°C air. With tiled floors in most Israeli homes, that is relevant.

## Air fryers for every household

- Series 2: four litres, elongated drawer
- Series 6 Double Stack: two stacked drawers, 12 litres
- Series 4 Double Drawer: two 4.5-litre drawers, plus a Double Flex with a divisible 10-litre drawer

## Also

- **HomeCafé Series 4**: Bosch's first portafilter espresso machine, 30 grind settings and five dose presets
- **Flex-Door freezer**: door shelves move so large trays fit
- **Green Collection ovens**: materials with a 20% lower carbon footprint, TÜV certified
