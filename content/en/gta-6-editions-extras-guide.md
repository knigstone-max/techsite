The first thing everyone asks: is Ultimate worth the extra ₪80? Short answer: Standard is the full game. No missing story chapters, no locked missions. Ultimate adds cosmetics, vehicles and weapons woven into the story, plus five shops and garages that only open for Ultimate owners. If you're the type to lose hours in the garage and the clothing stores, it's probably worth it. If you're here for the story and the heists, Standard is plenty.

One more thing before we start: GTA VI is a single-player game. That's what the official PlayStation page says. There's no new GTA Online at launch, and Rockstar hasn't given a date for an online mode.

## What it costs

| Edition | Israel, digital | US, before tax |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | ₪319 (about $98) | $79.99 (about ₪261) |
| Ultimate | ₪399 (about $122) | $99.99 (about ₪326) |

In Israel the gap is ₪80, roughly $25. The local price is close to the US price plus VAT, so there's no reason to hunt for a copy abroad.

### Weapons

A pair of Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers, one for Jason and one for Lucia, in classic Vice City style: palm-tree-etched grips, engraving and a scope. You also get engraved versions of Jason's Girardi ES9 and Lucia's Klose K17. It all waits in the weapon locker at any Ammu-Nation. Full details in the [weapons catalog](/techsite/en/posts/gta-6-weapons-catalog/).

## Vintage Vice City Pack: the pre-order bonus

Anyone who bought the game, Standard or Ultimate, by November 19 got this pack. It's a throwback to the '80s of GTA: Vice City:

- **'55 Vapid Stanier:** a yellow and white classic sedan, a nod to the original game's Oceanic.
- **Shore Court garage:** on Shore Drive near Ocean Beach, room for one car, with a weapon locker and a drop for stolen goods.
- **Outfits:** a vintage pastel suit for Jason, and a red sequin mini dress with curled hair for Lucia.
- **Weapon pattern:** palm trees inspired by Tommy Vercetti's shirt. It unlocks in Chapter 3.

![Lucia in a red sequin dress and Jason in a light suit in front of the Ocean View Hotel's neon sign](img/posts/gta6-extras-vintage-pack.webp "Vintage Vice City Pack. Screenshot: Rockstar Games")

![Jason and Lucia dancing in a club under purple light, her in a red sequin dress](img/posts/gta6-extras-vintage-looks.webp "The pack's looks. Screenshot: Rockstar Games")

None of it is there from minute one. It unlocks gradually through the story.

### What about GTA+?

Digital buyers who bought by November 19 can claim a month of GTA+ at no cost. The catch: it's a subscription that renews at a monthly price after the first month unless you cancel it in your subscription settings. You can claim it until March 31, 2027. If you don't want to pay, cancel right after you activate it. The month still runs to the end.

## What to do in Leonida besides the story

The 100% checklist only opens once you finish the story, but everything you did before already counts. PowerPyx says it's fairly lenient, closer to 60% of everything in the game. Here's how it breaks down:

**Crime**

- 39 gang operations, 22 bank jobs, 8 vaults, 7 payday loan jobs, 35 smash 'n' grabs and 91 stick-ups (50 needed).
- Fencing: $100,000 in narcotics, $100,000 in jewelry, $250,000 in marked bills and $200,000 in vehicles.

**Exploration and collectibles**

- 33 underwater discoveries (all needed).
- 125 cocaine flamingos to blow up (100 needed).
- 18 collectible card sets (15 needed), and 34 magazines, books and comics (30 needed).
- 18 meditation spots (15 needed). Each one counts twice if you do it with both Jason and Lucia.
- 100 WAiNK locations to scan, 200 vehicle models, and 100 of 255 animals. Looking at them through binoculars is enough.
- Bret's 15 hunting quests.

**Activities**

- 42 shooting range gold medals and 42 drone time trials.
- 40 races (30 needed) and 13 race challenges (10 golds needed).
- 100 basketball hoops (90 needed), mini golf at 2 of 3 courses, and 5 of 7 pool opponents.
- 4 in-game video games, 2 unique playlists, and 70 of 79 clothing styles.

## Cheats: yes, but there's a price

The classic cheats are back, but only after the story, and most of them unlock as rewards for challenges. Five exploration challenges unlock a Sea Sparrow helicopter cheat, for example, and blowing up 50 flamingos unlocks the inflatable flamingo. While a cheat is active you can't save and trophies are switched off.

## So which edition should you buy?

- **Standard (₪319):** if you're here for the story, the heists and the world. You won't miss any of the plot.
- **Ultimate (₪399):** if you love customizing cars and characters, you want the Cheetah and the Morgans, or you know you'll be playing for months.
