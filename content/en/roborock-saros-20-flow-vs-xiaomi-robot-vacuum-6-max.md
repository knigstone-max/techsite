This autumn brought a wave of mopping robots to Israel that use a roller instead of two spinning pads. We reviewed several, and two stood out: the [Roborock Saros 20 Flow Complete](/techsite/en/posts/roborock-saros-20-flow-complete-review-israel/) and the [Xiaomi Robot Vacuum 6 Max](/techsite/en/posts/xiaomi-robot-vacuum-6-max-review-israel/). Suction is similar, both docks wash the roller in hot water and dry it with hot air, and they're chasing the same money.

They're still different robots. Each solves a different problem in a typical Israeli apartment, and Black Friday has flipped the price order. Here they are head to head.

![Comparison table: Roborock Saros 20 Flow Complete vs Xiaomi Robot Vacuum 6 Max on price, suction, height, obstacle climbing, corners, dock washing, camera and warranty](img/posts/saros-20-vs-xiaomi-6-max-table-en.webp "The two robots side by side. Graphic: TechSite")

## Price: the order has flipped

When we reviewed them, the Xiaomi was the cheaper of the two: ₪4,690 against ₪4,990 for the Roborock. Today it's the other way round.

- **Roborock Saros 20 Flow Complete:** Zap lists 24 sellers today, from ₪3,789 (about $1,136) up to ₪4,990. The cheapest offers list a one-year warranty or don't say. On Modan's official import with a **three-year warranty**, prices start at **₪4,090 (about $1,227)**. Bug sells it for ₪4,390 (about $1,317), according to Walla on November 25.
- **Xiaomi Robot Vacuum 6 Max:** **₪4,690 (about $1,407)** on Hamilton's Xiaomi Israel site, with a **two-year warranty**. Buy before December 31 and you also get a year of home pickup and return for repairs. The Eilat price is ₪3,974 (about $1,192).

So right now the official-import Roborock is about ₪600 cheaper and comes with an extra year of warranty. That's a real shift from how this looked two weeks ago.

## Thresholds and steps: the Roborock climbs higher

Lots of Israeli apartments have a sill between the living room and the balcony, a small step into the kitchen or a lip at the bathroom door. Ordinary robots get stuck there.

Both solve it mechanically. On the Xiaomi, two **legs** push out from underneath and lift it over a step of **up to 6 cm**. On the Roborock, a climbing arm handles two-step sills of **up to 8.8 cm**. If your balcony step is 7 cm, only the Roborock gets over it. If every sill in your home is under 6 cm, there's no real difference.

## Under the sofa

The Roborock is **8.98 cm** tall, with a lidar that drops down when it goes under furniture. The Xiaomi is 9 cm with its lidar lowered and gets into gaps from **9.3 cm**. In practice that's three millimetres. Measure your lowest sofa before letting this decide for you.

## Corners and chair legs: the Xiaomi reaches further

This is where the Xiaomi wins. It has **three arms**: a side brush that extends into corners, a roller that shifts toward the wall, and a small round pad that swings out when the robot meets a corner or a chair leg. In a home with a dining table and six chairs, that means fewer crumbs left at the edges.

The Roborock has two side brushes and a dual main brush designed to stop hair tangling. It cleans well along walls, but it has nothing like the pad that works its way around chair legs.

## The dock: heat, smell and space

Both docks wash the roller in hot water and dry it, which is what stops the wet-rag smell.

- **Roborock:** washes at **100°C** and dries with 63°C air, holds 4 litres of clean water and 3 of dirty, and doses detergent automatically. It measures 38.1×45.4×45.1 cm and weighs 10.5 kg.
- **Xiaomi:** washes with water **up to 85°C** and dries at 50°C, with four wash temperatures to choose from. Dust goes into a bag that Xiaomi says lasts up to 75 days. The dock is 58 cm tall and pulls up to 1,200 watts while heating water.

An Israeli tip for both: if you've moved to a time-of-use electricity plan, schedule cleaning for the morning or midday, not between 17:00 and 22:00. [We covered that here](/techsite/en/posts/winter-tou-electricity-peak-hours-appliances/).

## Cameras and privacy

Both can see your home. The Roborock has a camera that recognises more than 300 obstacle types and stains too, and boosts suction on coffee or cat litter. The Xiaomi has three cameras that, according to Xiaomi, recognise more than 320 kinds of objects, furniture and stains, down to a 3 mm earphone cable.

Both let you watch your home live through the robot. On the Xiaomi, object recognition and the camera are off by default and you switch them on yourself. On the Roborock, go through the privacy settings in the app and turn off what you don't need.

## So which one?

**Get the Roborock if:** you have a sill higher than 6 cm, low sofas and beds, and you want three years of warranty. At today's official-import price it's also the cheaper of the two, which makes it the safer pick for most homes.

**Get the Xiaomi if:** your home is full of chair and table legs, corners matter to you, and your sills are low. If its price drops toward ₪4,000 over Black Friday, it's very competitive again.

**Watch the warranty:** with both robots, the cheapest Zap listings aren't always official imports. On a robot with a dock, a pump and several motors, a three-year warranty is worth a few hundred shekels.
