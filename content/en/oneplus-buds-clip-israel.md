The OnePlus Buds Clip, the company's first clip-style earbuds, are now on sale in Israel. They don't go into the ear canal. Instead they hook onto the ear with a flexible arc, so you can listen to music and still hear traffic. Official importer Cell Now is selling them for ₪499, roughly $160 at an approximate exchange rate.

## Design and fit

The arc is made of a nickel-titanium alloy that bends to the shape of your ear and springs back. OnePlus says it survived more than 30,000 bend cycles in lab testing. Each bud weighs about 5.2 g and the case about 35.9 g. There's no fixed left or right bud: put either one in either ear and the buds detect where they are and swap the stereo channels. They're rated IP55 for dust and water.

## Sound

Each bud has two drivers, an 11 mm and a 9 mm, each with its own DAC. Danish speaker maker Dynaudio did the tuning. There are six microphones for calls. Codec support covers LHDC 5.0, AAC and SBC over Bluetooth 6.1. LHDC doesn't work on iPhone, so iPhone owners get AAC.

## Battery

OnePlus quotes up to 9.5 hours per charge and 40 hours with the case, using AAC at 50% volume. With LHDC that drops to 8 and 36 hours. Charging is over USB-C.

## Price and availability in Israel

₪499 on the OnePlus Israel site and at authorized resellers, with a 12-month Cell Now warranty. Colors are Pearl White, Pebble Gray and Twilight Rose. OnePlus's global site doesn't list a dollar price yet.
