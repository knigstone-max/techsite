This year almost every wet-dry vacuum maker added steam to its flagship, and each one is trying to win with a different number. Dreame's number is 200°C. The **H16 Pro Steam** is the best-equipped cordless wet-dry vacuum Dreame sells in Israel right now, and the official importer lists it as new.

I went through the full spec sheet, what the dock does, and how it stacks up against its two direct rivals here, the Xiaomi Wet Dry Vacuum 5 Pro and the Roborock F25 Ultra Steam. Here's how it comes out.

![The Dreame H16 Pro Steam cleaning head with callouts: blue light, TangleCut roller, suction, runtime and weight](img/posts/dreame-h16-pro-steam-annotated-en.webp "What's in the cleaning head. Graphic: TechSite")

## What it costs in Israel

On the day of this review:

- **Dreame Israel** (the official importer): ₪2,533 (about $772) on sale, down from ₪2,990 (about $911)
- **Zap**: offers from ₪2,066 (about $629) up to ₪2,990, across 53 stores

The importer gives a **three-year warranty**, and that's one of the best things about this deal. A wet-dry vacuum has a battery, a pump, a steam boiler and a motorised roller, so plenty can go wrong. Before you order from the cheapest store on Zap, check that it's official stock and that the warranty really is three years, not one or two.

## The steam: 200°C, but not on your floor

This is where the small print matters. The importer's product page says the internal heater reaches **200°C**, and the steam leaves through **6 outlets** at a steady **99°C**. On top of that, it washes the floor with hot water at up to 90°C.

That isn't a flaw. Steam at around 100°C at floor level is what you'd expect from any machine like this, and it's what softens dried grease and sticky spots in the kitchen. But when you compare ads, Dreame's 200°C, Roborock's 180°C and Xiaomi's 160°C don't necessarily measure the same thing.

Steam runs for **up to 20 minutes straight**. That's enough for the kitchen, the dining area and a hallway, not the whole home. That's also how you'd use it anyway: steam where it's dirty, a normal wash everywhere else.

## Two arms: one for walls, one for stains

What sets the H16 Pro Steam apart from most rivals is a pair of mechanical arms:

- **A front arm with AI GapFree 2.0** that reaches forward to clean right up to baseboards and into corners. Per the spec sheet, edge cleaning comes from both sides of the brush plus this front arm, so three directions in all.
- **A rigid scrubbing arm** for stubborn stains. Instead of going over a sauce spot three times, it adds mechanical friction.

In an Israeli home, with tiles and baseboards in every room, cleaning tight to the wall isn't a gimmick. It's exactly where cheaper machines leave a grey line.

## Suction, roller and hair

Suction is rated at **28,000Pa**, the highest of the three models I compared. Dreame mentions a 12 mm intake, meant to swallow bigger crumbs and food scraps without clogging.

The roller is a **TangleCut** design that cuts hair as it wraps. If you live with a dog or someone with long hair, you know why that matters: without it, you're at the roller with scissors within a week.

The head has a **blue light** that makes dust and hair stand out on the floor, and a **dirt sensor** that adjusts suction and water flow. There's an LED display, the Dreame Home app, and **voice prompts in Hebrew**, a small touch that helps when it's telling you the tank is full.

## Drive and weight

It weighs **6.8 kg**. That's a lot. It's heavier than the Xiaomi Wet Dry Vacuum 5 Pro, which I already called heavy at 6.1 kg. If you live with stairs, you'll feel it every time you carry it up.

Dreame tries to make up for it with **GlideWheel**, a drive system that senses which way you're moving and helps push and pull, and **GravityEase**, which absorbs bumps. It also lies flat to **180°**, so the head fits under sofas and beds. On the floor, with the drive helping, you notice the weight less. In your hand, you notice it.

## Battery and tanks

- **Up to 72 minutes** of runtime, and up to 20 minutes with steam
- A six-cell 5,000mAh battery, with a **4-hour** charge
- An **800 ml** clean-water tank and a **600 ml** dirty-water tank
- A separate detergent compartment

72 minutes is far more than Xiaomi's 40. An 800 ml tank, though, isn't big, and in a five-room apartment you'll probably refill it once along the way.

## The dock

After cleaning, the vacuum goes back to its dock, which rinses the brush with **100°C water** and steam, then dries it with **90°C hot air**. In quick mode, drying takes **about 5 minutes**. That's what stops the wet-rag smell anyone who's owned a wet-dry vac without drying knows well, and at this price it should be there.

## Against the rivals

The **Xiaomi Wet Dry Vacuum 5 Pro** costs ₪2,490 (about $759), nearly the same. It's lighter, but only runs up to 40 minutes, and its importer warranty is less clear-cut. The H16 Pro Steam wins on runtime, suction, the arms and the warranty.

The **Roborock F25 Ultra Steam** costs ₪3,290 (about $1,002). It claims steam up to 180°C and runs up to 30 minutes with steam and up to 100 minutes in economy mode. If you need lots of steam in a big home, it's the better pick. If you don't, the H16 Pro Steam saves you about ₪750.

If you're not sure you need steam at all, our [wet-dry vacuum buying guide](/techsite/en/posts/wet-dry-vacuum-buying-guide-israel/) covers what actually matters day to day, and there are good models at half the price.

## Who it's not for

- **Real-wood parquet**: the importer says plainly it isn't suitable for parquet with a real wood finish.
- **Anyone carrying it between floors**: 6.8 kg is a lot.
- **Detergent**: per the importer, you should only use the original cleaning fluid. Factor in the running cost.

## The bottom line

8/10

The Dreame H16 Pro Steam is a very well-equipped wet-dry vacuum: strong suction, steam for the kitchen, two arms that handle walls and stains, a dock that dries with hot air and a three-year warranty. At around ₪2,500 it offers more than its direct Xiaomi rival. The weight, the small water tank and the parquet restriction hold it back a little, and the 200°C in the ads is the heater, not the steam.
:::

- Two arms: tight edge cleaning and stain scrubbing
- 28,000Pa suction and a roller that cuts hair
- Up to 72 minutes of runtime
